#     and Tables 2 to 4. Consensus rates under 80% are given on a ・ line

band: Diagnosis and molecular testing
  ① Management by a multidisciplinary team in a reference centre for sarcoma and GIST 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
  ② Diagnosis by morphology with immunohistochemistry for CD117 (KIT) and DOG1, and for SDHB when no KIT or PDGFRA mutation is found 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・About 5% of GIST are CD117 negative 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO〕
  ③ Mutational analysis of KIT and PDGFRA, including NGS, for advanced GIST and before neoadjuvant or adjuvant treatment 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・Mutational analysis is standard for all GIST, with the possible exception of non-rectal GIST under 2 cm 〔ESMO II, A〕
    ・GIST without a KIT or PDGFRA mutation are tested by NGS for SDH deficiency and other drivers, including BRAF, NF1 and NTRK or FGFR fusions 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
  ④ KIT mutations are found in 80% to 85% of GIST, exon 11 in 65% to 70% and exon 9 in about 10%, mostly outside the stomach, and PDGFRA mutations in 5% to 10%, mostly gastric 〔Asian〕
    ・KIT and PDGFRA wild-type GIST make up 10% to 15%, most of them SDH-deficient 〔Asian〕 〔NCCN〕
  ⑤ Biopsy to confirm the diagnosis before preoperative or systemic treatment, by EUS-guided fine-needle aspiration or biopsy 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・Percutaneous image-guided biopsy is not recommended for a localised primary tumour and is acceptable for metastatic or recurrent disease 〔Asian〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・Multiple core biopsies, EUS-guided or percutaneous, are standard for a large mass that would need multivisceral resection 〔ESMO〕
  ⑥ Genetic counselling for SDH-deficient GIST, and germline KIT and PDGFRA testing for multifocal GIST, a family history of GIST, or NF1 or SDH-deficient GIST 〔NCCN〕
    ・Syndromes: Carney triad (SDHC epimutation), Carney-Stratakis syndrome (germline SDH subunit mutation), NF1, and familial KIT or PDGFRA mutations 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO〕

band: Risk of recurrence after resection
  ① Risk is set by mitotic count per 5 mm² (50 high-power fields), tumour size, site and rupture, before any TKI 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
  ② Modified NIH criteria, high risk: size over 10 cm, more than 10 mitoses per 5 mm², rupture, or size over 5 cm with more than 5 mitoses per 5 mm² 〔Asian〕
    ・Non-gastric GIST with size over 5 cm or more than 5 mitoses per 5 mm² is also high risk 〔Asian〕
    ・The modified NIH criteria select patients for adjuvant imatinib 〔Asian〕
  ③ AFIP criteria (Miettinen), by size, mitotic count and gastric or non-gastric site 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・Prognostic contour maps use size and mitotic count as continuous variables and include rupture 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
  ④ Rupture: tumour spillage or fracture, piecemeal resection, incisional biopsy, perforation into the abdomen, blood-stained ascites, or microscopic transperitoneal infiltration 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・Core needle biopsy, superficial capsule laceration and R1 margins are not rupture 〔ESMO〕
  ⑤ Risk cannot be classified after neoadjuvant imatinib 〔Asian〕 〔NCCN〕

band: Response assessment on TKI
  ① Contrast CT or MRI every 8 to 12 weeks 〔NCCN〕
  ② Response is judged by tumour size and density, and an increase in size with a fall in density is a response 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・FDG-PET shows response within 2 to 4 weeks when an early answer is needed 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・A dense nodule within a responding mass marks progression 〔ESMO〕
  ③ Adherence and drug interactions are checked before progression is called 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・Plasma imatinib levels are measured in selected situations 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO〕

band: Regimens
  ① Imatinib 400 mg once daily, or 400 mg twice daily
  ② Sunitinib 50 mg once daily for 4 weeks of every 6 (A6181004), or 37.5 mg once daily continuously 〔ESMO III, C〕
    ~ George 2009 {pmid 19282169}
  ③ Regorafenib 160 mg once daily for 3 weeks of every 4 (GRID)
  ④ Ripretinib 150 mg once daily (INVICTUS), and 150 mg twice daily after progression on 150 mg once daily 〔NCCN〕
  ⑤ Avapritinib 300 mg once daily (NAVIGATOR)
  ⑥ Imatinib is stopped shortly before surgery and restarted when oral intake allows 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・Sunitinib, regorafenib, ripretinib and avapritinib are stopped at least 1 week before surgery 〔NCCN〕

course: GIST | parallel
  Localised GIST: surgery or preoperative imatinib
    branch: LOC
    ① Resectable GIST without expected major morbidity: complete resection with negative macroscopic margins and the pseudocapsule intact 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Wedge or segmental resection is usually sufficient, without routine lymphadenectomy 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Enlarged nodes are removed in SDH-deficient GIST 〔Asian〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Laparoscopic resection is recommended for smaller GIST in favourable sites 〔Asian〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Laparoscopic resection follows the principles of oncological surgery 〔ESMO III, A〕 and is discouraged for large tumours 〔ESMO〕
      ・Re-resection is not routine after R1 resection 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ② Gastric or duodenal GIST under 2 cm without high-risk features: active surveillance or resection 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO IV, C〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・High-risk EUS features: irregular border, cystic spaces, ulceration, echogenic foci and heterogeneity 〔NCCN〕
      ・When biopsy confirms GIST, resection is performed unless major morbidity is expected 〔ESMO IV, C〕
      ・Rectal nodules are biopsied or excised whatever their size 〔ESMO〕
    ③ R0 resection not feasible, or feasible only with major morbidity such as multivisceral resection, total gastrectomy or abdominoperineal resection: preoperative imatinib for an imatinib-sensitive mutation 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Size over 10 cm is an indication 〔Asian〕
      ・Surgery follows at maximal response, usually after 6 to 12 months 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Mutational analysis precedes treatment 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕, and imatinib may start before the result 〔NCCN〕
      ・KIT exon 9: imatinib 800 mg is an option 〔ESMO〕
      ~ RTOG 0132 {pmid 18942073}
    next: After resection: adjuvant imatinib
  After resection: adjuvant imatinib
    branch: LOC pale
    ① High risk of recurrence with an imatinib-sensitive mutation: imatinib 400 mg daily for 3 years 〔Asian strong〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕
      ・Intermediate risk is an indication 〔NCCN 1〕
      ・At intermediate risk, treatment is a shared decision 〔ESMO〕
      ・Benefit is greatest with KIT exon 11 deletions 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・A duration of at least 6 years, based on disease-free survival, is an option 〔Asian〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Consensus on 6 years was 66.7% 〔Asian〕
      + SSG XVIII {pmid 22453568}
      ~ ACOSOG Z9001 {pmid 19303137}
      ~ EORTC 62024 {pmid 26573069}
      ~ IMADGIST {pmid 39241959}
    ② KIT exon 9: imatinib 800 mg daily for 3 years may be considered 〔ESMO II, B〕
    ③ After preoperative imatinib: imatinib continued to a total of 3 years 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ④ Ruptured GIST: adjuvant imatinib 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO IV, A〕
      ・Treatment is extended or lifelong 〔Asian〕
      ・The optimal duration is not defined 〔ESMO〕
      ・Ruptured GIST is managed as metastatic disease 〔NCCN〕
    ⑤ Low risk, PDGFRA D842V, SDH-deficient, NF1-related, BRAF-mutated or NTRK-rearranged GIST: no adjuvant treatment 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO IV, D〕 〔NCCN〕
    ⑥ Follow-up: CT or MRI at intervals set by risk of recurrence and time since surgery 〔Asian weak〕 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
  Advanced or metastatic GIST: first line
    branch: ADV
    ① Imatinib-sensitive KIT or PDGFRA mutation: imatinib 400 mg daily 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕
      ~ B2222 {pmid 12181401}
      ~ S0033 {pmid 18235122}
    ② KIT exon 9: imatinib 400 mg with escalation to 800 mg on progression 〔Asian〕 〔NCCN〕, or 800 mg from the start 〔ESMO III, B〕
      ・Dose escalation is applied with caution in Asian patients 〔Asian〕
      ~ EORTC 62005 {pmid 15451219}
      ~ MetaGIST {pmid 20124181}
    ③ Continuation of imatinib for life while benefit lasts, also after complete resection of metastases 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Short interruptions of 1 to 2 weeks are acceptable 〔NCCN〕
      ~ BFR14 {pmid 20864406}
    ④ Responding metastatic disease: resection, individualised by the multidisciplinary team 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO III, C〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Surgery is not routine for disease responding to imatinib 〔Asian〕
      ・Resection is considered when complete resection is possible 〔NCCN〕
      ・Upfront surgery is not recommended for metastatic or recurrent GIST 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO〕
    ※ A randomised trial of resection of residual disease during imatinib closed early with 41 of 210 planned patients
      ~ Du 2014 {pmid 24768330}
    next: Progression on imatinib and later lines | Progression
  Progression on imatinib and later lines
    branch: ADV pale
    ① Limited progression: imatinib continued with resection or ablation of the progressing lesions 〔ESMO IV, C〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Embolisation, chemoembolisation and palliative radiotherapy are options 〔NCCN 2B〕
    ② Progression on 400 mg: imatinib 800 mg daily 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Switching to sunitinib may be preferable to dose escalation 〔Asian〕
      ~ S0033 {pmid 18235122}
      ~ EORTC 62005 {pmid 15451219}
    ③ Second line: sunitinib 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕
      ・Ripretinib is an option after intolerance of sunitinib 〔NCCN〕
      ~ A6181004 {pmid 17046465}
      - INTRIGUE {pmid 35947817}
    ④ Third line: regorafenib 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕
      ~ GRID {pmid 23177515}
    ⑤ Fourth line: ripretinib 150 mg daily 〔Asian strong〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕
      ・Escalation to 150 mg twice daily is an option after progression 〔NCCN〕
      ~ INVICTUS {pmid 32511981}
    ⑥ After fourth line: rechallenge with an earlier effective TKI, or continuation beyond progression 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Avapritinib, cabozantinib, everolimus with a TKI, nilotinib, pazopanib, ponatinib and sorafenib are options 〔NCCN〕
      ~ RIGHT {pmid 24140183}
      ~ PAZOGIST {pmid 27068858}
    ※ Pimitespib after imatinib, sunitinib and regorafenib is recommended 〔Asian〕
      ・Consensus on pimitespib was 75.0% 〔Asian〕
      ・Pimitespib is licensed in Japan only
      ~ CHAPTER-GIST-301 {pmid 35688358}
  Imatinib-insensitive or wild-type GIST: treatment by genotype
    branch: GEN
    ① PDGFRA exon 18 mutation including D842V: avapritinib 300 mg daily in advanced disease 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Preoperative avapritinib may be considered 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Dasatinib is an option in second line 〔NCCN〕
      ~ NAVIGATOR {pmid 32615108}
    ※ Avapritinib did not improve progression-free survival over regorafenib in third or fourth line GIST unselected by genotype
      - VOYAGER {pmid 34343033}
    ② NTRK fusion: larotrectinib or entrectinib 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Repotrectinib is an option 〔NCCN 2B〕
      ~ Larotrectinib pooled analysis {pmid 29466156}
      ~ ALKA/STARTRK {pmid 31838007}
      ~ TRIDENT-1 (NTRK) {pmid 41639379}
    ③ BRAF V600E: dabrafenib with trametinib 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO V, B〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Dabrafenib with trametinib holds an FDA licence for BRAF V600E solid tumours
      ~ ROAR {pmid 37059834}
      ~ NCI-MATCH subprotocol H {pmid 41533998}
    ④ SDH-deficient GIST: sunitinib or regorafenib 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Pazopanib 〔NCCN〕 and binimetinib with imatinib 〔NCCN 2B〕 are options
      ・Indolent disease may be observed 〔Asian〕 〔NCCN〕
    ⑤ NF1-associated GIST: observation for indolent disease 〔NCCN〕
    ° Toxicity of note: cognitive effects and intracranial bleeding with avapritinib 〔Asian〕 〔ESMO〕
