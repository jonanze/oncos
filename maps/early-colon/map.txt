# oncOS board map: Early CRC (localised colon cancer; rectal cancer is its own sheet)
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
#   branch: Key | legend   one colour for a branch of the map; "branch: Key" under a step colours it.
#                          Here pMMR or MSS and dMMR or MSI-H; steps for both stay plain (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Trial lines give the trial name only; results are on the trial's card (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Trial {pmid N}  a trial with no Evidence row yet: PubMed link, dashed cell, listed for intake
#
# Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#   〔JSCCR evidence, strength〕 JSCCR guidelines 2024 for colorectal cancer, English edition
#     (Int J Clin Oncol 2025;30:2410-63, PMID 41186794), CQ boxes read from the PDF; East Asian guideline first
#   〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Colon Cancer 3.2026 (6 November 2026); category 2A unless stated
#   〔ESMO evidence, grade〕 ESMO localised colon cancer 2020 (Ann Oncol 2020;31:1291-305, PMID 32702383)
#     with the 2026 Express Update grades where they replace it
#   Japan-only options (S-1, UFT with leucovorin) sit on a ※ line (Jonan 2026-11-11)

map: Early CRC

branch: pMMR | pMMR or MSS

band: Testing before treatment
  ① MMR or MSI testing in every tumour 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔JSCCR A, 1〕
  ② PI3K pathway testing in stage II–III 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ③ DPYD testing before a fluoropyrimidine
    ・NCCN names no specific test

course: Localised colon cancer
  Clinical stage I to III
    MMR status on the biopsy sets the treatment before and after resection
    next: Colectomy | Resectable
    next: Obstructing cancer
    next: cT4b or bulky nodes, pMMR or MSS
    next: T4 or bulky, dMMR or MSI-H
  Obstructing cancer
    ① One-stage colectomy, resection with diversion, or diversion alone 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ② A stent as a bridge to colectomy, in selected patients and expert centres 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔JSCCR B, 2〕 〔ESMO II〕
      ・JSCCR advises against a stent when systemic therapy is planned 〔JSCCR B, 2〕
      ~ ESCO {pmid 27924392}
    next: Colectomy
  cT4b or bulky nodes, pMMR or MSS
    branch: pMMR
    ① FOLFOX or CAPOX, then colectomy 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Chemotherapy before and after resection totals up to 6 months
      ・ESMO and JSCCR make no statement
      ~ FOxTROT
      - NeoCol
    ※ Locally unresectable: systemic therapy with or without radiotherapy, then reassessment 〔NCCN 2A〕
    next: Colectomy
  T4 or bulky, dMMR or MSI-H
    branch: dMMR
    ① A checkpoint inhibitor, then colectomy 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・cT4b or bulky nodes: a checkpoint inhibitor is preferred to FOLFOX or CAPOX 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
      ・Any PD-1 or PD-L1 antibody, or nivolumab with ipilimumab
      ・POLE or POLD1 mutated tumours are treated the same way
      ・The role of immunotherapy after resection is not defined
      ・ESMO and JSCCR make no statement
      ~ NICHE-2
      ※ dMMR tumours regressed less than pMMR tumours with chemotherapy before resection
        ~ FOxTROT
    next: Colectomy
  Colectomy
    ① Colectomy with en bloc removal of the regional nodes 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
      ・At least 12 nodes are examined 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO IV, B〕
      ・JSCCR sets the dissection by stage: D2 for cT1, D2 or D3 for cT2, and D3 for cT3, cT4a or node-positive disease
      ・Adjacent organs are resected en bloc for T4b 〔ESMO I, B〕
    ② Laparoscopic or robotic surgery by an experienced surgeon 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, C〕
      ・Robotic colectomy is a treatment option 〔JSCCR C, 2〕
      ・Not for locally advanced, obstructed or perforated cancer 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ~ JCOG0404 {pmid 28404155}
      ~ COLOR {pmid 19071061}
      ~ CLASICC {pmid 17634484}
      ~ COST {pmid 15141043}
    ※ JSCCR treats Tis or slightly invasive cT1 by endoscopic resection when en bloc resection is possible
      ・Colectomy with node dissection follows for a positive vertical margin 〔JSCCR C, 1〕
      ・Colectomy is also advised for T1b, lymphovascular invasion, poor differentiation or budding BD2–3 〔JSCCR B, 2〕
    next: Stage I and II, pMMR or MSS
    next: Stage III, pMMR or MSS
    next: Stage 0 to II, dMMR or MSI-H
    next: Stage III, dMMR or MSI-H
  Stage I and II, pMMR or MSS
    branch: pMMR
    ① Stage I: observation 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ② Low-risk stage II: observation 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔JSCCR A, 2〕
      ・Capecitabine or 5-FU/LV for 6 months can be given 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・MSS with one minor risk factor: a fluoropyrimidine for 6 months 〔ESMO I, B〕
      + QUASAR
    ③ High-risk stage II: capecitabine, 5-FU/LV, FOLFOX, CAPOX or observation 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔JSCCR B, 2〕
      ・No option is preferred
      ・Oxaliplatin can be considered for pT4, fewer than 12 nodes or several minor factors 〔ESMO I, C〕
      ※ Oxaliplatin did not improve OS in stage II
        - MOSAIC
      ※ High-risk features: T4, poor differentiation, lymphovascular or perineural invasion, obstruction, perforation, fewer than 12 nodes, close or positive margins, high-tier budding 〔NCCN 2A〕
    next: Exercise and aspirin after resection
  Stage III, pMMR or MSS
    branch: pMMR
    Treatment starts within 8 weeks of resection 〔ESMO I, A〕
    ① CAPOX or FOLFOX 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔JSCCR A, 1〕
      + MOSAIC
      + NO16968
      ※ FLOX is not recommended 〔NCCN 2A〕
        ~ NSABP C-07
      ※ Age 70 or over: no benefit from added oxaliplatin was shown
        - ACCENT 2013 [t-accent-pooled-analysis--early-crc]
      ※ Age 80 or over: oxaliplatin is not added 〔JSCCR C, 2〕
    ② Oxaliplatin unsuitable: capecitabine or 5-FU/LV for 6 months 〔NCCN 2A, other recommended〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔JSCCR A, 2〕
      ~ X-ACT
      + IMPACT
      + ACCENT 2001 [t-accent-pooled-analysis]
    ※ Japan only: S-1 or UFT with leucovorin for 6 months 〔JSCCR A, 2〕
      ・Not listed by NCCN or ESMO
      ・S-1 did not show non-inferiority to capecitabine
      ~ ACTS-CC {pmid 24942277}
      - JCOG0910 {pmid 29079411}
    next: Exercise and aspirin after resection
  Stage 0 to II, dMMR or MSI-H
    branch: dMMR
    ① Stage 0–IIB: observation 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ※ A fluoropyrimidine alone is not effective in stage II dMMR 〔NCCN 2A〕
        - Sargent 2010 {pmid 20498393}
    ② Stage IIC: observation, or treatment as for low-risk stage III 〔NCCN 2A〕
    next: Exercise and aspirin after resection
  Stage III, dMMR or MSI-H
    branch: dMMR
    ① FOLFOX or CAPOX with atezolizumab, then atezolizumab to 12 months 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
      ・ATOMIC gave mFOLFOX6, and CAPOX with atezolizumab is extrapolated
      ・Not in the ESMO 2026 update or JSCCR
      ~ ATOMIC
    ② CAPOX or FOLFOX, with duration by risk group 〔NCCN 2A〕
    next: Exercise and aspirin after resection
  Exercise and aspirin after resection
    ① A structured exercise programme after adjuvant chemotherapy 〔ESMO II, B〕
      ・At least 150 minutes of activity a week 〔NCCN 2A〕
      + CHALLENGE
    ② PI3K pathway alteration, stage II–III: aspirin 100–162 mg daily for 3 years 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Any MMR status
      ・ESMO gives no grade and JSCCR does not mention aspirin
      ~ ALASCCA

panel: Duration of CAPOX or FOLFOX > Stage III, pMMR or MSS
  Duration by risk group 〔NCCN 2A〕
  | Risk group | CAPOX | FOLFOX |
  | High-risk stage II | 3 months | 6 months |
  | Low-risk stage III, T1–3 N1 | 3 months | 3 to 6 months |
  | High-risk stage III, T4 or N2 | 3 to 6 months | 6 months |
  ・Low-risk stage III: CAPOX for 3 months or FOLFOX for 6 months 〔ESMO V, B〕
  ・High-risk stage III: CAPOX or FOLFOX for 6 months 〔ESMO V, B〕
  ・High-risk stage II: CAPOX for 3 or 6 months, or FOLFOX for 6 months 〔ESMO II, B〕
