# oncOS board map: Early CRC (localised colon cancer; rectal cancer is its own sheet)
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
#   branch: Key | legend   one colour for a branch of the map; "branch: Key" under a step colours it.
#                          Here pMMR or MSS and dMMR or MSI-H; steps for both stay plain (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Content and layout follow the whiteboard plan (Jonan 2026-11-11: "EXACTLY as in whiteboard")
#   Trial lines give the trial name only; results are on the trial's card (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Trial {pmid N}  a trial with no Evidence row yet: PubMed link, dashed cell, listed for intake
#
# Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#   〔JSCCR evidence, strength〕 JSCCR guidelines 2024 for colorectal cancer, English edition
#     (Int J Clin Oncol 2025;30:2410-63, PMID 41186794), CQ boxes read from the PDF; East Asian guideline first
#   〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Colon Cancer 3.2026 (6 November 2026); category 2A unless stated
#   〔ESMO evidence, grade〕 ESMO localised colon cancer 2020 (Ann Oncol 2020;31:1291-305, PMID 32702383)
#     with the 2026 Express Update grades where they replace it
#   Japan-only options (S-1, UFT with leucovorin) sit on a ※ line (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   No testing band (Jonan 2026-11-11: PI3K testing need not come before the adjuvant decision).
#   MMR sits in the first step; DPYD testing "can be considered" (lower DPYD deficiency in East Asian patients)

course: Localised colon cancer
  Clinical stage I to III
    MMR or MSI on the biopsy of every tumour 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔JSCCR A, 1〕
    Non-obstructing T1–3: straight to colectomy
    ※ DPYD testing can be considered before a fluoropyrimidine
    next: Obstructing cancer
    next: cT4b or bulky nodes, pMMR
    next: T4 or bulky, dMMR
  Obstructing cancer
    ① Colectomy or diversion 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ② Stent as a bridge to colectomy 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔JSCCR B, 2〕 〔ESMO II〕
      ・No stent if systemic therapy is planned 〔JSCCR B, 2〕
    ~ ESCO {pmid 27924392}
    next: Colectomy
  cT4b or bulky nodes, pMMR
    branch: pMMR
    ① FOLFOX or CAPOX, then colectomy 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ~ FOxTROT
    - NeoCol
    next: Colectomy
  T4 or bulky, dMMR
    branch: dMMR
    ① Checkpoint inhibitor, then colectomy 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
    ~ NICHE-2
    ※ Less regression with chemotherapy first
      ~ FOxTROT
    next: Colectomy
  Colectomy
    ① En bloc regional nodes, at least 12 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
      ・JSCCR: D2 for cT1, D2 or D3 for cT2, D3 for cT3–T4a or node-positive
    ② Laparoscopic or robotic, experienced surgeon 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, C〕
      ・Robotic colectomy 〔JSCCR C, 2〕
    ~ JCOG0404 {pmid 28404155}
    ~ COLOR {pmid 19071061}
    ~ CLASICC {pmid 17634484}
    ~ COST {pmid 15141043}
    ※ Tis or slight cT1: endoscopic resection (JSCCR)
      ・Colectomy after it for T1b, LVI, poor differentiation or BD2–3 〔JSCCR B, 2〕
    next: Stage I and II, pMMR
    next: Stage III, pMMR
    next: Stage 0 to II, dMMR
    next: Stage III, dMMR
  Stage I and II, pMMR
    branch: pMMR
    ① Stage I: observation 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ② Low-risk II: observation 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔JSCCR A, 2〕
    ③ High-risk II: fluoropyrimidine, CAPOX or FOLFOX, or observation 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, C〕 〔JSCCR B, 2〕
    + QUASAR
    - MOSAIC
    ※ Stage II: ctDNA-guided treatment was non-inferior in one randomised phase 2 trial
      ・Not for treatment decisions outside a trial 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ~ DYNAMIC

  Stage III, pMMR
    branch: pMMR
    ① CAPOX or FOLFOX 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔JSCCR A, 1〕

    ※ Japan only: S-1 or UFT with LV 〔JSCCR A, 2〕
      ~ ACTS-CC {pmid 24942277}

      ~ NSABP C-07

  Stage III, dMMR
    branch: dMMR
