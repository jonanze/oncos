# oncOS board map: Early & Locally Adv NSCLC
#
# How to read and edit this file
#   Indent 2 spaces per level.
#   tree:   a decision tree; each plain line is a node, its children are indented under it
#   course: a disease course instead of a tree; each line at 2 spaces is a step, everything indented
#           under it is its content, and "next: Step | label" draws a line to a later step;
#           "join: Step | label" draws the same line but keeps the two steps in separate branches,
#           and several join: lines to one step with one label share an upright and that label
#   band:   a block above the tree for the whole sheet
#   panel:  a block beside the tree, joined by a brace to the node named after ">"
#   = text          the treatment for a branch, drawn bold after ★
#   + Trial | fig   a trial line; the mark records the result and is not drawn:
#                   + OS benefit, ~ surrogate only (PFS, response, single-arm), - no benefit or harm, * no comparison
#   fig with no digits is read from the Evidence row at each publish:
#     OS HR, PFS HR   the HR on the row's OS or PFS line
#     OS median       the medians on that line, "a vs b months"
#     response        the first percentage on the ORR line
#   fig with digits is shown as written; the map check fails if the row no longer has those numbers
#   Trial [t-...]   picks the Evidence row by its anchor when the name is not enough
#   Trial {pmid N}  a trial with no Evidence row yet: PubMed link, listed for intake
#   Trial {url https://...}  a randomised trial with no PubMed record yet (congress abstract): link, listed for intake
#   trial lines in a row draw as one ruled grid: trial name | key figure
#   ※ text          caveat             ° text          side effects, grey
#   ① text          numbered item; plain lines in a band or panel are text
#   | a | b |       a row of a ruled grid; the first row is the header, - leaves a cell empty
#   off:            sheet rows left off on purpose: Trial [t-...] | reason
#   source: ESGO [t-...]   a guideline row; 〔ESGO II, A〕 at the end of a statement marks it as resting on
#                   that guideline, with its printed level of evidence and grade, and opens its Evidence card
#   source: ESMO {url https://...}   the same for a guideline held only as a link; the tag opens the link
#   scale: Low | Intermediate | High-intermediate | High   four risk groups of an adjuvant or local-treatment
#                   decision, lowest first, each naming a grid row; those rows, and the ① and ※ lines in
#                   the same block that open with a risk group, take that group's colour. Nothing else is coloured
#
# Rules (Jonan 2026-11-08; node maps/check.cjs enforces the starred ones)
#   Layout fits the disease: a tree where care branches on tests, a course where one state follows another
#   Square lines only; joins line up; no result colours (the marks above are kept as data, not drawn);
#   colour only for risk groups, through scale: (Jonan 2026-11-09)
# * Every sheet row is on the map or under off: with a reason
# * Trial lines cite clinical trials, pooled analyses of trials or meta-analyses; no cohort, review,
#   consensus, PK, animal or healthy-volunteer papers (the statement can stay as plain text)
#   Superseded trials go under off: unless an option still in use rests on them
#   Standard options only; trials of options not in standard use go under off: (Jonan 2026-11-09, Lung-MAP)
#   Which trials go in the database and on a map: maps/inclusion.txt (Jonan 2026-11-10); trials screened
#   and left out are listed in the map folder's excluded.txt
#   Adjuvant, maintenance and consolidation choices are a later step after the primary treatment,
#   never a branch beside it (Jonan 2026-11-09)
#   Columns follow the decisions in order: surgery or no surgery; treatment before surgery or
#   upfront resection; treatment after the primary treatment (Jonan 2026-11-09)
# * Plain clinical wording: no arrows, no metaphors such as "backbone"
# * Labels on connecting lines start with a capital, like every other label
#   A statement that rests on a guideline rather than a trial carries its 〔source level, grade〕 tag;
#   where a trial and the guideline disagree, or the trial came later, a ※ line says so
#   Rule ⑦ (Jonan 2026-11-10): an option one guideline recommends, with no EU or US licence and no NCCN
#     listing, is not a map option; options NCCN alone lists sit on ※ lines
#   East Asian guidelines come first in Jonan's practice (2026-11-10): a JLCS option licensed in Japan is a numbered
#     option with its tag and a licence note, never a ※ line or under off:
#   Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#     〔NCCN category〕 NCCN Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer 9.2026 (22 September 2026), stage I–III pages,
#       NSCL-B, NSCL-C and NSCL-E; category 2A unless stated
#     〔ESMO evidence, grade〕 ESMO living guideline, early and locally advanced NSCLC v1.3 (August 2026)
#     〔JLCS evidence, strength〕 Japan Lung Cancer Society guideline 2025 (web edition, 6 November 2025),
#       chapter Ⅰ CQ23–25 and chapter Ⅱ sections 1–6; strength 1 strong or 2 weak, evidence A to D;
#       errata of 28 November 2025 and 15 January 2026 checked
#     〔ACCP evidence, strength〕 ACCP stage I NSCLC guideline 2025

band: Staging before treatment
  ① CT and PET-CT before surgery, for nodal staging 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔JLCS A, 1〕
    ~ Fischer 2009 [t-fischer-et-al] | Futile thoracotomy 21% vs 42%, PET-CT vs conventional staging; PLUS found the same with PET
  ② Nodes abnormal on CT or PET: endosonography with needle aspiration first 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔JLCS A, 1〕
    ~ ASTER | N2 or N3 sensitivity 79% vs 94%, surgical staging alone vs endosonography first
  ③ Mediastinoscopy can be omitted after negative systematic endosonography
    ~ MEDIASTrial | Unforeseen N2 8.8% vs 7.7%, no mediastinoscopy vs mediastinoscopy
    ※ Guidelines differ: NCCN advises mediastinoscopy before resection when endosonography is negative but CT or PET is positive in the mediastinum 〔NCCN 2A〕; JLCS finds the evidence insufficient either way; ESMO 1.3 does not address it
  ④ Peripheral cN0 tumour of 3 cm or less: no invasive staging. cN1, central, larger than 3 cm or tumour not FDG-avid: invasive staging, EBUS first 〔ESMO I, A〕; peripheral 3 cm or less with negative CT and PET: invasive staging optional; central: invasive staging 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ⑤ Brain MRI in stage II–III before curative treatment 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕; optional in stage IB 〔NCCN 2A〕; JLCS sets no stage limit 〔JLCS A, 1〕, except ground-glass nodules of 2 cm or less with a solid part of 25% or less

band: Testing before treatment
  ① EGFR and RET from stage IB, ALK from stage II at 4 cm or more 〔ESMO I, A〕
  ② PD-L1 before neoadjuvant chemo-immunotherapy 〔ESMO IV, A〕, and in every resected stage II–IIIA without it 〔ESMO I, A〕; EGFR, ALK, RET and PD-L1 from stage IB, and in central stage I 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ③ Biomarker results are needed before treatment starts; the MDT decides the order of systemic treatment and surgery 〔ESMO V, A〕
  ④ ctDNA is not used to add or withhold adjuvant treatment 〔ESMO IV, E〕

course: Early and locally advanced NSCLC
  Surgery
    Operable, and the MDT judges complete resection possible: stage I–II 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔JLCS C, 1〕, and selected stage III
    ※ N2 disease: the MDT chooses between surgery and definitive chemoradiotherapy 〔ESMO V, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔JLCS C, 1〕
    Extent of resection
    ① Peripheral, node-negative, 2 cm or less: sublobar resection, segmentectomy rather than wedge 〔ESMO I, A〕
      + JCOG0802 [t-jcog-0802-wjog-4607l] | OS HR 0.663
      ~ CALGB 140503 [t-calgb-140503] | DFS HR 1.01
      ※ ACCP rates lobectomy, segmentectomy and wedge resection as equivalent here 〔ACCP Moderate, Conditional〕; NCCN: segmentectomy or wedge resection for peripheral T1a–b N0 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ※ JLCS chooses by the solid part of the tumour: 25% or less of its diameter, segmentectomy or wedge; over 25% to 50%, segmentectomy; over 50%, segmentectomy or lobectomy 〔JLCS B, 1〕
    ② Solid on CT and larger than 2 cm: lobectomy 〔ESMO II, B〕; stage IA3–II: lobectomy or more 〔JLCS B, 1〕
    ※ VATS or robotic surgery in stage I–II 〔ESMO I, A〕; VATS in stage I 〔JLCS B, 1〕
    ※ Sample at least 3 mediastinal and 3 hilar node stations 〔ESMO III, A〕; at least 3 N2 and 1 N1 stations, or complete dissection 〔NCCN 2A〕; node dissection or sampling 〔JLCS B, 1〕
    next: Neoadjuvant or perioperative
    next: Upfront resection
  No surgery
    Medically inoperable or declining surgery, or stage III that cannot be completely resected
    ※ Operable stage I: surgery is preferred over SBRT 〔ACCP Low, Conditional〕 〔NCCN 2A〕; SBRT when surgery is declined 〔JLCS C, 1〕
    next: SBRT
    next: Chemoradiotherapy
  Neoadjuvant or perioperative
    Stage II–III with no EGFR, ALK or RET alteration: platinum doublet with a PD-1 or PD-L1 antibody for 3 or 4 cycles, then surgery 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕
    ① Neoadjuvant only: nivolumab; EU licence PD-L1 1% or more 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕 〔JLCS B, 2〕
      + CheckMate 816 | OS HR
    ② Perioperative: pembrolizumab, durvalumab, nivolumab (EU licence PD-L1 1% or more) or tislelizumab (EU licence, trial in China), continued after surgery 〔ESMO I, A〕; pembrolizumab, durvalumab or nivolumab 〔NCCN 1〕; a PD-1 or PD-L1 antibody 〔JLCS A, 2〕
      + KEYNOTE-671 | OS HR
      ~ AEGEAN | EFS HR
      ~ CheckMate 77T | 18-month EFS 70.2% vs 50.0%
      + RATIONALE-315 | OS HR
      ※ Toripalimab improved EFS; trial in China; not in ESMO 1.3, NCCN or JLCS
        ~ Neotorch | EFS HR
    ※ ESMO states no preference between neoadjuvant only and perioperative treatment
    ※ EGFR exon 19 or L858R, stage II–IIIB: osimertinib with or without chemotherapy before surgery, then osimertinib 〔NCCN 2A〕; pathological response improved, no survival data yet; ESMO and JLCS recommend resection, then adjuvant osimertinib. No guideline lists lorlatinib before surgery for ALK
      ~ NeoADAURA | MPR 26% vs 25% vs 2%
      ~ LORIN [t-lorin] | pCR 46.9%, single arm; conversion surgery in 75.0% of initially unresectable stage III
    ※ Superior sulcus tumour, T3–4 N0–1: concurrent chemoradiotherapy, then surgery 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔JLCS C, 1〕
    ※ Chemotherapy alone before surgery improved survival, when immunotherapy is not suitable 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔JLCS BQ1, B〕; not in stage I–IIA 〔JLCS C, 2〕
      + Preoperative chemotherapy meta-analysis 2014 [t-nsclc-meta-analysis-collaborative-group] | OS HR
    next: After chemo-immunotherapy and surgery
  Upfront resection
    next: After upfront resection
  SBRT
    Stage I, node-negative 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕; medically inoperable stage I–II 〔JLCS C, 1〕, with SBRT or another high-precision technique 〔JLCS B, 1〕
    ※ Central or ultracentral tumours get more fractions; JLCS advises caution with central tumours
      + CHISEL [t-chisel] | Local control HR
    ※ Atezolizumab added to SBRT did not improve OS or PFS
      - SWOG S1914 [t-swog-s1914-nrg-s1914] | OS HR
  Chemoradiotherapy
    Concurrent chemoradiotherapy with 2–3 cycles of a platinum doublet 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕 〔JLCS A, 1〕; 60 Gy in daily fractions, NCCN 60–70 Gy 〔NCCN 2A〕
      + Concurrent vs sequential meta-analysis [t-nsclc-collaborative-group-concomitant-versus-sequential-radiochemotherapy-meta-analysis] | 3-year OS 23.8% vs 18.1%
      + RTOG 9410 | OS median
    ※ Unfit for concurrent treatment: sequential chemoradiotherapy or radiotherapy alone 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕; radiotherapy alone 〔JLCS C, 1〕; older patients unfit for cisplatin: daily carboplatin with radiotherapy 〔JLCS B, 2〕
    ※ 74 Gy was worse than 60 Gy and is not used 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔JLCS B, 1〕
      - RTOG 0617 | OS 28.7 vs 20.3 months, 60 vs 74 Gy
    ※ Cisplatin–pemetrexed with radiotherapy was no better than cisplatin–etoposide; both remain preferred regimens, cisplatin–pemetrexed for non-squamous only 〔ESMO I, A〕
      - PROCLAIM | OS median
    ※ Durvalumab or nivolumab started during chemoradiotherapy did not improve PFS
      - PACIFIC-2 | PFS HR
      - CheckMate 73L | PFS HR
    next: After chemoradiotherapy, no progression | No progression
  After chemo-immunotherapy and surgery
    ① After a perioperative regimen: the same antibody alone, for the duration used in its trial, e.g. pembrolizumab up to 39 weeks 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕 〔JLCS A, 2〕
    ② After neoadjuvant nivolumab: adjuvant nivolumab optional 〔NCCN 1〕; ESMO also gives the option
  After upfront resection
    Chosen by pathological stage
    ※ Stages: ESMO uses TNM 8th edition, NCCN and JLCS the 9th
    ① Stage II–III: cisplatin doublet for 4 cycles 〔ESMO I, A〕; stage II–IIIB 〔JLCS A, 1〕; carboplatin doublet if cisplatin is unsuitable 〔ESMO II, B〕; stage IIB–IIIB, and IB–IIA with high-risk features 〔NCCN 2A〕
      + LACE meta-analysis | OS HR 0.89; 5-year OS +5.4%
        ※ By stage: IA HR 1.40, IB 0.93, II 0.83, III 0.83; benefit significant in stage II and III only
      + IALT | OS HR 0.86 in the first 5 years; 0.91 (0.81–1.02) at 7.5 years
      + JBR.10 | OS HR 0.69; stage II 0.59, stage IB not significant
      + ANITA | OS HR 0.80; stage II 0.71, IIIA 0.69, IB 1.10
      ※ Stage I under 4 cm without EGFR or RET: no adjuvant treatment 〔ACCP Moderate, Strong〕; JLCS differs, see ②
      ※ Carboplatin and paclitaxel in stage IB did not improve survival; the benefit in tumours of 4 cm or more came from an unplanned analysis
        - CALGB 9633 | OS median
    ② Node-negative, whole tumour over 2 cm, pathological stage IA3–IIIA, excluding T3 and T4 by invasion or nodule: tegafur-uracil for 1–2 years; adenocarcinoma 〔JLCS A, 1〕, other histology 〔JLCS C, 2〕; licensed in Japan, not in the EU or US; NCCN, ESMO and ACCP do not list it
      + Kato 2004 {pmid 15102997} | OS improved in stage I adenocarcinoma, P = 0.04
      + Tegafur-uracil meta-analysis 2005 {pmid 16051951} | OS HR 0.74; 5-year OS 81.5% vs 77.2%
    ③ No PORT after complete resection, including N2 or extranodal extension with clear margins 〔ESMO I, D〕; pathological stage I–II 〔JLCS A, 1〕
      ※ NCCN allows PORT for selected high-risk N2, such as extranodal extension or several stations involved 〔NCCN 2A〕; JLCS finds the evidence in N2 insufficient
      - Lung ART | 3-year DFS 47% vs 44%
    ④ Positive margin (R1): PORT may be given, 60 Gy in 30 fractions 〔ESMO III, C〕; re-resection preferred in stage IA–IIA 〔NCCN 2A〕
    next: No driver
    next: EGFR, ALK or RET
  No driver
    Stage II–IIIA, after chemotherapy: atezolizumab for 1 year if PD-L1 1% or more (EU licence 50% or more), or pembrolizumab for 1 year regardless of PD-L1 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕
    ※ JLCS: atezolizumab only at PD-L1 50% or more 〔JLCS C, 2〕, stage IIA–IIIB, evidence insufficient at 1–49%; pembrolizumab not addressed by JLCS. NCCN: benefit of pembrolizumab at PD-L1 under 1% unclear
      ~ IMpower010 | DFS HR
      ~ PEARLS/KEYNOTE-091 [t-pearls-keynote-091] | DFS HR
      ※ PEARLS: DFS improved in all patients; the co-primary in TPS 50% or more was not met
      ※ Adjuvant nivolumab for 1 year did not improve DFS
        - ANVIL | DFS HR
  EGFR, ALK or RET
    ① EGFR exon 19 or L858R, stage IB–IIIA: osimertinib for 3 years, after chemotherapy where given 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕; stage II–IIIB, after platinum chemotherapy 〔JLCS B, 2〕
      + ADAURA | OS HR
    ② ALK, stage II (4 cm or more) to IIIA: alectinib for 2 years in place of chemotherapy 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕 〔JLCS B, 2〕; chemotherapy first may be considered 〔ESMO II, C〕; JLCS covers stage II–IIIB
      ~ ALINA | DFS HR
      ※ Ensartinib also improved DFS; not in ESMO 1.3, NCCN or JLCS
        ~ ELEVATE | DFS HR
    ③ RET, stage IB–IIIA: selpercatinib for 3 years, also after radiotherapy 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕; not in JLCS
      ~ LIBRETTO-432 | 2-year EFS 92% vs 61%
  After chemoradiotherapy, no progression
    ① EGFR exon 19 or L858R: osimertinib until progression 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕 〔JLCS B, 2〕
      ~ LAURA | PFS HR
    ② No driver: durvalumab for 12 months, starting within 42 days of concurrent chemoradiotherapy; EU licence PD-L1 1% or more 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕 〔JLCS B, 1〕
      + PACIFIC | OS HR
    ③ No driver: sugemalimab for up to 24 months, after concurrent or sequential chemoradiotherapy; trial in China, EU licence PD-L1 1% or more 〔ESMO I, A〕; not in NCCN or JLCS
      ~ GEMSTONE-301 [t-gemstone-301] | PFS HR 0.64
    ※ After sequential chemoradiotherapy: osimertinib with EGFR exon 19 or L858R 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕; durvalumab 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔NCCN 2A〕, EU licence PD-L1 1% or more, not US
    ※ Durvalumab after sequential treatment: smaller PFS benefit than PACIFIC and no OS benefit at interim; effect similar after sequential and concurrent chemoradiotherapy, neither subgroup powered
      ~ PACIFIC-5 | PFS HR 0.75, OS HR 0.87; after sequential 0.75 (0.49–1.18), after concurrent 0.76
