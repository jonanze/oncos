# How to read and edit this file
#   Indent 2 spaces per level.
#   tree:   a decision tree; each plain line is a node, its children are indented under it
#   course: a disease course instead of a tree; each line at 2 spaces is a step, everything indented
#           under it is its content, and "next: Step | label" draws a line to a later step;
#           "join: Step | label" draws the same line but keeps the two steps in separate branches,
#           and several join: lines to one step with one label share an upright and that label
#   band:   a block above the tree for the whole sheet
#   panel:  a block beside the tree, joined by a brace to the node named after ">"
#   + Trial         a trial line, the trial's name only; it draws as a chip, and a tap opens its card with the
#                   results. No figures on maps (Jonan 2026-11-11). The mark records the result and is not drawn:
#                   + OS benefit, ~ surrogate only (PFS, response, single-arm), - no benefit or harm, * no comparison
#   Trial [t-...]   picks the Evidence row by its anchor when the name is not enough
#   Trial {pmid N}  a trial with no Evidence row yet: PubMed link, dashed chip, listed for intake
#   Trial {url https://...}  a randomised trial with no PubMed record yet (congress abstract): link, listed for intake
#   trial lines in a row draw as one row of chips, starting at the text of the line above
#   ※ text          caveat             ° text          side effects, grey
#   ・ text          one statement under the line above (a guideline's position, a licence, a condition);
#                   one statement per line, no chains of clauses joined by semicolons (Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   ① text          a numbered option or item, in order of guideline preference, in every step, node,
#                   band and panel. A single option is ① too (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   | a | b |       a row of a ruled grid; the first row is the header, - leaves a cell empty
#   off:            sheet rows left off on purpose: Trial [t-...] | reason
#   source: ESMO {url https://...}   a guideline, held as a link; 〔ESMO I, A〕 at the end of a statement marks it as
#                   resting on that guideline, with its printed level of evidence and grade, and opens the link.
#                   Guidelines are not Evidence rows (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   scale: Low | Intermediate | High-intermediate | High   four risk groups of an adjuvant or local-treatment
#                   decision, lowest first, each naming a grid row; those rows, and the ① and ※ lines in
#                   the same block that open with a risk group, take that group's colour
#   branch: Key | legend | hue   at the top: one fill colour for a branch of the map. Hues: blue, violet, sage,
#                   rose, sand, teal, apricot, olive (at most eight; without a hue the next unused one is taken).
#                   "branch: Key" under a step or tree node gives it that fill, "branch: Key pale" the pale tint
#                   (later steps of the same branch); a node's children take it too; a step for every branch stays
#                   plain grey; a panel takes the pale tint of the node it is joined to. Every map has branches
#                   (Jonan 2026-11-11, "more colours and variations"). Never on a map with scale: (2026-11-11)
#
# Rules (Jonan 2026-11-08; node maps/check.cjs enforces the starred ones)
#   Layout fits the disease: a tree where care branches on tests, a course where one state follows another
#   Whiteboard look (Jonan 2026-11-11, early colon map as the reference): filled rounded blocks, no title block,
#   bold numbered options, trial names as chips with no figures, lines with rounded corners and no arrowheads
#   that share one trunk where they fan out; spec doc https://claude.ai/artifact/Gt1iJC47Qa9LSMGDteysBJ
#   No result colours (the marks above are kept as data, not drawn); colour means one thing on a box: a box
#   carrying the risk ramp (scale:, a grid or a line opening with a risk group) stays grey, branch: colours go
#   on the other boxes (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Size and shape (Jonan 2026-11-11, "optimal box height/width ratio ... on a typical pc screen"): text runs in
#   columns of one reading width (460 px) balanced by the browser; a box takes another column only when every
#   column gets text and the columns come out near even; the columns per box are chosen so the whole map shows
#   at the largest scale in the 1600 x 850 map area of a 1920 x 1080 screen (most maps come out near 0.55
#   height to width); grid columns 220 px; bands share one row (canvas.js)
#   Full screen button beside Fit (evidence-maps.js); lines meet boxes at their middle and boxes centre on
#   each other; rounded corners; chips centred on their line (render-check.cjs)
# * Every sheet row is on the map or under off: with a reason
# * Trial lines cite clinical trials, pooled analyses of trials or meta-analyses; no cohort, review,
#   consensus, PK, animal or healthy-volunteer papers (the statement can stay as plain text)
#   Superseded trials go under off: unless an option still in use rests on them
#   Standard options only; trials of options not in standard use go under off: (Jonan 2026-11-09, Lung-MAP)
#   Which trials go in the database and on a map: maps/inclusion.txt (Jonan 2026-11-10); trials screened
#   and left out are listed in the map folder's excluded.txt
#   Adjuvant, maintenance and consolidation choices are a later step after the primary treatment,
#   never a branch beside it (Jonan 2026-11-09)
#   Columns follow the decisions in order: surgery or no surgery; treatment before surgery or
#   upfront resection; treatment after the primary treatment (Jonan 2026-11-09)
# * Plain clinical wording: no arrows, no metaphors such as "backbone"
#   Full words in prose: "chemotherapy", "with or without"; plain sentences with a subject and verb (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Language rule (Jonan 2026-11-13, every map): simple clinical statements with the backing guidelines as tags,
#     never "PAGA suggests", "ESMO uses" or "JGCA lists". One narrative: a point agreed by several guidelines is one
#     statement carrying all their tags, never repeated per guideline. A ・ line explains a difference only where
#     guidelines disagree (a cut-off, a line of treatment, a licence, a grade set before a trial)
#   Register (Jonan 2026-11-13): the impersonal register of clinical papers and abstracts. No second person, no
#     imperatives ("Test HER2" reads "HER2 testing before first-line treatment"), no conversational phrasing
#     ("case by case", "is not done", "adds little")
#   No explanation where no reason is known: two facts stay two lines, never joined by "as" or "because"
#     unless a guideline or trial gives that reason (Jonan 2026-11-13)
#   Side-effect lines open with "Toxicity of note:" (Jonan 2026-11-13)
#   PD-L1 and similar cut-offs: lead with the band every guideline agrees on, then one line per band where they
#     split (Jonan 2026-11-13)
# * Labels on connecting lines start with a capital, like every other label
#   A statement that rests on a guideline rather than a trial carries its 〔source level, grade〕 tag;
#   where a trial and the guideline disagree, or the trial came later, a ※ line says so
#   Rule ⑦ (Jonan 2026-11-10): an option one guideline recommends, with no EU or US licence and no NCCN
#     listing, is not a map option; options NCCN alone lists sit on ※ lines or as ・ lines in their drug class;
#     the tag names the guideline, so the text does not repeat it (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   East Asian guidelines come first in Jonan's practice (2026-11-10): a JLCS option licensed in Japan and marketed
#     internationally is a numbered option with its tag and a licence note, never ※-only or under off: (S-1).
#     A drug licensed in China only, with no international marketing, stays on a ※ line (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Presentation (Jonan 2026-11-11). node maps/render-check.cjs checks the drawn map and must report 0 faults
#     on every draft and release, with node maps/check.cjs
#     Square lines with no arrowheads. Every line label is centred on the line as drawn. No line runs under
#       a box or crosses another line
#     Steps are named, never numbered. Text never points to a step or an option by its number
#     No restated lines. Guidelines that agree share one line with all their tags. A ・ line is only for a
#       real disagreement
#     Options with the same population and guideline support merge into one line, with any licence
#       difference on a ・ line
#     One disease state is one step. Differences within it, such as de novo and metachronous disease, go on
#       ・ lines under the shared option
#     A vague guideline term such as "selected men" is defined from the guideline's text. Where a paraphrase
#       reads unclear, the guideline's own words are used
#     A laboratory threshold gives the SI unit beside the conventional unit: 50 ng/dL (1.7 nmol/L)
#     A wrapped line starts under its text. No word is carried down to a tag's line. An untagged statement
#       keeps no single word alone on its last line. Each trial name has a ・ bullet and never wraps
#   Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#     〔NCCN category〕 NCCN Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer 9.2026 (22 September 2026), stage I–III pages,
#       NSCL-B, NSCL-C and NSCL-E; category 2A unless stated
#     〔ESMO evidence, grade〕 ESMO living guideline, early and locally advanced NSCLC v1.3 (August 2026)
#     〔JLCS evidence, strength〕 Japan Lung Cancer Society guideline 2025 (web edition, 6 November 2025),
#       chapter Ⅰ CQ23–25 and chapter Ⅱ sections 1–6; strength 1 strong or 2 weak, evidence A to D;
#       errata of 28 November 2025 and 15 January 2026 checked
#     〔ACCP evidence, strength〕 ACCP stage I NSCLC guideline 2025

  ② Nodes abnormal on CT or PET: endosonography with needle aspiration first 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔JLCS A, 1〕

    ~ MEDIASTrial
    ※ Guidelines differ
    ・Mediastinoscopy before resection when endosonography is negative but CT or PET is positive in the mediastinum 〔NCCN 2A〕

  ④ Peripheral cN0 tumour of 3 cm or less: invasive staging is not required
  ・Invasive staging, EBUS first, for cN1, central, larger than 3 cm or non-FDG-avid tumours 〔ESMO I, A〕
  ・Invasive staging is optional for a peripheral tumour of 3 cm or less with negative CT and PET 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ・Invasive staging for a central tumour 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ⑤ Brain MRI in stage II–III before curative treatment 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕

  ・Without a stage limit 〔JLCS A, 1〕
  ・Except ground-glass nodules of 2 cm or less with a solid part of 25% or less
