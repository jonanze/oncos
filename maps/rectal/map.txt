# oncOS board map: Rectal Cancer (localised and locally advanced; metastatic disease is on the Advanced CRC map)
# Draft 2, 2026-11-11: JSCCR removed (Jonan), ASCO 2024 tags added from the full PDF
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
# Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#   〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Rectal Cancer 2.2026 (7 April 2026), algorithm and principles pages. 2A unless stated.
#     A preference appears only where NCCN prints one. The Discussion (31 October 2025) is older than the algorithm
#   〔ESMO evidence, grade〕 ESMO localised rectal cancer guideline 2025 (Ann Oncol 2025;36:1007-24), living guideline v1.0 July 2025
#   〔ASCO evidence, strength〕 ASCO locally advanced rectal cancer guideline 2024 (J Clin Oncol 2024;42:3355-75).
#     Its MRI and MMR statements are good practice statements, tagged 〔ASCO good practice〕
# JSCCR is not used on this map (Jonan 2026-11-12). The other colon maps keep it
# Camrelizumab has no licence outside China, so UNION is a ※ line

map: Rectal Cancer
source: NCCN {url https://www.nccn.org/guidelines/category_1}
source: ESMO {url https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2025.05.528}
source: ASCO {url https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.24.01160}
branch: IR | Intermediate risk, pMMR | blue
branch: HR | High risk, pMMR | teal
branch: dMMR | dMMR or MSI-H | violet
branch: WW | Watch and wait | sage

band: Staging
  ① Pelvic MRI that reports tumour height, depth, nodes, lateral nodes, EMVI and the distance to the MRF 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO good practice〕
  ② MMR or MSI on the biopsy 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO good practice〕
    ・RAS and BRAF testing is not recommended in localised disease 〔ESMO IV, D〕
  ③ CT of the chest and abdomen, CEA and colonoscopy to the caecum 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO III, A〕
  ④ Endorectal ultrasound to separate cT1 from cT2 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO II, A〕
  ※ FDG-PET is not indicated 〔NCCN 2A〕

band: cT1–2 N0
  ① Low-risk T1: local excision 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO III, A〕
  ② High-risk T1 or T2: TME 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔ESMO III, A〕
  ③ Organ preservation as the aim: CRT or short-course radiotherapy, then watch and wait or local excision after a good response 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO II, B〕
    ~ STAR-TREC
    - GRECCAR-2 {pmid 28601342}

course: Stage II and III rectal cancer
  Risk group on MRI
    ① High risk: cT4, involved MRF, cN2, EMVI or a lateral node of 7 mm or more 〔ESMO I, A〕
    ② Intermediate risk: cT2 N+ or cT3 N0–1, clear MRF
    ※ Upper third: TNT only for cT4 or an involved MRF 〔ESMO I, A〕
    next: Intermediate risk, pMMR
    next: High risk, pMMR
    next: dMMR or MSI-H
  Intermediate risk, pMMR
    branch: IR
    ① CRT or short-course radiotherapy, then TME 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
    + Swedish Rectal Cancer Trial
    + Dutch TME [t-eortc-40971]
    ~ CAO/ARO/AIO-94
    + MRC CR07 [t-mrc-cr07-ncic-ctg-c016]
    ② FOLFOX or CAPOX for 3 months, with CRT only after a poor response 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO Moderate to high, Conditional〕
      ・This is for a tumour that is not cT4, has a clear MRF and suits sphincter-sparing surgery
      ・ASCO limits this to the mid or upper rectum
    ~ PROSPECT
    ~ FOWARC
    - CONVERT [t-convert--rectal-cancer]
    ③ TNT 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・ESMO keeps TNT for high-risk disease
    ④ Organ preservation as the aim, tumour under 5 cm: CRT with a contact brachytherapy boost 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
    ~ OPERA
    ⑤ Upper rectum, cT3 N0 with a clear MRF: TME first 〔NCCN 2A〕
    next: Restaging
  High risk, pMMR
    branch: HR
    ① TNT: radiotherapy and 3 to 4 months of FOLFOX or CAPOX, all before TME 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO Moderate to high, Strong〕
      ・ASCO also offers TNT first for any low rectal tumour
    ~ RAPIDO
    + PRODIGE 23
    ~ STELLAR
    ~ TNTCRT
    - Polish II
    ② TNT not feasible: CRT or short-course radiotherapy, then TME 〔ESMO I, A〕
      ・Not fit for intensive treatment: CRT or radiotherapy alone 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ※ Short-course radiotherapy, then camrelizumab with CAPOX, raised the pCR rate in a trial in China
      ・Camrelizumab is not licensed in the EU or US
      ~ UNION
    next: Restaging
  dMMR or MSI-H
    branch: dMMR
    ① A PD-1 inhibitor for 6 months 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
      ・ESMO grades dostarlimab, and notes a US licence and no EU licence
      ・NCCN lists any PD-1 or PD-L1 inhibitor, with none preferred
    ~ Cercek 2022 [t-cercek-et-al]
    ~ Cercek 2025 [t-cercek-et-al--tumour-agnostic-basket-trials]
    ② Not a candidate for a checkpoint inhibitor: TNT 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ※ Chemotherapy before surgery is not recommended 〔ESMO V, D〕
    ※ Response is assessed at 12 and 24 weeks 〔ESMO III, A〕
    ※ Persistent disease at 6 months: CRT or short-course radiotherapy, then TME 〔NCCN 2A〕
    next: Restaging
  Restaging
    ① MRI, endoscopy and digital rectal examination 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
      ・A clinical complete response is a flat scar, a smooth wall and no restricted diffusion or nodes on MRI
    ② Near-complete response: a second assessment 4 to 8 weeks later 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, B〕
    ※ A biopsy is not needed to confirm a complete response 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO V, D〕
    ※ ctDNA has no role in the decision 〔NCCN 2A〕
    next: TME | Residual tumour
    next: Watch and wait | Clinical complete response
  TME
    ① TME, or partial mesorectal excision in the upper third 〔ESMO III, A〕
    ② Laparoscopic or robotic surgery by an experienced surgeon 〔ESMO I, A〕
      ・Open surgery is preferred for a threatened margin 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ~ CLASICC [t-uk-mrc-clasicc]
    ~ COLOR II {pmid 25830422}
    ~ COREAN {pmid 24837215}
    - ACOSOG Z6051 {pmid 26441179}
    - ALaCaRT {pmid 26441180}
    ③ Lateral nodes of 7 mm or more in short axis: dissection after treatment before surgery 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO IV, A〕
    ※ Lateral dissection is not indicated without suspicious nodes 〔NCCN 2A〕
    next: Adjuvant treatment
  Watch and wait
    branch: WW
    ① Watch and wait in an experienced team, for a patient committed to surveillance 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕
    ~ OPRA
    ② Examination, endoscopy and MRI every 3 months for 2 years, then every 6 months 〔ESMO I, A〕
      ・NCCN spaces MRI every 6 months for up to 3 years
    ※ Most regrowths arise in the bowel wall within 2 years
    ※ Watch and wait is not recommended after chemotherapy alone 〔ESMO I, D〕
    ※ Near-complete response in a baseline cT2 or cT3a N0 tumour: local excision 〔ESMO II, B〕
    next: Salvage resection | Regrowth
  Adjuvant treatment
    ① No preoperative treatment, stage III: CAPOX or FOLFOX 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
    ② No preoperative radiotherapy, with an involved margin, pT4b or poor TME: CRT 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO III, A〕
    ③ After CRT or short-course radiotherapy: a fluoropyrimidine with or without oxaliplatin may be considered 〔ESMO V, B〕
    ~ ADORE
    + CAO/ARO/AIO-04
    ※ After TNT, adjuvant chemotherapy is not generally recommended 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, D〕
    ※ PI3K pathway altered: aspirin for 3 years 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ~ ALASCCA
    ※ ctDNA is not used for adjuvant decisions 〔ESMO II, D〕
  Salvage resection
    branch: WW pale
    ① TME for every regrowth 〔ESMO IV, A〕
    ※ Local excision for regrowth is not recommended 〔ESMO IV, D〕

panel: Radiotherapy before surgery > Intermediate risk, pMMR
  ① Long-course CRT with capecitabine or infusional 5-FU 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕
    ・ASCO prefers long-course CRT to short-course radiotherapy
  ~ NSABP R-04
  ② Short-course radiotherapy, 5 fractions of 5 Gy 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
  ~ Polish I
  ~ TROG 01.04
  ~ Stockholm III
  ※ Do not add oxaliplatin to CRT 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・One of four trials found longer disease-free survival
    - STAR-01
    - ACCORD 12 [t-accord-12-0405-prodige-2]
    - NSABP R-04
    + CAO/ARO/AIO-04
  ※ Do not add irinotecan to CRT 〔NCCN 2A〕
    - ARISTOTLE
  ※ Age 75 or over: short-course radiotherapy was better tolerated than CRT, with no gain in R0 resection
    - PRODIGE 42 [t-prodige-42-gerico-12]

panel: Order of TNT > High risk, pMMR
  ① Radiotherapy first, then FOLFOX or CAPOX 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕
    ・This order is preferred when organ preservation is the aim 〔ESMO I, B〕
  ~ CAO/ARO/AIO-12
  ~ OPRA
  ② FOLFOX or CAPOX first, then CRT or short-course radiotherapy 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
  ③ FOLFIRINOX for 3 months, then CRT 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
    ・ASCO allows it for a higher risk of distant spread
    ・ESMO gives irinotecan only in this sequence
  + PRODIGE 23
  ※ TNT with short-course radiotherapy had more locoregional recurrence than CRT at 5 years
    ・NCCN advises caution with short-course radiotherapy in high-risk disease
    ~ RAPIDO
