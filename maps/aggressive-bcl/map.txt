# oncOS board map: High Grade B-Cell Lymphoma (large B-cell lymphoma from first line to relapse, with PMBCL, primary CNS lymphoma and Burkitt lymphoma)
# 2026-11-13, draft 1, not approved. Structure plan: board-maps/aggressive-bcl/structure-plan.md. Changes from draft 0: draft1-changes.md
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
# Scope (Jonan 2026-11-13): one map for the whole sheet. DLBCL and other large B-cell lymphoma from first line through relapse
#   is the course, including CAR-T and bispecific antibodies, with HGBL and transformed disease inside it. Primary mediastinal
#   B-cell lymphoma, primary CNS lymphoma and Burkitt lymphoma are side panels
# One narrative: a point several guidelines agree on is one statement with all their tags. A ・ line gives a difference
#   only where guidelines disagree (a cut-off, a licence, a grade, a recommendation against) or a condition
# Options NCCN alone lists sit on ※ lines (inclusion.txt, Jonan 2026-11-10): polatuzumab with R-GemOx, mosunetuzumab with
#   polatuzumab, glofitamab with polatuzumab, brentuximab vedotin with lenalidomide and rituximab, selinexor, consolidative
#   ISRT in localised HGBL, pembrolizumab and ICE consolidation in PMBCL, high-dose methotrexate with R-CHOP for CNS disease
#   at diagnosis, methotrexate 8 g/m² induction and non-myeloablative consolidation in primary CNS lymphoma.
#   Exception kept by Jonan's default 2 (question open): second-line lisocabtagene maraleucel for relapse after 12 months
#   without transplant is NCCN alone and stays numbered ①
# Tags give the grade as printed, on the statement the guideline graded. East Asian guidelines first:
#   〔JSH category〕 JSH practical guidelines 2023, DLBCL NOS chapter (Ohmachi, Int J Hematol 2025;122:3-16, online 28 May 2025).
#     Categories 1, 2A and 2B, definitions not printed. A bare 〔JSH〕 marks ungraded text. No bispecific antibody, tafasitamab,
#     loncastuximab or glofitamab with GemOx content. The Japanese 3.1 edition (2024) has no English version
#   〔JSH-BL category〕 JSH practical guidelines 2023, Burkitt lymphoma and HGBL chapter (Nishikori, Int J Hematol 2025;122:17-24).
#     Categories 2A and 2B only. WHO 2017 classification. No risk tiers, cycle numbers or doses
#   〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO Lymphomas living guideline v1.2, October 2026 (parent guideline Eyre, Ann Oncol 2025;36:1263-1284,
#     to which the tag links). Levels I to V, grades A to E. It prints no trial references and no CNS prophylaxis recommendation
#   〔EHA level, grade〕 EHA large B-cell lymphoma guideline (Thieblemont, HemaSphere 2025;9:e70207). Levels I to V, grades A to E.
#     Where its text, box and figure print different grades, the box grade is used and the plan lists the conflict. The
#     intravascular lymphoma box prints BSH grades, written here as 〔EHA I, B〕
#   〔BSH grade〕 BSH newly diagnosed large B-cell lymphoma 2024 (Fox, Br J Haematol 2024;204:1178-1192). GRADE: strength 1 or 2,
#     evidence A to C
#   〔BSH-RR grade〕 BSH relapsed or refractory large B-cell lymphoma 2025 (Chaganti, Br J Haematol 2025;206:1593-1603). GRADE
#   〔BSH-PM grade〕 BSH primary mediastinal B-cell lymphoma 2026 (Osborne, Br J Haematol 2026;209:1505-1517). GRADE
#   〔EHA-ESMO level, grade〕 EHA-ESMO primary CNS lymphoma guideline 2024 (Ferreri, HemaSphere 2024;8:e89)
#   〔EHA-ESMO-HIV level, grade〕 EHA-ESMO HIV-associated lymphomas guideline 2024 (Hübel, HemaSphere 2024;8:e150). The only European
#     source for Burkitt lymphoma. It applies the regimens used in HIV-negative disease
#   〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN B-Cell Lymphomas 4.2026 (20 May 2026) and NCCN CNS Cancers 4.2026 (2 November 2026), one tag.
#     2A unless stated, with preferred, other recommended and useful in certain circumstances
# Not read: BSH primary CNS lymphoma 2019, BSH secondary CNS lymphoma 2022, ASTCT 2023, CSCO 2026. No KSH, PAGA or Asian
#   CAR-T guideline exists for large B-cell lymphoma
# Written before the trial: JSH 2023 names no bispecific antibody trial. No guideline read cites DEB or the 2026 frontMIND,
#   MATRix/IELSG43 or Wilson papers. HOVON/SAKK 127 is cited by NCCN alone
# Licensing is taken from the guideline texts, labels not read. Glofitamab with GemOx and odronextamab: EU licence, no US
#   licence (ESMO, NCCN). Tafasitamab and lenalidomide with R-CHOP: no EU or US licence in first line (ESMO). Rituximab in
#   primary CNS lymphoma, and ibrutinib, lenalidomide and temozolomide in primary CNS lymphoma: no EU or US licence
#   (EHA-ESMO). Rituximab in Burkitt lymphoma: licensed in the EU and US for children only (FDA December 2021). Axicabtagene ciloleucel: not approved in second
#   line for PMBCL (BSH-PM). Japan: lisocabtagene maraleucel and axicabtagene ciloleucel are covered for HGBL (JSH-BL), and
#   first-line bendamustine, and liposomal doxorubicin and oxaliplatin for lymphoma, are not covered (JSH). Singapore status
#   of every agent was not checked. Tucidinostat is licensed in China for this use, with no EU or US licence (not from a
#   guideline, to be confirmed)
# Trial marks: + OS benefit, ~ surrogate endpoint, non-inferiority, single-arm or inconclusive, - no benefit or harm. Marks
#   follow the Evidence row or intake paper, with two exceptions. ZUMA-7 is + on the final analysis (PMID 37272527, cited by
#   EHA), which the row should take. SWOG S8736 is - on its final long-term analysis (PMID 27382104, cited by JSH and BSH).
#   The ~ marks for non-inferiority and inconclusive results go beyond the format header and await Jonan's confirmation
# Single-arm trials are cited only where a licence or recommendation rests on them and no randomised trial exists for that
#   option (inclusion.txt ①). Cohort and observational rows are under off:

band: CNS prophylaxis
  ① CNS-IPI in every patient 〔EHA III, A〕 〔BSH 1A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・High risk: CNS-IPI of 4 to 6, or testicular, renal, adrenal or breast involvement 〔JSH〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Two or more extranodal sites 〔EHA〕, three or more 〔BSH〕
    ・High risk: brain MRI and CSF examination 〔EHA III, A〕 〔BSH 2B〕
    ・MRI and CSF examination in selected patients 〔ESMO V, B〕
  ② High risk: high-dose methotrexate, 3 g/m² or more for two cycles, is an option 〔JSH 2B〕 〔EHA III, B〕 〔BSH 2B〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Given after a complete metabolic response to first-line treatment 〔EHA〕
    ・Two to four cycles of 3 to 3.5 g/m² 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・No recommendation on CNS prophylaxis 〔ESMO〕
    ・Retrospective cohorts show limited benefit 〔NCCN〕
  ※ In a retrospective analysis of 1,923 patients at ultra-high CNS risk, high-dose methotrexate did not reduce CNS relapse at 3 years, 8.1% against 9.3%, adjusted HR 1.13
    ・Ultra-high risk: CNS-IPI of 5 to 6, testicular, renal, adrenal or breast involvement, or three or more extranodal sites
    ・No guideline read cites this analysis
  ③ Intrathecal methotrexate or cytarabine is an option 〔JSH 2B〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Four to eight doses 〔NCCN〕
    ・Not recommended 〔EHA〕
  ④ Testicular lymphoma: R-CHOP, CNS prophylaxis and radiotherapy to the contralateral testis 〔EHA II, A〕 〔BSH 1B〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Intrathecal prophylaxis 〔JSH 2A〕
    ・Contralateral testicular radiotherapy 〔EHA I, A〕, at 25 to 30 Gy 〔NCCN 2A〕

  ⑤ Intravascular lymphoma: six cycles of R-CHOP with CNS prophylaxis, after brain MRI and CSF examination 〔EHA I, B〕 〔BSH 1B〕
