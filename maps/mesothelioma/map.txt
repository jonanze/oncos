# oncOS board map: Mesothelioma
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
#
# Rules (Jonan 2026-11-08/09; node maps/check.cjs enforces the starred ones)
#   Layout fits the disease: a course, first line then progression, split by what was given before
#   Square lines only; no result colours
# * Every sheet row is on the map or under off: with a reason
# * Trial lines cite clinical trials, pooled analyses of trials or meta-analyses only
#   Inclusion: maps/inclusion.txt; trials left out are in excluded.txt
#   ATOMIC-Meso off the map under ⑦ (recommended by ASCO 2025 only; no licence; Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   BEAT-meso kept under ⑥f (atezolizumab combined with bevacizumab and chemotherapy, each standard here) (Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   Standard options only; an option a guideline recommends but no licence covers stays on the line with that noted
#   ESMO-recommended options sit on the main line; where ESMO 2022 and ASCO 2025 disagree a ※ line says so
#   Maintenance is a later step after the primary treatment, never a branch beside it
# * Plain clinical wording
#   Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#     〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO 2022 (Popat); 〔ASCO quality, strength〕 ASCO 2025 (Kindler);
#     〔PSOGI quality, strength〕 PSOGI 2024 peritoneal consensus;
#     〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Mesothelioma: Pleural and Peritoneal 1.2027 (28 Oct 2026); options NCCN alone lists sit on ※ lines
#   Independent review 2026-11-10; accepted items applied

band: Diagnosis and staging
  ① Thoracoscopic biopsy when treatment is planned 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕; core needle biopsy when thoracoscopy or open biopsy is not possible 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ② Report the subtype: epithelioid, sarcomatoid or biphasic 〔ESMO IV, A〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕; the sarcomatoid percentage in biphasic tumours 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕; BAP1 and MTAP immunohistochemistry when the diagnosis is uncertain, including suspected mesothelioma in situ 〔ESMO IV, A〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕
  ③ Contrast CT of chest and upper abdomen 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕; PET-CT at staging, may be omitted when surgery is not considered 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕; required when surgery is considered 〔ESMO III, B〕
  ④ Response by modified RECIST 1.1 for mesothelioma 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ⑤ PD-L1, tumour mutational burden and MSI do not select patients for immunotherapy 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕; PD-L1 and tumour mutational burden 〔ESMO I, D〕
  ⑥ Offer germline testing to every patient 〔ASCO High, Strong〕; consider it 〔NCCN 2A〕, with pretest genetic counselling 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
  ※ ESMO 2022 advises against germline testing for a BAP1-deficient tumour without a family history suggesting BAP1 syndrome 〔ESMO V, D〕
  ※ Tumour genomic testing: not routine 〔ESMO III, D〕; not part of standard care 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕; NCCN recommends a multigene panel, including NTRK and ALK 〔NCCN 2A〕

band: Surgery
  ① Surgery is not offered on anatomic resectability alone 〔ASCO High, Strong〕; only for highly selected patients with clinical T1–3 N0 epithelioid disease 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕, within multimodality treatment after MDT review in an expert centre 〔ESMO III, C〕; NCCN limits surgery to cT1–2 N0 epithelioid disease 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ② Extended pleurectomy decortication rather than extrapleural pneumonectomy 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕; no surgery for sarcomatoid disease 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ③ Platinum and pemetrexed before or after surgery 〔ASCO High, Strong〕, 4 to 6 cycles; surgery alone is not enough 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
    ~ EORTC 1205 {pmid 38843916} | Feasibility only; no preferred sequence
  ※ Pleurectomy decortication or extended pleurectomy decortication added to chemotherapy shortened survival and increased serious adverse events
    - MARS 2 | OS 19.3 vs 24.8 months; HR 1.28 to 42 months; serious adverse events 318 vs 169
  ※ ESMO 2022 predates MARS 2; it lists sarcomatoid histology, contralateral or supraclavicular nodes and extrathoracic disease against surgery, without a stage limit; NCCN 1.2027 notes no evidence that surgery added to systemic therapy improves survival
  ※ Extrapleural pneumonectomy within trimodal therapy gave no benefit and possible harm; ASCO still allows it in highly selected patients in expert centres 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
    - MARS | OS HR 1.90 (0.92–3.93); 50 patients
  ※ Adjuvant hemithoracic radiotherapy is not standard; it can be considered in expert centres 〔ESMO II, D〕; after extrapleural pneumonectomy 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
    - SAKK 17/04 {pmid 26538423} | Locoregional relapse-free survival 9.4 vs 7.6 months, not significant, 54 patients
  ※ After pleurectomy decortication or lung-sparing surgery: hemithoracic IMRT 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕, in experienced centres 〔NCCN 2A〕; no radiotherapy before lung-sparing surgery 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
    + Trovo 2021 {pmid 33259933} | 2-year OS 58% vs 28%; HR 0.54, against palliative radiotherapy
  ※ Neoadjuvant immunotherapy may be offered 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕; ESMO 2022 calls its role unknown; NCCN marks nivolumab and ipilimumab suitable before surgery; evidence for adjuvant immunotherapy is insufficient 〔ASCO Very Low, Conditional〕

band: Effusion and radiotherapy
  ① Recurrent effusion: talc poudrage at thoracoscopy, preferred over VATS partial pleurectomy 〔ESMO I, A〕; or an indwelling pleural catheter 〔ESMO I, B〕, except in candidates for surgery because of tract seeding 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕; NCCN: drainage when disease may be operable, drainage or pleurodesis when it is not 〔NCCN 2A〕
    - MesoVATS {pmid 24942631} | OS HR 1.04 for VATS partial pleurectomy against talc pleurodesis
  ② Palliative radiotherapy to painful or symptomatic sites 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ③ No prophylactic radiotherapy to procedure tracts 〔ESMO I, D〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    - SMART {pmid 27345639} | Tract metastases 9% vs 16%, not significant
    - PIT {pmid 30920878} | Chest wall metastases 3.2% vs 5.3% at 6 months, not significant

band: Peritoneal mesothelioma
  ① Selected patients with completely resectable disease, fit for surgery: cytoreductive surgery with HIPEC in an expert centre 〔PSOGI Very Low, Strong〕; epithelioid without high-risk features 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ② Unresectable disease or unfit for surgery: systemic treatment largely as for pleural disease, all 〔NCCN 2A〕, including nivolumab and ipilimumab as preferred; no gemcitabine with ramucirumab; the EU and US immunotherapy licences cover pleural disease only
  ※ Biphasic or sarcomatoid histology, nodal disease or PCI over 17: systemic treatment first, then reassess for cytoreduction 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ※ After progression, without a checkpoint inhibitor before: atezolizumab with bevacizumab; NCCN only 〔NCCN 2A, other〕
  ※ No randomised trial of cytoreductive surgery with HIPEC; the evidence is cohort studies
  ※ CheckMate 743 and KEYNOTE-483 enrolled pleural disease only; CONFIRM included peritoneal disease

course: Unresectable pleural mesothelioma
  First line
    Non-epithelioid, PS 0–1
    ① Nivolumab and ipilimumab 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕, for up to 2 years 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
      + CheckMate 743 | OS HR
      + CheckMate 743, 5 years {pmid 41734361} | 5-year OS 14% vs 6%; HR 0.74
    ② Platinum and pemetrexed for up to 6 cycles with pembrolizumab, pembrolizumab continued for up to 2 years 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕; EU licence for non-epithelioid only
      + KEYNOTE-483 | OS HR
    ③ Platinum and pemetrexed 〔ESMO I, A〕, only when immunotherapy is contraindicated 〔ASCO High, Strong〕; with or without bevacizumab 〔NCCN 1, other〕
    ※ Non-epithelioid subgroups: OS HR 0.46 in CheckMate 743 and 0.57 in KEYNOTE-483
    ※ ESMO 2022 predates KEYNOTE-483 and lists chemotherapy as an option in all histologies; ASCO 2025 does not for non-epithelioid disease
    Epithelioid, PS 0–1
    ① Nivolumab and ipilimumab 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
    ② Platinum and pemetrexed, with or without bevacizumab 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕, 4 to 6 cycles 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕, with folic acid and vitamin B12; when given, bevacizumab continues until progression 〔ESMO I, A〕; bevacizumab is not licensed for mesothelioma
      + EMPHACIS | OS HR
      + MAPS | OS HR
    ③ Platinum and pemetrexed with pembrolizumab 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, other〕, moved down from preferred in NCCN 1.2027; US licence, the EU licence does not cover epithelioid
    ④ Asymptomatic, minimal disease, not for surgery: observation before chemotherapy may be offered 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ※ The order of ①②③ is not a ranking; ASCO calls nivolumab and ipilimumab or chemotherapy equally reasonable; epithelioid subgroups: OS HR 0.86 (0.69–1.08) in CheckMate 743 and 0.89 (0.70–1.13) in KEYNOTE-483
    ※ Carboplatin can replace cisplatin 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕; CheckMate 743 and KEYNOTE-483 allowed either
    ※ No bevacizumab at PS 2 or more, age over 75, cardiovascular disease or bleeding risk 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕; ASCO allows it in fit patients over 75
    ※ Stopping immunotherapy for severe toxicity does not lose its benefit 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
    ※ Tumour treating fields with platinum and pemetrexed: insufficient evidence 〔ASCO Very Low, NA〕; NCCN does not list it
    ※ Atezolizumab added to carboplatin, pemetrexed and bevacizumab did not improve OS; non-epithelioid subgroup better, prespecified; not licensed; NCCN names it as not included
      - BEAT-meso {pmid 39814199} | OS HR 0.84 (0.66–1.06); non-epithelioid 0.51
    PS 2 or more
    ① PS 2: single-agent chemotherapy or palliative care alone; PS 3 or more: palliative care 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
    ※ ESMO 2022 offers platinum and pemetrexed, with or without bevacizumab, up to PS 2
    ※ Pemetrexed not possible: platinum with gemcitabine; unfit for a platinum doublet: pemetrexed or vinorelbine alone 〔NCCN 2A, certain circumstances〕

  Maintenance or observation
    ① After bevacizumab with cisplatin and pemetrexed: continue bevacizumab 〔ESMO I, A〕, graded in the ESMO algorithm only; MAPS did not test maintenance separately
    ② Otherwise a break after 4 to 6 cycles 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕; switch maintenance gemcitabine can be considered, not routine 〔ESMO II, C〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕; gemcitabine improved PFS, not OS
      ~ NVALT19 {pmid 33515500} | PFS HR 0.48
    ※ No pemetrexed maintenance 〔ESMO II, E〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
      - CALGB 30901 {pmid 32727707} | PFS HR 0.99; OS HR 0.86, not significant
    next: Second line and beyond | Progression
  Second line and beyond
    After chemotherapy alone
    ① Immunotherapy: nivolumab and ipilimumab 〔ESMO II, C〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕, or nivolumab alone 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕, or pembrolizumab alone 〔ESMO II, C〕; nivolumab, or nivolumab and ipilimumab 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕; NCCN does not support pembrolizumab alone; none licensed in this setting in the EU or US
      ~ MAPS2 | 12-week disease control 44% and 50%
      ~ CONFIRM | PFS HR 0.65; OS HR 0.81, not significant
      - PROMISE-meso {pmid 32976938} | Pembrolizumab against gemcitabine or vinorelbine: PFS HR 1.06; OS HR 1.12
    ② Gemcitabine with ramucirumab 〔ESMO III, C〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A, other〕; ramucirumab is not licensed for mesothelioma
      + RAMES {pmid 34499874} | Randomised phase II: OS 13.8 vs 7.5 months; HR 0.71
    ③ Vinorelbine or gemcitabine alone 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔NCCN 2A, other〕; oral vinorelbine 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕; PFS gain, no OS gain
      ~ VIM | PFS HR
    ④ Pemetrexed alone if not given before 〔ESMO III, C〕 〔NCCN 1, other〕
      ~ Jassem 2008 {pmid 18375898} | Response 18.7% vs 1.7%; OS 8.4 vs 9.7 months, against supportive care
    ⑤ Platinum and pemetrexed again in selected patients 〔ESMO II, B〕, after disease control of more than 6 months 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕; after a good response 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ※ Both guideline grades for nivolumab alone predate the final CONFIRM analysis, in which OS was not significant
    After nivolumab and ipilimumab
    ① Platinum and pemetrexed, with or without bevacizumab 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
    ※ No randomised trial after first-line nivolumab and ipilimumab; ESMO 2022 routes these patients to first-line chemotherapy, whose grade comes from untreated patients
    After pembrolizumab with chemotherapy, or chemotherapy then immunotherapy
    ① Vinorelbine or gemcitabine, with or without ramucirumab 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕
    ② Platinum and pemetrexed again after disease control of more than 6 months 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
    ③ Immunotherapy again when disease was controlled and progressed after treatment had been completed 〔ASCO Very Low, Conditional〕
    ※ After pembrolizumab with chemotherapy: nivolumab and ipilimumab; NCCN only, new in 1.2027, no trial in this setting 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
    ※ After two lines, ESMO 2022 finds no evidence for routine further treatment; consider a clinical trial 〔ESMO V, C〕
