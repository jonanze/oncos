# oncOS board map: Mesothelioma
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
#
# Rules (Jonan 2026-11-08/09; node maps/check.cjs enforces the starred ones)
#   Layout fits the disease: a course, first line then progression, split by what was given before
#   Square lines only; no result colours
# * Every sheet row is on the map or under off: with a reason
# * Trial lines cite clinical trials, pooled analyses of trials or meta-analyses only
#   Inclusion: maps/inclusion.txt; trials left out are in excluded.txt
#   ATOMIC-Meso out under ⑦ (recommended by ASCO 2025 only; no licence; row removed; in excluded.txt; Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   BEAT-meso kept under ⑥f (atezolizumab combined with bevacizumab and chemotherapy, each standard here) (Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   Standard options only; an option a guideline recommends but no licence covers stays on the line with that noted
#   Shared options sit on the main line with the tags that agree; each guideline that differs gets its own ・ line
#   One statement per line, no semicolon chains: a guideline's position, a licence or a condition goes on a
#     ・ line under the line it qualifies (Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   Maintenance is a later step after the primary treatment, never a branch beside it
# * Plain clinical wording
#   Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#     〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO 2022 (Popat); 〔ASCO quality, strength〕 ASCO 2025 (Kindler);
#     〔PSOGI quality, strength〕 PSOGI 2024 peritoneal consensus;
#     〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Mesothelioma: Pleural and Peritoneal 1.2027 (28 Oct 2026); options NCCN alone lists sit on ※ lines
#       or as ・ lines in their drug class; the tag names the guideline, so the text does not repeat it (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#     〔STS statement〕 STS 2026 expert consensus on multimodal treatment (Velotta); statements are ungraded (Delphi, at least 75% agreement), so the tag gives the statement number
#     〔JLCS evidence, strength〕 Japan Lung Cancer Society guideline 2025 (web edition), part 2 pleural mesothelioma:
#       diagnosis CQ1–14, treatment CQ1–15; strength 1 strong or 2 weak, evidence A to D (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Independent review 2026-11-10; accepted items applied

band: Diagnosis and staging
  ① Thoracoscopic biopsy when treatment is planned 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔STS 1.1〕
  ・As few incisions as possible, placed in line with any later thoracotomy 〔STS 1.2〕
  ・Biopsy even when pleural fluid cytology is negative 〔STS 1.4〕
  ・Surgical pleural biopsy under general anaesthesia, with enough tissue 〔JLCS C, 1〕
  ・Core needle biopsy when thoracoscopy or open biopsy is not possible 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・CT-guided needle biopsy for a mass when surgical biopsy is not suitable 〔JLCS C, 1〕
  ・Blood markers and effusion markers, including hyaluronic acid, do not establish the diagnosis 〔JLCS D, 1〕
  ② Report the subtype: epithelioid, sarcomatoid or biphasic 〔ESMO IV, A〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕
  ・The sarcomatoid percentage in biphasic tumours 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・BAP1 and MTAP immunohistochemistry when the diagnosis is uncertain, including suspected mesothelioma in situ 〔ESMO IV, A〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕
  ・BAP1 and MTAP immunohistochemistry and CDKN2A FISH to separate epithelioid mesothelioma from reactive proliferation 〔JLCS B, 1〕
  ・BAP1 and MTAP immunohistochemistry on effusion cytology 〔JLCS B, 1〕
  ・An immunohistochemistry panel of at least two positive and two negative markers to separate epithelioid mesothelioma from lung adenocarcinoma 〔JLCS B, 1〕
  ・Grade reported for epithelioid tumours 〔JLCS C, 2〕
  ③ Contrast CT of chest and upper abdomen 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・Contrast CT for diagnosis and staging 〔JLCS C, 1〕
  ・PET-CT for staging 〔JLCS C, 1〕
  ・No MRI added to CT 〔JLCS D, 1〕
  ・PET-CT at staging, may be omitted when surgery is not considered 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・PET-CT required when surgery is considered 〔ESMO III, B〕, in every patient 〔STS 2.1〕
  ④ EBUS or mediastinoscopy when imaging suggests nodal disease, laparoscopy when abdominal disease is suspected 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔STS 2.2, 11.1〕
  ⑤ Response by modified RECIST 1.1 for mesothelioma 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ⑥ PD-L1, tumour mutational burden and MSI do not select patients for immunotherapy 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕
  ・PD-L1 and tumour mutational burden do not select patients 〔ESMO I, D〕
  ⑦ Offer germline testing to every patient 〔ASCO High, Strong〕
  ・Germline testing can be considered 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ・With pretest genetic counselling 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
  ・No germline testing for a BAP1-deficient tumour without a family history suggesting BAP1 syndrome 〔ESMO V, D〕
  ※ Tumour genomic testing
  ・Not routine 〔ESMO III, D〕
  ・Not part of standard care 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕
  ・A multigene panel, including NTRK and ALK 〔NCCN 2A〕

band: Surgery
  ① Surgery is not offered on anatomic resectability alone 〔ASCO High, Strong〕
  ・Only for highly selected patients with clinical T1–3 N0 epithelioid disease 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・Surgery for clinical stage I–IIIA when macroscopic complete resection is expected 〔JLCS C, 1〕
  ・Surgery for biphasic disease 〔JLCS C, 2〕
  ・Within multimodality treatment after MDT review in an expert centre 〔ESMO III, C〕 〔STS 3.1, 4.1〕
  ・Only for cT1–2 N0 epithelioid disease 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ② Extended pleurectomy decortication rather than extrapleural pneumonectomy 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ・Pleurectomy decortication 〔JLCS C, 1〕
  ・Extrapleural pneumonectomy 〔JLCS C, 2〕
  ・Pleurectomy decortication or extended pleurectomy decortication 〔STS 7.1〕
  ・The aim is complete macroscopic resection 〔STS 9.1〕
  ・No partial debulking by any approach 〔STS 9.3〕
  ・No surgery for sarcomatoid disease 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔JLCS C, 2〕
  ③ Platinum and pemetrexed before or after surgery, 4 to 6 cycles 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔STS 5〕
  ・Cisplatin and pemetrexed before or after surgery 〔JLCS C, 1〕
  ・Surgery alone is not enough 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・Adjuvant treatment after upfront surgery 〔STS 6〕
  ④ Lymph node dissection at surgery, including hilar, mediastinal, internal mammary, diaphragmatic, pericardial and intercostal stations 〔STS 8.1, 8.2〕
    ~ EORTC 1205 [t-eortc-1205] | Feasibility only, no preferred sequence
  ※ Pleurectomy decortication or extended pleurectomy decortication added to chemotherapy shortened survival and increased serious adverse events
    - MARS 2 | OS 19.3 vs 24.8 months, HR 1.28 to 42 months, serious adverse events 318 vs 169
  ※ ESMO 2022 predates MARS 2
  ・It lists sarcomatoid histology, contralateral or supraclavicular nodes and extrathoracic disease against surgery, without a stage limit
  ・NCCN 1.2027 notes no evidence that surgery added to systemic therapy improves survival
  ※ JLCS 2025 still recommends surgery after considering MARS and MARS 2
  ・It asks for surgeons, physicians and radiation oncologists experienced in mesothelioma
  ※ STS 2026 keeps lung-sparing surgery for selected patients
  ・It attributes the MARS 2 result to CT-only staging and 9% 90-day mortality
  ・Surgery for confirmed ipsilateral N1 disease 〔STS 11.2〕, after EBUS or mediastinoscopy when imaging suggests N1 〔STS 11.1〕
  ・No surgery for contralateral or supraclavicular N2 disease 〔STS 11.3〕
  ・It cites NCCN 2.2026 text on surgery in N1 and N2 disease that NCCN 1.2027 removed
  ※ Extrapleural pneumonectomy within trimodal therapy gave no benefit and possible harm
  ・Still allowed in highly selected patients in expert centres 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・Only when pleurectomy decortication cannot clear the tumour and prognostic factors are favourable 〔STS 7.2〕
    - MARS | OS HR 1.90 (0.92–3.93), 50 patients

band: Treatment around surgery
  ※ Adjuvant hemithoracic radiotherapy is not standard
  ・It can be considered in expert centres 〔ESMO II, D〕
  ・It may be offered after extrapleural pneumonectomy, preferably in expert centres 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・Hemithoracic radiotherapy after extrapleural pneumonectomy 〔JLCS D, 2〕, by 3D conformal radiotherapy or IMRT 〔JLCS C, 2〕
    - SAKK 17/04 [t-sakk-17-04] | Locoregional relapse-free survival 9.4 vs 7.6 months with and without radiotherapy, no between-arm test
  ※ After pleurectomy decortication or lung-sparing surgery: hemithoracic IMRT 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕, in experienced centres 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ・No radiotherapy after pleurectomy decortication or without surgery 〔JLCS D, 2〕
  ・No radiotherapy before lung-sparing surgery 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
    + Trovo 2021 {pmid 33259933} | 2-year OS 58% vs 28%, HR 0.54, against palliative radiotherapy
  ※ Neoadjuvant immunotherapy may be offered 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕
  ・ESMO 2022 calls its role unknown
  ・NCCN marks nivolumab and ipilimumab suitable before surgery
  ・Evidence for adjuvant immunotherapy is insufficient 〔ASCO Very Low, Conditional〕
  ※ Intraoperative regional treatment (chemotherapy, povidone-iodine lavage or photodynamic therapy) may be considered 〔STS 12.1〕
  ・No randomised comparison
  ・Its benefit is unproven 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕

band: Effusion and radiotherapy
  ① Recurrent effusion: talc poudrage at thoracoscopy, preferred over VATS partial pleurectomy 〔ESMO I, A〕
  ・An indwelling pleural catheter is an alternative 〔ESMO I, B〕
  ・No catheter in candidates for surgery, because of tract seeding 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・Pleurodesis after drainage to control the effusion 〔JLCS C, 2〕
  ・Drainage when disease may be operable, drainage or pleurodesis when it is not 〔NCCN 2A〕
    - MesoVATS [t-mesovats] | OS HR 1.04 for VATS partial pleurectomy against talc pleurodesis
  ② Palliative radiotherapy to painful or symptomatic sites 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ③ No prophylactic radiotherapy to procedure tracts 〔ESMO I, D〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    - SMART [t-smart] | Tract metastases 8.8% vs 15.8%, OR 0.51, not significant
    - PIT [t-pit] | Chest wall metastases 3.2% vs 5.3% at 6 months, not significant
  ※ Recurrence after surgery
  ・Resection of an isolated recurrence 〔STS 13.1〕 〔JLCS D, 2〕, or SBRT to an isolated pleural recurrence 〔STS 13.2〕
  ・Conventionally fractionated or mildly hypofractionated radiotherapy for diffuse or bulky recurrence in selected patients 〔STS 13.3〕

band: Peritoneal mesothelioma
  ① Selected patients with completely resectable disease, fit for surgery: cytoreductive surgery with HIPEC in an expert centre 〔PSOGI Very Low, Strong〕
  ・For epithelioid disease without high-risk features 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ② Unresectable disease or unfit for surgery: systemic treatment largely as for pleural disease 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ・Every regimen is category 2A
  ・Nivolumab and ipilimumab is preferred
  ・No gemcitabine with ramucirumab
  ・The EU and US immunotherapy licences cover pleural disease only
  ※ Biphasic or sarcomatoid histology, nodal disease or PCI over 17: systemic treatment first, then reassess for cytoreduction 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ※ After progression, without a checkpoint inhibitor before: atezolizumab with bevacizumab 〔NCCN 2A, other recommended〕
  ※ No randomised trial has tested cytoreductive surgery with HIPEC
  ・The evidence is from cohort studies
  ※ CheckMate 743 and KEYNOTE-483 enrolled pleural disease only
  ・CONFIRM included peritoneal disease

course: Unresectable pleural mesothelioma
  First line
    Chosen by histology and performance status
    ※ JLCS does not split first line by histology
    ・Nivolumab and ipilimumab at PS 0–2 〔JLCS B, 1〕
    ・Platinum and pemetrexed with a PD-1 antibody at PS 0–2 〔JLCS B, 2〕
    ・Platinum and pemetrexed at PS 0–2 〔JLCS B, 2〕
    ・Any of the three at age 75 or over with PS 0–2 〔JLCS D, 2〕
    next: Non-epithelioid, PS 0–1
    next: Epithelioid, PS 0–1
    next: PS 2 or more
  Non-epithelioid, PS 0–1
    ① Nivolumab and ipilimumab 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
    ・For up to 2 years 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
      + CheckMate 743 [t-checkmate-743] | 5-year OS 14% vs 6%, HR 0.74
    ② Platinum and pemetrexed for up to 6 cycles with pembrolizumab, pembrolizumab continued for up to 2 years 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
    ・EU licence for non-epithelioid only
      + KEYNOTE-483 | OS HR
    ③ Platinum and pemetrexed 〔ESMO I, A〕
    ・Only when immunotherapy is contraindicated 〔ASCO High, Strong〕
    ・With or without bevacizumab 〔NCCN 1, other recommended〕
    ※ Non-epithelioid subgroups: OS HR 0.46 in CheckMate 743 and 0.57 in KEYNOTE-483
    ※ ESMO 2022 predates KEYNOTE-483 and lists chemotherapy as an option in all histologies
    ・ASCO 2025 does not for non-epithelioid disease
    join: Maintenance or observation | No progression
    join: Second line and beyond | Progression
  Epithelioid, PS 0–1
    ① Nivolumab and ipilimumab 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
    ② Platinum and pemetrexed, with or without bevacizumab 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
    ・4 to 6 cycles 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕, with folic acid and vitamin B12
    ・When given, bevacizumab continues until progression 〔ESMO I, A〕
    ・Bevacizumab added to cisplatin and pemetrexed 〔JLCS B, 2〕
    ・Bevacizumab is not licensed for mesothelioma
      + EMPHACIS | OS HR
      + MAPS | OS HR
    ③ Platinum and pemetrexed with pembrolizumab 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, other recommended〕
    ・Moved down from preferred in NCCN 1.2027
    ・US licence, the EU licence does not cover epithelioid
    ④ Asymptomatic, minimal disease, not for surgery: observation before chemotherapy may be offered 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ※ The order of ①②③ is not a ranking
    ・ASCO calls nivolumab and ipilimumab or chemotherapy equally reasonable
    ・JLCS recommends nivolumab and ipilimumab strongly and the other two weakly
    ・Epithelioid subgroups: OS HR 0.86 (0.69–1.08) in CheckMate 743 and 0.89 (0.70–1.13) in KEYNOTE-483
    ※ Carboplatin can replace cisplatin 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
    ・CheckMate 743 and KEYNOTE-483 allowed either
    ※ No bevacizumab at PS 2 or more, age over 75, cardiovascular disease or bleeding risk 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
    ・ASCO allows it in fit patients over 75
    ※ Stopping immunotherapy for severe toxicity does not lose its benefit 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
    ※ Evidence for tumour treating fields with platinum and pemetrexed is insufficient 〔ASCO Very Low, NA〕
    ・NCCN does not list it
    ※ Atezolizumab added to carboplatin, pemetrexed and bevacizumab did not improve OS
    ・This regimen is not licensed
    ・The prespecified non-epithelioid subgroup did better
    ・NCCN names it as not included
      - BEAT-meso [t-beat-meso] | OS HR 0.84 (0.66–1.06), non-epithelioid 0.50
    join: Maintenance or observation | No progression
    join: Second line and beyond | Progression
  PS 2 or more
    ① PS 2: single-agent chemotherapy or palliative care alone 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
    ・PS 3 or more: palliative care
    ・No drug treatment at PS 3–4 〔JLCS D, 1〕
    ※ ESMO 2022 offers platinum and pemetrexed, with or without bevacizumab, up to PS 2
    ※ Other chemotherapy options 〔NCCN 2A, useful in certain circumstances〕
    ・Pemetrexed not possible: platinum with gemcitabine
    ・Unfit for a platinum doublet: pemetrexed or vinorelbine alone
    join: Maintenance or observation | No progression
    join: Second line and beyond | Progression
  Maintenance or observation
    ① After bevacizumab with cisplatin and pemetrexed: continue bevacizumab 〔ESMO I, A〕
    ・Graded in the ESMO algorithm only
    ・MAPS did not test maintenance separately
    ② Otherwise a break after 4 to 6 cycles 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
    ・Switch maintenance gemcitabine can be considered, not routine 〔ESMO II, C〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕 〔JLCS C, 2〕
    ・Gemcitabine improved PFS, not OS
      ~ NVALT19 [t-nvalt-19] | PFS HR 0.48
    ※ No pemetrexed maintenance 〔ESMO II, E〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔JLCS C, 2〕
      - CALGB 30901 [t-calgb-30901] | PFS HR 0.99, OS HR 0.86, not significant
    next: Second line and beyond | Progression
  Second line and beyond
    After chemotherapy alone
    ① Immunotherapy
    ・Nivolumab alone 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔JLCS B, 1〕
    ・Nivolumab and ipilimumab 〔ESMO II, C〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
    ・Nivolumab alone after chemotherapy is licensed in Japan (2018)
    ・None is licensed in this setting in the EU or US
    ・Pembrolizumab alone 〔ESMO II, C〕
    ・NCCN does not list pembrolizumab alone
      ~ MAPS2 | 12-week disease control 44% and 50%
      ~ CONFIRM | PFS HR 0.65, OS HR 0.81, not significant
      - PROMISE-meso [t-promise-meso] | Pembrolizumab against gemcitabine or vinorelbine: PFS HR 1.06, OS HR 1.12
    ② Gemcitabine with ramucirumab 〔ESMO III, C〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A, other recommended〕
    ・Ramucirumab is not licensed for mesothelioma
    ・JLCS does not list it
      + RAMES [t-rames] | Randomised phase II: OS 13.8 vs 7.5 months, HR 0.71
    ③ Vinorelbine or gemcitabine alone 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔NCCN 2A, other recommended〕
    ・Oral vinorelbine 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
    ・Vinorelbine, gemcitabine, or both together 〔JLCS C, 2〕
    ・Vinorelbine improved PFS but not OS
      ~ VIM | PFS HR
    ④ Pemetrexed alone if not given before 〔ESMO III, C〕 〔NCCN 1, other recommended〕
    ・Pemetrexed alone, including retreatment 〔JLCS C, 2〕
      ~ Jassem 2008 {pmid 18375898} | Response 18.7% vs 1.7%, OS 8.4 vs 9.7 months, against supportive care
    ⑤ Platinum and pemetrexed again in selected patients 〔ESMO II, B〕
    ・After disease control of more than 6 months 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
    ・After a good response 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ※ Both guideline grades for nivolumab alone predate the final CONFIRM analysis, in which OS was not significant
    After nivolumab and ipilimumab
    ① Platinum and pemetrexed, with or without bevacizumab 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
    ※ No randomised trial after first-line nivolumab and ipilimumab
    ・ESMO 2022 routes these patients to first-line chemotherapy, whose grade comes from untreated patients
    After pembrolizumab with chemotherapy, or chemotherapy then immunotherapy
    ① Vinorelbine or gemcitabine, with or without ramucirumab 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕
    ② Platinum and pemetrexed again after disease control of more than 6 months 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
    ③ Immunotherapy again when disease was controlled and progressed after treatment had been completed 〔ASCO Very Low, Conditional〕
    ※ After pembrolizumab with chemotherapy: nivolumab and ipilimumab 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
    ・No other guideline lists it
    ・New in NCCN 1.2027
    ・No trial has tested it in this setting
    ※ After two lines, ESMO 2022 finds no evidence for routine further treatment
    ・Consider a clinical trial 〔ESMO V, C〕
