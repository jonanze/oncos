# oncOS board map: Mesothelioma
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
#
# Rules (Jonan 2026-11-08/09; node maps/check.cjs enforces the starred ones)
#   Layout fits the disease: a course, first line then progression, split by what was given before
#   Square lines only; no result colours
# * Every sheet row is on the map or under off: with a reason
# * Trial lines cite clinical trials, pooled analyses of trials or meta-analyses only
#   Inclusion: maps/inclusion.txt; trials left out are in excluded.txt
#   ATOMIC-Meso out under ⑦ (recommended by ASCO 2025 only; no licence; row removed; in excluded.txt; Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   BEAT-meso kept under ⑥f (atezolizumab combined with bevacizumab and chemotherapy, each standard here) (Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   Standard options only; an option a guideline recommends but no licence covers stays on the line with that noted
#   Shared options sit on the main line with the tags that agree; each guideline that differs gets its own ・ line
#   One statement per line, no semicolon chains: a guideline's position, a licence or a condition goes on a
#     ・ line under the line it qualifies (Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   Maintenance is a later step after the primary treatment, never a branch beside it
# * Plain clinical wording
#   Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#     〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO 2022 (Popat); 〔ASCO quality, strength〕 ASCO 2025 (Kindler);
#     〔PSOGI quality, strength〕 PSOGI 2024 peritoneal consensus;
#     〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Mesothelioma: Pleural and Peritoneal 1.2027 (28 Oct 2026); options NCCN alone lists sit on ※ lines
#       or as ・ lines in their drug class; the tag names the guideline, so the text does not repeat it (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#     〔STS statement〕 STS 2026 expert consensus on multimodal treatment (Velotta); statements are ungraded (Delphi, at least 75% agreement), so the tag gives the statement number
#     〔JLCS evidence, strength〕 Japan Lung Cancer Society guideline 2025 (web edition), part 2 pleural mesothelioma:
#       diagnosis CQ1–14, treatment CQ1–15; strength 1 strong or 2 weak, evidence A to D (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Independent review 2026-11-10; accepted items applied

band: Diagnosis and staging
  ① Thoracoscopic biopsy when treatment is planned 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔STS 1.1〕
  ・Surgical pleural biopsy under general anaesthesia, with adequate tissue 〔JLCS C, 1〕
  ・As few incisions as possible, placed in line with any later thoracotomy 〔STS 1.2〕
  ・Biopsy even when pleural fluid cytology is negative 〔STS 1.4〕
  ・Core needle biopsy when surgical biopsy is not possible 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・CT-guided needle biopsy for a mass when surgical biopsy is not suitable 〔JLCS C, 1〕
  ・Blood markers and effusion markers, including hyaluronic acid, do not establish the diagnosis 〔JLCS D, 1〕
  ② Histological subtype reported: epithelioid, sarcomatoid or biphasic 〔ESMO IV, A〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕
  ・The sarcomatoid percentage in biphasic tumours 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・BAP1 and MTAP immunohistochemistry when the diagnosis is uncertain, including suspected mesothelioma in situ 〔ESMO IV, A〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕
  ・BAP1 and MTAP immunohistochemistry with CDKN2A FISH to separate epithelioid mesothelioma from reactive proliferation 〔JLCS B, 1〕
  ・BAP1 and MTAP immunohistochemistry on effusion cytology 〔JLCS B, 1〕
  ・An immunohistochemistry panel of at least two positive and two negative markers to separate epithelioid mesothelioma from lung adenocarcinoma 〔JLCS B, 1〕
  ・Grade reported for epithelioid tumours 〔JLCS C, 2〕
  ③ Contrast CT of chest and upper abdomen 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔JLCS C, 1〕
  ・PET-CT for staging 〔JLCS C, 1〕, in every patient 〔STS 2.1〕
  ・MRI is not added to CT 〔JLCS D, 1〕
  ・PET-CT is required when surgery is considered 〔ESMO III, B〕
  ・PET-CT at staging, optional when surgery is not considered 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ④ EBUS or mediastinoscopy when imaging suggests nodal disease, laparoscopy when abdominal disease is suspected 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔STS 2.2, 11.1〕
  ⑤ Response by modified RECIST 1.1 for mesothelioma 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ⑥ PD-L1 and tumour mutational burden do not select patients for immunotherapy 〔ESMO I, D〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕
  ・MSI also does not select patients 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕
  ⑦ Germline testing for every patient 〔ASCO High, Strong〕
  ・Germline testing as an option 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ・With pretest genetic counselling 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
  ・Germline testing is not indicated for a BAP1-deficient tumour without a family history suggesting BAP1 syndrome 〔ESMO V, D〕
  ※ Tumour genomic testing
  ・Not routine 〔ESMO III, D〕 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕
  ・A multigene panel, including NTRK and ALK 〔NCCN 2A〕

band: Surgery
  ① Surgery is not recommended on anatomic resectability alone 〔ASCO High, Strong〕
  ・Only for highly selected patients with clinical T1–3 N0 epithelioid disease 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・Clinical stage I–IIIA when macroscopic complete resection is expected 〔JLCS C, 1〕
  ・Surgery for biphasic disease 〔JLCS C, 2〕
  ・Within multimodality treatment after MDT review in an expert centre 〔ESMO III, C〕 〔STS 3.1, 4.1〕
  ・Only for cT1–2 N0 epithelioid disease 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ② Extended pleurectomy decortication rather than extrapleural pneumonectomy 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ・Pleurectomy decortication or extended pleurectomy decortication 〔STS 7.1〕
  ・Pleurectomy decortication 〔JLCS C, 1〕, or extrapleural pneumonectomy 〔JLCS C, 2〕
  ・Complete macroscopic resection as the aim 〔STS 9.1〕
  ・Partial debulking by any approach is not recommended 〔STS 9.3〕
  ・Surgery is not recommended for sarcomatoid disease 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔JLCS C, 2〕
  ③ Platinum and pemetrexed before or after surgery, 4 to 6 cycles 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔STS 5〕
  ・Cisplatin and pemetrexed before or after surgery 〔JLCS C, 1〕
  ・Surgery alone is insufficient 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・Adjuvant treatment after upfront surgery 〔STS 6〕
  ④ Lymph node dissection at surgery, including hilar, mediastinal, internal mammary, diaphragmatic, pericardial and intercostal stations 〔STS 8.1, 8.2〕
    ~ EORTC 1205 [t-eortc-1205]
  ※ Pleurectomy decortication or extended pleurectomy decortication added to chemotherapy shortened survival and increased serious adverse events
    - MARS 2
  ※ ESMO 2022 predates MARS 2
  ・Contraindications to surgery: sarcomatoid histology, contralateral or supraclavicular nodes and extrathoracic disease, without a stage limit 〔ESMO 2022〕
  ・No evidence that surgery added to systemic therapy improves survival 〔NCCN 1.2027〕
  ※ Surgery remains recommended after review of MARS and MARS 2 〔JLCS 2025〕
  ・Surgeons, physicians and radiation oncologists experienced in mesothelioma are required 〔JLCS〕
  ※ Lung-sparing surgery for selected patients 〔STS 2026〕
  ・The MARS 2 result is attributed to CT-only staging and high 90-day mortality 〔STS〕
  ・Surgery for confirmed ipsilateral N1 disease 〔STS 11.2〕, after EBUS or mediastinoscopy when imaging suggests N1 〔STS 11.1〕
  ・Surgery is not recommended for contralateral or supraclavicular N2 disease 〔STS 11.3〕
  ・NCCN 2.2026 text on surgery in N1 and N2 disease, cited by STS, was removed in NCCN 1.2027
  ※ Extrapleural pneumonectomy within trimodal therapy showed no benefit and possible harm
  ・An option in highly selected patients in expert centres 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・Only when pleurectomy decortication cannot clear the tumour and prognostic factors are favourable 〔STS 7.2〕
    - MARS

band: Treatment around surgery
  ※ Adjuvant hemithoracic radiotherapy is not standard
  ・An option in expert centres 〔ESMO II, D〕
  ・An option after extrapleural pneumonectomy, preferably in expert centres 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・Hemithoracic radiotherapy after extrapleural pneumonectomy 〔JLCS D, 2〕
  ・3D conformal radiotherapy or IMRT 〔JLCS C, 2〕

  ※ After pleurectomy decortication or lung-sparing surgery: hemithoracic IMRT 〔ASCO Moderate, Conditional〕, in experienced centres 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ・Radiotherapy is not recommended after pleurectomy decortication or without surgery 〔JLCS D, 2〕
  ・Radiotherapy is not recommended before lung-sparing surgery 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
    + Trovo 2021 [t-cro-2012-38]
  ※ Neoadjuvant immunotherapy is an option 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕
  ・Role unknown 〔ESMO 2022〕
  ・Nivolumab and ipilimumab before surgery 〔NCCN〕
  ・Evidence for adjuvant immunotherapy is insufficient 〔ASCO Very Low, Conditional〕
  ※ Intraoperative regional treatment (chemotherapy, povidone-iodine lavage or photodynamic therapy) is an option 〔STS 12.1〕
  ・No randomised comparison
  ・Benefit unproven 〔ASCO Low, Conditional〕

band: Effusion and radiotherapy
  ① Recurrent effusion: talc poudrage at thoracoscopy, preferred over VATS partial pleurectomy 〔ESMO I, A〕
  ・An indwelling pleural catheter is an alternative 〔ESMO I, B〕
  ・Indwelling catheters are not recommended in surgical candidates, because of tract seeding 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
  ・Pleurodesis after drainage to control the effusion 〔JLCS C, 2〕
  ・Drainage when disease may be operable, drainage or pleurodesis when it is not 〔NCCN 2A〕
    - MesoVATS [t-mesovats]
  ② Palliative radiotherapy to painful or symptomatic sites 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ③ Prophylactic radiotherapy to procedure tracts is not recommended 〔ESMO I, D〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    - SMART [t-smart]
    - PIT [t-pit]
  ※ Recurrence after surgery
  ・Resection of an isolated recurrence 〔STS 13.1〕 〔JLCS D, 2〕, or SBRT to an isolated pleural recurrence 〔STS 13.2〕
  ・Conventionally fractionated or mildly hypofractionated radiotherapy for diffuse or bulky recurrence in selected patients 〔STS 13.3〕

band: Peritoneal mesothelioma
  ① Selected patients with completely resectable disease, fit for surgery: cytoreductive surgery with HIPEC in an expert centre 〔PSOGI Very Low, Strong〕
  ・For epithelioid disease without high-risk features 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ② Unresectable disease or unfit for surgery: systemic treatment largely as for pleural disease 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ・Every regimen is category 2A
  ・Nivolumab and ipilimumab is preferred
  ・The EU and US immunotherapy licences cover pleural disease only
  ※ Biphasic or sarcomatoid histology, nodal disease or PCI over 17: systemic treatment first, then reassessment for cytoreduction 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ※ After progression, without a checkpoint inhibitor before: atezolizumab with bevacizumab 〔NCCN 2A, other recommended〕
  ※ No randomised trial has tested cytoreductive surgery with HIPEC
  ・The evidence is from cohort studies
  ※ CheckMate 743 and KEYNOTE-483 enrolled pleural disease only
  ・CONFIRM included peritoneal disease
