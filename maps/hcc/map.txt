#   board-maps/hcc/guideline-read-notes.md and notes-*.md, hcc-rows.json and plan.md only. Licence facts checked

# How to read and edit this file
#   Indent 2 spaces per level.
#   tree:   a decision tree; each plain line is a node, its children are indented under it
#   course: a disease course instead of a tree; each line at 2 spaces is a step, everything indented
#           under it is its content, and "next: Step | label" draws a line to a later step;
#           "join: Step | label" draws the same line but keeps the two steps in separate branches,
#           and several join: lines to one step with one label share an upright and that label
#   band:   a block above the tree for the whole sheet
#   panel:  a block beside the tree, joined by a brace to the node named after ">"
#   + Trial         a trial line, the trial's name only; it draws as a chip, and a tap opens its card with the
#                   results. No figures on maps (Jonan 2026-11-11). The mark records the result and is not drawn:
#                   + OS benefit, ~ surrogate only (PFS, response, single-arm), - no benefit or harm, * no comparison
#   Trial {pmid N}  the usual form: links to the Evidence row that cites the PMID (on this map's sheet if filed on two),
#                   so filing and publishing link it with no map edit; before that, PubMed link, dashed chip, listed for intake
#   Trial [t-...]   picks the Evidence row by its anchor: only where the row has no PMID or the PMID is on two rows of this sheet
#   Trial {url https://...}  a randomised trial with no PubMed record yet (congress abstract): link, listed for intake
#   trial lines in a row draw as one row of chips, starting at the text of the line above
#   ※ text          caveat             ° text          side effects, grey
#   ・ text          one statement under the line above (a guideline's position, a licence, a condition);
#                   one statement per line, no chains of clauses joined by semicolons (Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   ① text          a numbered option or item, in order of guideline preference, in every step, node,
#                   band and panel. A single option is ① too (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   | a | b |       a row of a ruled grid; the first row is the header, - leaves a cell empty
#   off:            sheet rows left off on purpose: Trial [t-...] | reason
#   source: ESMO {url https://...}   a guideline, held as a link; 〔ESMO I, A〕 at the end of a statement marks it as
#                   resting on that guideline, with its printed level of evidence and grade, and opens the link.
#                   Guidelines are not Evidence rows (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   scale: Low | Intermediate | High-intermediate | High   four risk groups of an adjuvant or local-treatment
#                   decision, lowest first, each naming a grid row; those rows, and the ① and ※ lines in
#                   the same block that open with a risk group, take that group's colour
#   branch: Key | legend | hue   at the top: one fill colour for a branch of the map. Hues: blue, violet, sage,
#                   rose, sand, teal, apricot, olive (at most eight; without a hue the next unused one is taken).
#                   "branch: Key" under a step or tree node gives it that fill, "branch: Key pale" the pale tint
#                   (later steps of the same branch); a node's children take it too; a step for every branch stays
#                   plain grey; a panel takes the pale tint of the node it is joined to. Every map has branches
#                   (Jonan 2026-11-11, "more colours and variations"). Never on a map with scale: (2026-11-11)
#
# Rules (Jonan 2026-11-08; node maps/check.cjs enforces the starred ones)
#   Layout fits the disease: a tree where care branches on tests, a course where one state follows another
#   Whiteboard look (Jonan 2026-11-11, early colon map as the reference): filled rounded blocks, no title block,
#   bold numbered options, trial names as chips with no figures, lines with rounded corners and no arrowheads
#   that share one trunk where they fan out; spec doc https://claude.ai/artifact/Gt1iJC47Qa9LSMGDteysBJ
#   No result colours (the marks above are kept as data, not drawn); colour means one thing on a box: a box
#   carrying the risk ramp (scale:, a grid or a line opening with a risk group) stays grey, branch: colours go
#   on the other boxes (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Size and shape (Jonan 2026-11-11, "optimal box height/width ratio ... on a typical pc screen"): text runs in
#   columns of one reading width (460 px) balanced by the browser; a box takes another column only when every
#   column gets text and the columns come out near even; the columns per box are chosen so the whole map shows
#   at the largest scale in the 1600 x 850 map area of a 1920 x 1080 screen (most maps come out near 0.55
#   height to width); grid columns 220 px; bands share one row (canvas.js)
#   Full screen button beside Fit (evidence-maps.js); lines meet boxes at their middle and boxes centre on
#   each other; rounded corners; chips centred on their line (render-check.cjs)
# * Every sheet row is on the map or under off: with a reason
# * Trial lines cite clinical trials, pooled analyses of trials or meta-analyses; no cohort, review,
#   consensus, PK, animal or healthy-volunteer papers (the statement can stay as plain text)
#   Superseded trials go under off: unless an option still in use rests on them
#   Standard options only; trials of options not in standard use go under off: (Jonan 2026-11-09, Lung-MAP)
#   Which trials go in the database and on a map: maps/inclusion.txt (Jonan 2026-11-10); trials screened
#   and left out are listed in the map folder's excluded.txt
#   Adjuvant, maintenance and consolidation choices are a later step after the primary treatment,
#   never a branch beside it (Jonan 2026-11-09)
#   Columns follow the decisions in order: surgery or no surgery; treatment before surgery or
#   upfront resection; treatment after the primary treatment (Jonan 2026-11-09)
# * Plain clinical wording: no arrows, no metaphors such as "backbone"
#   Full words in prose: "chemotherapy", "with or without"; plain sentences with a subject and verb (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Language rule (Jonan 2026-11-13, every map): simple clinical statements with the backing guidelines as tags,
#     never "PAGA suggests", "ESMO uses" or "JGCA lists". One narrative: a point agreed by several guidelines is one
#     statement carrying all their tags, never repeated per guideline. A ・ line explains a difference only where
#     guidelines disagree (a cut-off, a line of treatment, a licence, a grade set before a trial)
#   Register (Jonan 2026-11-13): the impersonal register of clinical papers and abstracts. No second person, no
#     imperatives ("Test HER2" reads "HER2 testing before first-line treatment"), no conversational phrasing
#     ("case by case", "is not done", "adds little")
#   No explanation where no reason is known: two facts stay two lines, never joined by "as" or "because"
#     unless a guideline or trial gives that reason (Jonan 2026-11-13)
#   Side-effect lines open with "Toxicity of note:" (Jonan 2026-11-13)
#   No omission lines (Jonan 2026-11-13): a ・ line never says that a guideline does not list, cover or mention an
#     option ("Not in NCCN"). It gives a different recommendation, cut-off or grade, never an absence
#   No meta lines (Jonan 2026-11-13): no comment on our own reading ("no guideline read cites this analysis"). The
#     option is stated plainly ("can be considered")
#   node maps/prose-check.cjs fails omission and meta lines, a guideline as the subject of a sentence, semicolons
#     and second person, and lists for review a ・ line that repeats its parent or a sibling. A draft is shown only
#     after it passes and a line-by-line read against these rules (2026-11-14)
#   A bare 〔ESMO〕 tag names the guideline on a sub-line whose grade sits on the line above, or on an ungraded
#     statement. An optional recommendation keeps its strength in words: "is an option" (2026-11-13)
#   PD-L1 and similar cut-offs: lead with the band every guideline agrees on, then one line per band where they
#     split (Jonan 2026-11-13)
# * Labels on connecting lines start with a capital, like every other label
#   A statement that rests on a guideline rather than a trial carries its 〔source level, grade〕 tag;
#   where a trial and the guideline disagree, or the trial came later, a ※ line says so
#   Rule ⑦ (Jonan 2026-11-10): an option one guideline recommends, with no EU or US licence and no NCCN
#     listing, is not a map option; options NCCN alone lists sit on ※ lines or as ・ lines in their drug class;
#     the tag names the guideline, so the text does not repeat it (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   East Asian guidelines come first in Jonan's practice (2026-11-10): a JLCS option licensed in Japan and marketed
#     internationally is a numbered option with its tag and a licence note, never ※-only or under off: (S-1).
#     A drug licensed in China only, with no international marketing, stays on a ※ line (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Presentation (Jonan 2026-11-11). node maps/render-check.cjs checks the drawn map and must report 0 faults
#     on every draft and release, with node maps/check.cjs
#     Square lines with no arrowheads. Every line label is centred on the line as drawn. No line runs under
#       a box or crosses another line
#     Steps are named, never numbered. Text never points to a step or an option by its number
#     No restated lines. Guidelines that agree share one line with all their tags. A ・ line is only for a
#       real disagreement
#     No value given once per guideline and joined by "or" when the guidelines agree in substance: "4 to 6 months
#       〔ASCO〕 〔NCCN〕", never "at least 4 and ideally 6 months 〔ASCO〕, or 4 to 6 months 〔NCCN〕". Lists that
#       complement each other merge into one list with all the tags. Keep a split only for a different method,
#       a clinically different cut-off or a different grade for the same thing (Jonan 2026-11-15)
#     Options for the same population or condition are one numbered line, even when their guideline support
#       differs: each regimen carries its own tags in the line ("Good risk: three cycles of BEP 〔…〕, or four
#       cycles of EP 〔…〕"). A new numbered line needs a new population or condition, never a new regimen alone.
#       A licence difference goes on a ・ line. Supportive-care and regimen bands do not repeat choices that a
#       step already states (Jonan 2026-11-14, germ cell map). prose-check.cjs fails two numbered lines in one
#       block that open with the same text before the colon
#     One disease state is one step. Differences within it, such as de novo and metachronous disease, go on
#       ・ lines under the shared option
#     A vague guideline term such as "selected men" is defined from the guideline's text. Where a paraphrase
#       reads unclear, the guideline's own words are used
#     A laboratory threshold gives the SI unit beside the conventional unit: 50 ng/dL (1.7 nmol/L)
#     A wrapped line starts under its text. No word is carried down to a tag's line. An untagged statement
#       keeps no single word alone on its last line. Each trial name has a ・ bullet and never wraps
#   Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded. Tag order on every
#   line: PAGA, JSH, KLCA, ESMO, NCCN, EASL, AASLD (East Asian guidelines first, Jonan 2026-11-17)
#     〔PAGA level, grade〕 Pan-Asian adapted ESMO guideline for HCC 2026 (Tan et al. ESMO Open 2026;11:107771),
#       Table 1 rows 1a to 8e. A bare 〔PAGA〕 marks the narrative or a regional note. Figures 1 and 2 unread
#     〔JSH evidence, strength〕 Japan Society of Hepatology HCC guideline. A graded tag is the 2021 fifth edition
#       (English text) or its 2023 web revision of CQ39, used only where the 2025 sixth edition keeps the content.
#       A bare 〔JSH〕 marks a 2025 sentence whose strength and evidence grade are unknown (main text unread), a 2021
#       recommendation whose 2025 counterpart is unread, or the 2021 explanation text
#     〔KLCA level, strength〕 2026 KLCA-NCC Korea practice guidelines (Clin Mol Hepatol 2026;32:e2026.0943),
#       Oxford levels 1 to 5, strong or weak. A bare 〔KLCA〕 marks ungraded narrative. Tables and the
#       treatment overview figure unread, so no modified UICC definition is on the map
#     〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO HCC guideline 2025 (Vogel et al. Ann Oncol 2025;36:491-506). A bare 〔ESMO〕
#       marks the narrative
#     〔NCCN category〕 NCCN Hepatocellular Carcinoma 2.2026 (14 August 2026). Category 2A unless stated, with
#       "preferred" where printed. A bare 〔NCCN〕 marks a footnote or the Discussion
#     〔EASL level, strength〕 EASL HCC guideline 2025 (J Hepatol 2025;82:315-374), Oxford levels. A bare 〔EASL〕
#       marks the narrative
#     〔AASLD level, strength〕 AASLD Practice Guidance 2023 (Singal et al. Hepatology 2023;78:1922-1965) with the
#       2025 Critical Update (revised statement 32). READING GAP: statements 38 onward are unread, so every AASLD
#       point on ablation, TACE, radioembolisation, radiotherapy, systemic therapy and Child-Pugh B is a bare
#       〔AASLD〕 from the narrative and carries no grade yet
#     A bare tag on a ・ line names a guideline whose grade sits on the numbered line above
#     BCLC 2022 (Reig et al. J Hepatol 2022;76:681-693) is the staging frame, named in text and never tagged
#   Licence lines carry no tag when they state a regulator's current decision checked on 2026-11-17

band: Staging and liver function
  ① Staging: the BCLC 2022 system is used for prognosis and treatment allocation 〔PAGA IV, A〕 〔ESMO IV, A〕 〔EASL 1, strong〕 〔AASLD 5, strong〕
    ・Other validated systems may be used in Asia 〔PAGA IV, A〕, with an algorithm by liver function, extrahepatic spread, vascular invasion, tumour number and tumour size in Japan 〔JSH〕 and modified UICC staging as the primary staging system in Korea 〔KLCA 3, strong〕
  | BCLC stage | Tumour | Liver function and performance status |
  | 0, very early | Single, 2 cm or less | Preserved, performance status 0 |
  | A, early | Single of any size, or up to 3 nodules of 3 cm or less | Preserved, performance status 0 |
  | B, intermediate | Multifocal beyond stage A, no vascular invasion or extrahepatic spread | Preserved, performance status 0 |
  | C, advanced | Vascular invasion or extrahepatic spread | Preserved, performance status 0 to 2 |
  | D, end stage | Any | Performance status above 2, or impaired liver function without a transplant option |
  ② Liver function: Child-Pugh class or ALBI grade is assessed 〔PAGA III, A〕 〔ESMO III, A〕, and MELD-Na sets transplant priority 〔PAGA IV, A〕 〔ESMO IV, A〕
    ・ALBI grade 1 is a score of −2.60 or less, grade 2 above −2.60 to −1.39, and grade 3 above −1.39 〔NCCN〕
    ・Before resection, indocyanine green retention at 15 minutes is weighed with laboratory tests against the planned resection volume 〔JSH〕 〔EASL〕, and a retention of 14% or less allows major hepatectomy 〔KLCA〕
    ・The minimum future liver remnant in cirrhosis is 40% 〔KLCA〕 〔AASLD〕, or 30 to 40% in Child-Pugh A 〔NCCN〕, and without cirrhosis 20% 〔NCCN〕 or 30% 〔AASLD〕
  ③ Portal hypertension: assessment follows the Baveno VII criteria 〔PAGA III, A〕 〔ESMO III, A〕
    ・Non-invasive assessment comes first, with invasive measurement reserved for inconclusive results or for a value that alters management 〔PAGA III, A〕
    ・Clinically significant portal hypertension is a hepatic venous pressure gradient of 10 mmHg or more 〔KLCA〕 〔AASLD〕 or above 10 mmHg 〔ESMO〕 〔EASL〕
    ・Indirect signs are varices or splenomegaly with a platelet count below 100 × 10⁹/L 〔KLCA〕 〔ESMO〕, and ascites 〔KLCA〕
  ④ Histology before systemic therapy for advanced disease: confirmation is recommended 〔PAGA III, A〕 〔ESMO III, A〕, and biopsy, the preferred method, is considered when no earlier histology exists 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Imaging-based diagnosis is the practice in Japan 〔PAGA〕, and non-invasive imaging criteria establish the diagnosis in cirrhosis, chronic hepatitis B or after an earlier hepatocellular carcinoma 〔EASL 1, strong〕
  ⑤ Tumour board: multidisciplinary management is strongly recommended in early and intermediate stage disease 〔PAGA II, A〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 and is part of the initial work-up 〔NCCN〕 〔AASLD 3, strong〕
  ⑥ Viral hepatitis: antiviral therapy is recommended for every patient with active hepatitis B 〔PAGA II, A〕 〔ESMO II, A〕, and direct-acting antivirals for active hepatitis C are timed individually 〔PAGA IV, B〕 〔ESMO IV, B〕
    ・Treatment of hepatitis B extends below the usual HBV DNA threshold of 2000 IU/mL 〔PAGA〕, while antiviral therapy follows the standard hepatitis B and C treatment criteria 〔AASLD 2, strong〕
    ・HBsAg is tested before any treatment 〔KLCA 2, strong〕, and HBsAg-positive patients receive entecavir or tenofovir during TACE, chemotherapy or resection 〔KLCA 2, strong〕, during tyrosine kinase inhibitor, immune checkpoint inhibitor, radiotherapy or hepatic arterial infusion treatment 〔KLCA 3, strong〕, and during radiofrequency ablation 〔KLCA 3, weak〕

band: Regimens and doses
  ① Atezolizumab with bevacizumab: atezolizumab 1200 mg and bevacizumab 15 mg/kg are given intravenously every 3 weeks 〔KLCA〕
  ② STRIDE: one dose of tremelimumab 300 mg with durvalumab 1500 mg is followed by durvalumab 1500 mg every 4 weeks 〔KLCA〕 〔ESMO〕, and durvalumab alone is given at 1500 mg every 4 weeks in HIMALAYA
  ③ Nivolumab with ipilimumab: nivolumab 1 mg/kg with ipilimumab 3 mg/kg every 3 weeks for 4 doses is followed by nivolumab 480 mg every 4 weeks 〔KLCA〕 for at most 2 years 〔EASL〕
    ・After sorafenib, the same 4 doses are followed by nivolumab 240 mg every 2 weeks 〔KLCA〕 〔NCCN〕
  ④ Tyrosine kinase inhibitors: lenvatinib is given at 12 mg once daily at a body weight of 60 kg or more and 8 mg once daily below 60 kg, and sorafenib at 400 mg twice daily 〔KLCA〕
  ⑤ Other agents: regorafenib is given at 160 mg once daily for the first 3 weeks of each 4-week cycle, cabozantinib at 60 mg once daily, ramucirumab at 8 mg/kg intravenously every 2 weeks and pembrolizumab at 200 mg intravenously every 3 weeks for up to 35 cycles
  ⑥ With TACE: durvalumab 1500 mg every 4 weeks starts 7 days after the first TACE and durvalumab 1120 mg with bevacizumab 15 mg/kg every 3 weeks starts 14 days after the last TACE in EMERALD-1, and lenvatinib at the weight-based dose with pembrolizumab 400 mg every 6 weeks is given for up to 2 years in LEAP-012
  ⑦ Radioembolisation with yttrium-90: a tumour dose of 205 Gy or more may be associated with longer survival, and a dose above 400 Gy to 25% or less of the liver is recommended in Child-Pugh A 〔NCCN〕
  ⑧ External beam radiotherapy: SBRT delivers 27.5 to 60 Gy in 3 to 5 fractions, and hepatic pain from a high tumour burden is treated with 8 Gy in 1 fraction 〔NCCN〕

course: Hepatocellular carcinoma | parallel
  Very early and early stage: first treatment
    branch: EARLY
    ① Single tumour of 2 cm or less: thermal ablation 〔PAGA II, A〕 〔JSH A, strong〕 〔KLCA 2, strong〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔EASL 2, strong〕 〔AASLD〕, or resection 〔JSH A, strong〕 〔KLCA 2, strong〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔EASL 2, strong〕
      ・Ablation and resection are equal choices, decided by liver function, tumour location and the risk of recurrence 〔KLCA〕 〔EASL〕, and ablation is preferred when resection would need a major hepatectomy 〔EASL〕
      ・In transplant candidates, an ablate-and-wait strategy can be recommended 〔EASL 2, strong〕
      - SURF {pmid 35949295}
    ② Single tumour larger than 2 cm, with preserved liver function, no portal hypertension and a sufficient liver remnant: resection 〔PAGA II, A〕 〔JSH A, strong〕 〔KLCA 2, strong〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔EASL 2, strong〕 〔AASLD 2, strong〕
      ・Ablation is an alternative to resection in BCLC stage A 〔PAGA II, A〕 〔ESMO II, A〕, and the choice between them considers technical limitations and is made by the multidisciplinary team 〔PAGA III, A〕 〔ESMO III, A〕
      ・Up to 3 cm, ablation and resection are equal choices 〔JSH A, strong〕, while from 2 to 3 cm radiofrequency ablation is preferred only when resection is difficult 〔KLCA 2, strong〕
    ③ 2 or 3 tumours of 3 cm or less: resection 〔JSH A, strong〕 〔KLCA 3, weak〕 〔EASL 2, weak〕, or ablation 〔PAGA II, B〕 〔JSH A, strong〕 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔EASL 2, weak〕
      ・Resection requires preserved liver function 〔KLCA〕, and ablation is reserved for patients unsuitable for surgery 〔PAGA〕 〔ESMO〕
    ④ Single tumour of 3 to 5 cm, not suitable for surgery: ablation combined with TACE 〔PAGA II, B〕 〔JSH〕 〔KLCA〕, or ablation alone 〔JSH〕 〔ESMO II, B〕
      ・Local control after ablation declines from 3 cm, and ablation is not recommended above 5 cm 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ⑤ Within the Milan criteria: liver transplantation 〔PAGA II, A〕 〔KLCA 2, strong〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔EASL 2, strong〕 〔AASLD 2, strong〕
      ・Transplantation is the first choice when resection is unsuitable because of tumour location, liver dysfunction or clinically significant portal hypertension 〔KLCA〕 〔EASL〕 〔AASLD〕, and resection and transplantation are both preferred when both sets of criteria are met 〔NCCN〕
      ・In Japan, transplantation is limited to Child-Pugh B and C 〔PAGA〕 and is recommended within the Milan criteria or the 5-5-500 rule, of up to 5 tumours of 5 cm or less with AFP of 500 ng/mL (500 µg/L) or less, in Child-Pugh C 〔JSH B, strong〕 and Child-Pugh B 〔JSH〕
    ⑥ Resection and ablation not suitable: SBRT or proton therapy 〔PAGA II, B〕 〔JSH B, weak〕 〔KLCA 3, strong〕 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔EASL 4, weak〕, high-dose-rate brachytherapy 〔PAGA II, B〕 〔ESMO II, B〕, or radiation segmentectomy with yttrium-90 for a single tumour of 8 cm or less 〔PAGA III, B〕 〔KLCA 3, weak〕 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔EASL 3, weak〕
      ・Radiotherapy is an option for a single tumour of 3 cm or less when resection is not feasible 〔KLCA 3, weak〕, an alternative to ablation 〔NCCN〕, and an option when a size above 3 cm or the tumour location gives a high risk of recurrence after ablation 〔EASL〕
      ・Radiotherapy applies to 1 to 3 tumours of 5 cm or less in Child-Pugh A to B7 〔JSH〕 or to 1 to 5 tumours 〔NCCN〕, and is avoided at a Child-Pugh score of 8 or more 〔AASLD〕
      ・Radioembolisation is not approved in Japan 〔PAGA〕
    ⑦ Child-Pugh B or portal hypertension: limited resection can be considered in well-selected patients 〔PAGA III, B〕 〔KLCA 3, weak〕 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔EASL 3, weak〕
      ・Resection is limited to a single tumour in Child-Pugh A or B7 with mild portal hypertension 〔KLCA〕, to highly selected patients with Child-Pugh B and no portal hypertension 〔NCCN 2A〕, and to 2 segments or fewer 〔EASL 3, strong〕
    ※ Advanced age alone does not exclude resection 〔JSH B, strong〕 〔KLCA 2, strong〕 〔EASL 3, strong〕
    ※ Neoadjuvant systemic therapy is not recommended outside clinical trials 〔JSH〕 〔EASL 3, strong〕 〔AASLD 1, strong〕
