PCWG4 (2026):
APMN, APMS, APMR  ≡  androgen pathway modulator naive, sensitive, resistant
APMN and APMS replace HSPC; APMR replaces CRPC. Modulators include ADT and the ARSIs. PCWG3 (2016) used castration-sensitive and castration-resistant

Course and prognosis

De novo disease has a poorer prognosis than metastases after radical treatment
On ADT alone, median OS 92 months for low volume relapsed disease and 43 months for high volume de novo disease (Francini 2018)
In current practice the ARSI, and docetaxel when used, start together with ADT
The triplet trials compared ADT with or without docetaxel against a triplet, not ADT plus an ARSI with or without docetaxel
Median OS, rough gauge only: CHAARTED 57.6 months, LATITUDE 53.3 months; first-line CRPC in TALAPRO-2 45.8 months

ARSI options

  1. ①Abiraterone with prednisone (LATITUDE, STAMPEDE arm G) raised BP, low potassium, oedema, cardiovascular risk
    Grade 3–4 hypertension 21% vs 10%. Check liver tests often in the first 12 weeks (COU-AA-301). Prednisone 5 mg once daily in HSPC, twice daily in CRPC
  2. ②Enzalutamide (ENZAMET, ARCHES)
  3. ③Apalutamide (TITAN) rash 24%, hypothyroidism 8%, fracture 12% in SPARTAN
  4. ④Darolutamide (ARANOTE; ARASENS with docetaxel) low brain penetration, fatigue 5.6%
    Fewer drug interactions, except rosuvastatin: exposure rises 5.2-fold through BCRP and OATP inhibition (Zurth 2019). Rosuvastatin at most 5 mg once daily
    Enzalutamide and apalutamide are strong CYP3A4 inducers: midazolam exposure fell 86% and 92% (Gibbons 2015, Duran 2020). Expect lower levels of simvastatin, atorvastatin and warfarin
    Abiraterone inhibits CYP2D6: dextromethorphan exposure doubled (Chi 2012)
    Brain to blood ratio for darolutamide 0.079 in rats (Taavitsainen 2020); no change in cerebral blood flow in volunteers, unlike enzalutamide (Williams 2023)

ADT, testing and bone

Flutamide for 2 weeks before the first LHRH agonist injection prevents PSA flare (Noguchi 2001); clinical harm from flare is poorly evidenced except with extensive spinal disease (Krakowsky 2017)
Relugolix: major cardiovascular events 2.9% vs 6.2% against leuprolide (HERO)
Intermittent ADT: non-inferior after radiotherapy relapse (PR.7, HR 1.02); not shown in metastatic disease (SWOG 9346, HR 1.10)
Germline panel and tumour testing in all metastatic disease; BRCA2, BRCA1 and mismatch repair genes first (Philadelphia 2019)
Bone loss on ADT: denosumab 60 mg every 6 months, vertebral fractures 1.5% vs 3.9% (Smith 2009). Bone metastases in CRPC: denosumab 120 mg every 4 weeks beat zoledronic acid for first skeletal event, HR 0.82 (Fizazi 2011)
