# oncOS board map: Advanced Prostate
#
# How to read and edit this file
#   Indent 2 spaces per level.
#   tree:   the decision tree; each plain line is a node, its children are indented under it
#   band:   a block above the tree for the whole sheet
#   panel:  a block beside the tree, joined by a brace to the node named after ">"
#   = text          the treatment for a branch
#   + Trial | fig   a trial line; the mark records the result and is not drawn:
#                   + OS benefit, ~ surrogate only (PFS, response, single-arm), - no benefit or harm, * no comparison
#   fig with no digits is read from the Evidence row at each publish:
#     OS HR, PFS HR   the HR on the row's OS or PFS line
#     OS median       the medians on that line, "a vs b months"
#     response        the first percentage on the ORR line
#   fig with digits is shown as written; the map check fails if the row no longer has those numbers
#   Trial [t-...]   picks the Evidence row by its anchor when the name is not enough
#   Trial {pmid N}  a trial with no Evidence row yet: PubMed link, listed for intake
#   ※ text          caveat             ° text          side effects, grey
#   ① text          numbered item; plain lines in a band or panel are text
#   off:            sheet rows left off on purpose: Trial [t-...] | reason

map: Advanced Prostate

band: States and volume
  PCWG4 (2026): androgen pathway modulator naive, sensitive or resistant (APMN, APMS, APMR) replace castration-sensitive and castration-resistant (PCWG3, 2016)
    * PCWG4 {pmid 41744290} | consensus
    * PCWG3 {pmid 26903579} | consensus
  CHAARTED high volume: 4 or more bone metastases with at least 1 beyond the spine and pelvis, or visceral metastases
  LATITUDE high risk: at least 2 of Gleason 8 or more, 3 or more bone lesions, visceral metastases
  ※ Both defined on CT and bone scan; PSMA PET finds more lesions and was not used for volume in these trials
  De novo disease does worse than relapse after radical treatment
    * Francini 2018 {pmid 29707790} | OS on ADT alone 92 months relapse, low volume vs 43 de novo, high volume

band: ADT, testing and bone
  ① Flutamide for 2 weeks before the first LHRH agonist injection prevents PSA flare
    * Noguchi 2001 {pmid 11706524} | flare prevented
    ※ Clinical harm from flare is poorly evidenced except with extensive spinal disease
      * Krakowsky 2017 {pmid 28753828} | review
  ② Relugolix: fewer major cardiovascular events than leuprolide
    ~ HERO {pmid 32469183} | 2.9% vs 6.2%
  ③ Intermittent ADT: non-inferior after radiotherapy relapse, not shown in metastatic disease
    + PR.7 {pmid 22931259} | OS HR 1.02
    - SWOG 9346 {pmid 23550669} | OS HR 1.10
  ④ Germline panel and tumour testing in all metastatic disease; BRCA2, BRCA1 and mismatch repair genes first
    * Philadelphia 2019 {pmid 32516092} | consensus
  ⑤ Bone loss on ADT: denosumab 60 mg every 6 months
    ~ HALT {pmid 19671656} | vertebral fractures 1.5% vs 3.9%
  ⑥ Bone metastases in castration-resistant disease: denosumab 120 mg every 4 weeks
    ~ Fizazi 2011 {pmid 21353695} | first skeletal event HR 0.82

course: Advanced prostate cancer
  Rising PSA after surgery or radiotherapy, no metastases on CT or bone scan
    next: Metastatic relapse, castration-sensitive | metastases appear
    next: Castration-resistant, no metastases on CT or bone scan | PSA rises on ADT
    = PSA doubling 9 months or less: enzalutamide + leuprolide
    + EMBARK | OS HR
    ※ Enzalutamide alone delayed metastases but did not lengthen OS

  Metastatic relapse, castration-sensitive
    next: Metastatic, castration-resistant
    = ADT + ARSI, any volume
    ※ 1 to 3 metastases: stereotactic radiotherapy to every site delays progression; no OS endpoint
      ~ ORIOLE {pmid 32215577} | PFS HR 0.30
      ~ STOMP {pmid 29240541} | ADT-free survival 21 vs 13 months

  Castration-resistant, no metastases on CT or bone scan
    next: Metastatic, castration-resistant | metastases appear
    = PSA doubling 10 months or less: apalutamide, enzalutamide or darolutamide, with ADT
    + SPARTAN | OS HR
    + PROSPER | OS HR
    + ARAMIS | OS HR
    ※ Metastasis-free survival roughly doubled in each
    Metastases on this treatment: ③

  Metastatic at diagnosis, castration-sensitive
    next: Metastatic, castration-resistant
    ① Low volume: ADT + ARSI + radiotherapy to the prostate
      + STAMPEDE arm H [t-stampede--advanced-prostate-3] | low burden OS HR 0.68
      ※ Arm H gave ADT with or without docetaxel, no ARSI
      ※ With abiraterone, radiotherapy lengthened rPFS but not OS, and reduced serious urinary events
        ~ PEACE-1 radiotherapy [t-peace-1] | low volume rPFS HR 0.65
      ※ Benefit confined to fewer than 5 bone metastases
        - HORRAD {pmid 30266309} | OS HR 0.90
        + STOPCAP {pmid 30826218} | 3-year OS 7% higher
    ② High volume: ADT + ARSI + docetaxel; ADT + ARSI if unfit for docetaxel
      + PEACE-1 | OS HR
      + ARASENS | OS HR
      ° Grade 3 or higher hypertension 22% vs 13% (PEACE-1)
      ※ Both compared with ADT + docetaxel; neither with ADT + ARSI
      ※ Docetaxel with ADT alone: OS benefit in high volume, none in low volume
        + CHAARTED | OS HR
        + STAMPEDE docetaxel [t-stampede--advanced-prostate] | docetaxel OS HR 0.78

    next: ② After ADT and docetaxel

panel: ARSI options > Metastatic at diagnosis, castration-sensitive
  ① Abiraterone with prednisone 5 mg once daily
    + LATITUDE | OS HR
    + STAMPEDE arm G [t-stampede--advanced-prostate-2] | OS HR
    ° Raised blood pressure, low potassium, oedema; liver tests often in the first 12 weeks
  ② Enzalutamide
    + ENZAMET | OS HR
    + ARCHES | OS HR
  ③ Apalutamide
    + TITAN | OS HR
    ° Rash 24%, hypothyroidism 8%, fracture 12% (SPARTAN)
  ④ Darolutamide
    ~ ARANOTE | rPFS HR
    ° Fatigue 5.6%; low brain penetration
  ※ Enzalutamide and apalutamide are strong CYP3A4 inducers: lower levels of simvastatin, atorvastatin and warfarin
    * Gibbons 2015 {pmid 25929560} | enzalutamide, midazolam exposure 86% lower
    * Duran 2020 {pmid 32338345} | apalutamide, midazolam exposure 92% lower
  ※ Darolutamide raises rosuvastatin exposure; rosuvastatin at most 5 mg daily
    * Zurth 2019 {pmid 31571146} | 5.2-fold
  ※ Abiraterone inhibits CYP2D6
    * Chi 2012 {pmid 23064959} | dextromethorphan exposure doubled
  ※ Darolutamide crosses into the brain little
    * Taavitsainen 2020 {pmid 32003293} | brain to blood ratio 0.079 in rats
    * Williams 2023 {pmid 37103658} | no change in cerebral blood flow, unlike enzalutamide
