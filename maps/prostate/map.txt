# How to read and edit this file
#   Indent 2 spaces per level.
#   tree:   a decision tree; each plain line is a node, its children are indented under it
#   course: a disease course instead of a tree; each line at 2 spaces is a step, everything indented
#           under it is its content, and "next: Step | label" draws a line to a later step
#   band:   a block above the tree for the whole sheet
#   panel:  a block beside the tree, joined by a brace to the node named after ">"
#   = text          the treatment for a branch
#   + Trial | fig   a trial line; the mark records the result and is not drawn:
#                   + OS benefit, ~ surrogate only (PFS, response, single-arm), - no benefit or harm, * no comparison
#   fig with no digits is read from the Evidence row at each publish:
#     OS HR, PFS HR   the HR on the row's OS or PFS line
#     OS median       the medians on that line, "a vs b months"
#     response        the first percentage on the ORR line
#   fig with digits is shown as written; the map check fails if the row no longer has those numbers
#   Trial [t-...]   picks the Evidence row by its anchor when the name is not enough
#   Trial {pmid N}  a trial with no Evidence row yet: PubMed link, listed for intake
#   ※ text          caveat             ° text          side effects, grey
#   ・ text          one statement under the line above (a drug of a class, a licence, a condition);
#                   one statement per line, no chains of clauses joined by semicolons (Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   ① text          numbered item; plain lines in a band or panel are text
#   | a | b |       a row of a ruled grid; the first row is the header, - leaves a cell empty
#   off:            sheet rows left off on purpose: Trial [t-...] | reason
#
# Rules (Jonan 2026-11-08; node maps/check.cjs enforces the starred ones)
#   Layout fits the disease: a tree where care branches on tests, a course where one state follows another
#   Square lines only; joins line up; no result colours (the marks above are kept as data, not drawn)
# * Every sheet row is on the map or under off: with a reason
# * Trial lines cite clinical trials, pooled analyses of trials or meta-analyses; no cohort, review,
#   consensus, PK, animal or healthy-volunteer papers (the statement can stay as plain text)
#   Superseded trials go under off: unless an option still in use rests on them
#   Standard options only; trials of options not in standard use go under off: (Jonan 2026-11-09, Lung-MAP)
# * Plain clinical wording: no arrows, no metaphors such as "backbone"
# * Labels on connecting lines start with a capital, like every other label
#   Standard options only; an EU or US licensed option that a guideline does not list stays on the
#     line with its licence noted and says which guidelines leave it out
#   Option order follows the guidelines' preference; where they disagree each differing guideline gets its own ・ line
#   source: ESMO {url https://...}   a guideline held as a link; 〔ESMO I, A〕 at the end of a statement
#                   marks it as resting on that guideline, with its printed evidence and grade
#   Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#     〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Prostate Cancer 1.2027 (12 October 2026), pages PROS-12 to PROS-18A,
#       PROS-B, PROS-C and PROS-G; category 2A unless stated; options NCCN alone lists sit on ※ lines;
#       the tag names the guideline, so the text does not repeat it (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#     〔ESMO evidence, grade〕 ESMO living guideline, advanced and metastatic prostate cancer v1.0 (June 2026),
#       read on esmo.org, from the Clinical Practice Guideline (Fizazi, Ann Oncol 2026, PMID 41679466);
#       it starts at metastatic or castration-resistant disease, so rising PSA without metastases has NCCN tags only
#   Guideline set: ESMO and NCCN; JUA 2023 and PAGA 2022 not used (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#   Full words in prose: "with", "chemotherapy", "with or without" (Jonan 2026-11-11)

band: States and volume
  PCWG4 (2026) names three states: androgen pathway modulator naive, sensitive and resistant (APMN, APMS, APMR)
    ・These replace castration-sensitive and castration-resistant from PCWG3 (2016)
  CHAARTED high volume: 4 or more bone metastases with at least 1 beyond the spine and pelvis, or visceral metastases
  LATITUDE high risk: at least 2 of Gleason 8 or more, 3 or more bone lesions, visceral metastases
  ※ Both are defined on CT and bone scan
    ・PSMA PET finds more lesions and was not used for volume in these trials

band: ADT, testing and bone
  ① A first-generation antiandrogen for at least 7 days with the first LHRH agonist injection prevents testosterone flare 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・It is given when metastases are in weight-bearing bone or the prostate mass is large
    * Noguchi 2001 {pmid 11706524} | Flare prevented
  ② Relugolix kept castration in 96.7% against 88.8% with leuprolide 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Major cardiovascular events were fewer than with leuprolide, in a prespecified safety analysis
    ・EU and US licence
    ・ESMO does not name an LHRH antagonist
    ~ HERO [t-hero] | Cardiovascular events 2.9% vs 6.2%
  ③ Intermittent ADT is considered for PSA relapse after a good PSA response 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・It was non-inferior to continuous ADT for relapse after radiotherapy
    ・Non-inferiority was not shown in metastatic disease
    ・In metastatic disease it is considered only when ADT is given alone 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ~ PR.7 [t-swog-jpr7] | Non-inferior, OS HR 1.02
    - SWOG 9346 [t-hussain-et-al] | OS HR
  ④ Germline and tumour testing in all metastatic disease 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Germline testing in castration-sensitive disease 〔ESMO III, A〕
    ・Tumour testing for BRCA1, BRCA2, CDK12 and PALB2 before the first treatment for castration-resistant disease 〔ESMO III, A〕
    ・Mismatch repair testing after an ARSI 〔ESMO III, A〕
    ・PTEN IHC in castration-sensitive disease 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ⑤ Fracture risk on ADT: denosumab 60 mg every 6 months, zoledronic acid 5 mg yearly or alendronate 70 mg weekly 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Low bone density or a prior fragility fracture: a bisphosphonate or denosumab 〔ESMO III, A〕
    ~ HALT [t-halt] | Vertebral fractures 1.5% vs 3.9%
  ⑥ Bone metastases in castration-resistant disease: denosumab 120 mg every 4 weeks 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
    ・Zoledronic acid 4 mg every 12 weeks 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Monthly dosing is not given in castration-sensitive disease 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO II, E〕
    ~ Fizazi 2011 [t-fizazi-et-al] | First skeletal event HR 0.82

course: Advanced prostate cancer
  Rising PSA after surgery or radiotherapy, no metastases on CT or bone scan
    next: Metastatic, castration-sensitive | Metastases appear
    next: Castration-resistant, no metastases on CT or bone scan | PSA rises on ADT
    = PSA doubling time 9 months or less: enzalutamide with leuprolide 〔NCCN 1〕
      ・Enzalutamide alone 〔NCCN 2A〕
    + EMBARK | OS HR
    ※ Enzalutamide alone delayed metastases but did not lengthen OS
    ※ After prostatectomy with PSA 0.5 ng/mL or more: apalutamide with ADT 〔NCCN 2B〕

  Metastatic, castration-sensitive
    next: Metastatic, castration-resistant
    ① Low volume: ADT with an ARSI 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
      ・Radiotherapy to the prostate can be added in de novo disease 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔NCCN 2A, other recommended〕
      ・Docetaxel can be added in de novo disease for men fit for it who want the most intensive treatment 〔ESMO III, C〕 〔NCCN 2B, other recommended〕
      ・Docetaxel is not given for relapse after surgery or radiotherapy 〔ESMO II, E〕
      - STAMPEDE arm H [t-stampede--advanced-prostate-3] | All patients OS HR 0.92, low burden 0.68
      ※ The trial was negative in all patients
        ・The low-burden subgroup was prespecified
      ※ Arm H gave ADT with or without docetaxel, and no ARSI
      ※ With abiraterone, radiotherapy lengthened rPFS but not OS, and reduced serious urinary events
        ~ PEACE-1 radiotherapy [t-peace-1] | Low volume rPFS HR 0.65
      ※ The benefit was confined to fewer than 5 bone metastases
        - HORRAD [t-horrad] | OS HR
        ~ STOPCAP [t-stopcap-m1-prostate-radiotherapy-meta-analysis] | Fewer than 5 bone metastases 3-year OS 7% higher, all patients OS HR 0.92
    ② High volume: ADT with an ARSI and docetaxel 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕 〔ESMO II, A〕
      ・The ARSI is abiraterone or darolutamide
      ・ADT with an ARSI alone is also preferred 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
      ・ADT with an ARSI alone only if unfit for docetaxel 〔ESMO I, A〕
      ・For relapse after surgery or radiotherapy, docetaxel is optional 〔ESMO II, B〕
      ・Radiotherapy to the prostate can be added in de novo disease to prevent severe urinary symptoms 〔ESMO II, B〕
      + PEACE-1 | OS HR
      + ARASENS | OS HR
      ° Grade 3 or higher hypertension 22% vs 13% (PEACE-1)
      ※ Both trials compared with ADT and docetaxel
        ・Neither compared with ADT and an ARSI
      ※ Docetaxel added to ADT alone improved OS in high volume and not in low volume
        ・ADT with docetaxel alone only where no ARSI is available 〔ESMO I, A〕
        + CHAARTED | OS HR
        + STAMPEDE docetaxel [t-stampede--advanced-prostate] | Docetaxel OS HR 0.78
        - GETUG-AFU 15 | OS HR
    ③ Relapse with 1 to 3 metastases: stereotactic radiotherapy to every site, with or without ADT 〔NCCN 2A, useful in certain circumstances〕 〔ESMO II, B〕
      ・Neither trial had an OS endpoint
      ~ ORIOLE [t-oriole] | PFS HR
      ~ STOMP [t-stomp] | ADT-free survival 21 vs 13 months
    ※ ADT alone is discouraged 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO I, D〕
      ・It is kept for unfit men with a short life expectancy 〔ESMO I, C〕
