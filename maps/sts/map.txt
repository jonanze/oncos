# Scope (Jonan 2026-11-20): adult soft tissue sarcoma of the extremity, trunk wall, retroperitoneum and uterus,
#   with desmoid tumour and tenosynovial giant cell tumour (TGCT) as a locally aggressive branch. GIST and bone
#   sarcomas have their own maps. Ewing sarcoma of soft tissue is on the bone map. Kaposi sarcoma and paediatric
#   rhabdomyosarcoma are left out, as are breast sarcoma, phyllodes tumour, radiotherapy technique beyond dose and
#   timing, and follow-up imaging intervals
# Layout (draft 6): the course is the treatment tree. Diagnosis and extent of disease, then localised disease by
#   site and grade, isolated lung metastases, and advanced disease by chemotherapy sensitivity, fitness for a doublet
#   and histotype. All first-line paths meet at one box after first line, which branches by histotype and biomarker.
#   Every trial chip appears once. "Ifosfamide-sensitive" is undefined in both guidelines: ESMO names synovial
#   sarcoma as chemosensitive and ifosfamide as less active in LMS, and NCCN lists doxorubicin with ifosfamide for
#   any chemotherapy-sensitive histotype. Independent review 2026-11-20 applied (biomarker box joined from both
#   advanced decision points, angiosarcoma later lines, local relapse, lung metastases to advanced, tag wording)

  Soft tissue sarcoma at diagnosis: extent of disease
    ① Referral to a sarcoma reference centre before treatment, for any deep soft tissue mass or a superficial mass of 5 cm or more 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔NCCN〕
    ② Core needle biopsy before treatment, planned so that the tract is removed at surgery 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Excisional biopsy is an option for superficial lesions under 3 cm 〔ESMO〕
    ③ Diagnosis by a sarcoma expert pathologist using the 2020 WHO classification, with FNCLCC grade 〔ESMO IV, A〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Molecular pathology is added when the diagnosis is in doubt, the presentation is unusual, or the entity is defined by a molecular alteration 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Focused molecular testing changed the expert diagnosis in about 1 in 7 sarcomas in a prospective study 〔NCCN〕
    ④ Staging CT of the chest, with CT of the abdomen and pelvis for myxoid liposarcoma and leiomyosarcoma 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Brain MRI is indicated for alveolar soft part sarcoma (ASPS) 〔ESMO IV, A〕
    ⑤ Germline TP53 testing for selected patients under 46 years 〔ESMO IV, A〕
      ・Li-Fraumeni syndrome, NF1 and Lynch syndrome are assessed in the work-up 〔NCCN〕
    ⑥ Extraskeletal Ewing sarcoma: treatment as Ewing sarcoma of bone 〔NCCN〕

  Desmoid tumour
    branch: LAG
    ① Newly diagnosed desmoid tumour without a life-threatening site: active surveillance with MRI 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Spontaneous regression occurs in up to 20% 〔NCCN〕
      ・Familial adenomatous polyposis is excluded, especially in CTNNB1 wild-type tumours 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ② Progressive, symptomatic or morbid disease: systemic treatment with nirogacestat 〔NCCN 1〕 or sorafenib 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔NCCN 1〕
      ・Methotrexate with vinblastine or vinorelbine, pazopanib, imatinib, liposomal doxorubicin, doxorubicin, and doxorubicin with dacarbazine are options 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Surveillance can continue after progression with few symptoms 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ~ DeFi {pmid 36884323}
      ~ Alliance A091105 {pmid 30575484}
      ~ DESMOPAZ {pmid 31331699}
    ③ Progressive extra-abdominal disease: cryoablation 〔ESMO IV, C〕 〔NCCN〕, surgery in favourable sites 〔ESMO IV, C〕 〔NCCN〕, or definitive radiotherapy 〔ESMO III, C〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Surgery is not first-line treatment 〔NCCN〕
      ・Radiotherapy is used after several failed lines or at sites where surgery would cause major morbidity 〔ESMO III, C〕
  Tenosynovial giant cell tumour
    branch: LAG
    ① Tenosynovial giant cell tumour needing systemic treatment: pexidartinib 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN 1〕, vimseltinib 〔NCCN 1〕, or pimicotinib
      ・Pexidartinib holds a US licence and no EU licence
      ・Pimicotinib received an FDA licence in November 2026
      ・Imatinib and nilotinib are options 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ~ ENLIVEN {pmid 31229240}
      ~ MOTION {pmid 38843860}
      ~ MANEUVER {pmid 41796601}

  Low-grade STS
    branch: LOC

  High-grade STS
    branch: LOC
    ① Limb-sparing wide excision with radiotherapy, 50 Gy before surgery or up to 66 Gy after surgery 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔NCCN 1〕
      ・Radiotherapy before surgery is preferred 〔NCCN〕, or when a critical structure is to be preserved 〔ESMO〕
      ・Radiotherapy before surgery causes more wound complications and less late fibrosis, oedema and joint stiffness 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Radiotherapy may be omitted after multidisciplinary review of margins, size and histotype 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・36 Gy before surgery can be considered for myxoid liposarcoma 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ~ Rosenberg 1982 {pmid 7114936}
      ~ Yang 1998 {pmid 9440743}
      ~ Pisters 1996 {pmid 8622034}
      ~ O’Sullivan (NCIC SR2) {pmid 12103287}
      ~ DOREMY {pmid 33180100}
    ② High risk of death, a chemotherapy-sensitive histotype and fitness for treatment: 3 cycles of an anthracycline with ifosfamide, preferably before surgery 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・High risk is a predicted 10-year overall survival under 60% by the Sarculator nomogram 〔ESMO II, B〕
      ・Trabectedin is an option for myxoid liposarcoma in place of an anthracycline with ifosfamide 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Pembrolizumab with radiotherapy before surgery, then pembrolizumab after surgery, is preferred for UPS-related sarcomas of the extremity and limb girdle 〔NCCN〕
      ・Regional hyperthermia with chemotherapy is an option 〔ESMO I, B〕
      ~ Frustaci 2001 {pmid 11230464}
      ~ Gronchi 2012 {pmid 22312103}
      ~ ISG-STS 1001 {pmid 28499583}
      ~ Pasquali 2019 (EORTC 62931) {pmid 30690293}
      + Pervaiz meta-analysis {pmid 18521899}
      ~ SU2C-SARC032 {pmid 39547252}
      + EORTC 62961 {pmid 20434400}
    ※ Adjuvant doxorubicin with ifosfamide did not improve overall or relapse-free survival after resection of grade 2 to 3 STS
      - EORTC 62931 {pmid 22954508}
  Retroperitoneal sarcoma
    branch: VIS
    ① Primary retroperitoneal sarcoma: en bloc resection with adherent organs at a high-volume centre 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Percutaneous core biopsy by a retroperitoneal route precedes resection 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Grossly incomplete resection is avoided 〔ESMO〕
    ② Low- or intermediate-grade liposarcoma: radiotherapy before surgery may be discussed 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Radiotherapy before surgery is not considered for leiomyosarcoma or high-grade dedifferentiated liposarcoma 〔ESMO〕
    ※ Radiotherapy before surgery did not improve abdominal recurrence-free survival in resectable retroperitoneal sarcoma 〔ESMO I, D〕 〔NCCN〕
      - STRASS (EORTC-62092) {pmid 32941794}
    ③ Borderline resectable disease or a chemotherapy-sensitive histotype, such as synovial sarcoma: chemotherapy, radiotherapy or regional hyperthermia with chemotherapy before surgery 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ④ After resection: no routine radiotherapy 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・The value of chemotherapy before or after surgery is not established 〔ESMO〕
  Localised uterine sarcoma
    branch: VIS
    ① Uterine leiomyosarcoma, endometrial stromal sarcoma or undifferentiated uterine sarcoma: en bloc total hysterectomy without morcellation 〔ESMO IV, A〕 〔NCCN Uterine〕
      ・Systematic lymphadenectomy is not useful 〔ESMO〕
      ・Ovarian preservation is possible 〔ESMO〕
      ・Oophorectomy is decided individually at reproductive age, and favoured for oestrogen or progesterone receptor-positive tumours 〔NCCN Uterine〕
      ・Oestrogen and progesterone receptor testing is considered for leiomyosarcoma, endometrial stromal sarcoma and adenosarcoma 〔NCCN Uterine〕
    ② After hysterectomy: no adjuvant radiotherapy 〔ESMO I, D〕
      ・Adjuvant chemotherapy for uterine leiomyosarcoma is not standard 〔ESMO IV, C〕
      ・Stage I high-grade endometrial stromal sarcoma, undifferentiated uterine sarcoma or leiomyosarcoma: observation 〔NCCN Uterine〕
      ・Stage II or III: observation after complete resection with negative margins, or systemic therapy, radiotherapy or both are considered 〔NCCN Uterine〕
      - EORTC 55874 {pmid 18378136}
    ※ Adjuvant gemcitabine with docetaxel, then doxorubicin, did not improve overall survival over observation in uterine-limited leiomyosarcoma, in a trial closed for poor accrual
      - GOG 0277 {pmid 30289732}
    ③ Low-grade endometrial stromal sarcoma or adenosarcoma without sarcomatous overgrowth: bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy 〔NCCN Uterine〕
      ・Stage I: observation after salpingo-oophorectomy 〔NCCN Uterine〕
      ・Stage II to IV: endocrine therapy is an option 〔NCCN Uterine〕, and radiotherapy for stage II to IVA 〔NCCN Uterine 2B〕
      ・Adenosarcoma with sarcomatous overgrowth, stage II to IV: systemic therapy is considered, and recommended for residual measurable disease 〔NCCN Uterine〕
  Isolated lung metastases
    ① Resectable lung metastases after a disease-free interval of 1 year or more: surgery 〔ESMO IV, B〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Chemotherapy before surgery is an option 〔ESMO IV, B〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Stereotactic radiotherapy or ablation are alternatives 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ② Synchronous lung metastases: chemotherapy 〔ESMO III, B〕
      ・Surgery for resectable residual lung metastases is an option after chemotherapy, especially after a response 〔ESMO〕
  Advanced STS: chemotherapy sensitivity of the histotype
    ① Unresectable lung or extrapulmonary metastatic disease: systemic treatment with palliative intent 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Histotypes insensitive to cytotoxic chemotherapy include ASPS, extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma, clear cell sarcoma and extremity atypical lipomatous tumour 〔NCCN〕
      ・ASPS and clear cell sarcoma are refractory to an anthracycline with ifosfamide 〔ESMO〕
      ・Advanced uterine leiomyosarcoma, undifferentiated endometrial sarcoma and adenosarcoma with sarcomatous overgrowth are treated as STS 〔ESMO〕
      ・Gemcitabine with docetaxel is a preferred first-line option for uterine sarcoma 〔NCCN Uterine, preferred〕
    ② NTRK fusion screening: pan-TRK immunohistochemistry, with molecular confirmation of a positive result 〔ESMO〕
