# Scope (Jonan 2026-11-16): one map for the sheet. Systemic therapy only. Surgery, the axilla, radiotherapy,
#   bone-modifying agents, surgery of the primary tumour in de novo stage IV disease and oligometastatic disease are
#   out. Chips may cite rows of the shared sheet "General Principles" (OlympiA, OlympiAD, EMBRACA, TNT, EMBRACE,
#   Gennari, CALOR, CTNeoBC and the chemotherapy regimen rows)
# Layout: a root step fans out to three steps side by side: neoadjuvant therapy, primary surgery and first-line
#   treatment of metastatic disease. Early and metastatic disease are never joined by a line. Each next: line joins
#   true time order only: neoadjuvant therapy, then treatment after surgery. Primary surgery with adjuvant
#   chemotherapy, then treatment after it. First line, then later lines.
#   Testing, regimens with doses, and toxicity are bands. Chemotherapy for metastatic disease and special
#   populations are panels. Criteria (stage, size, nodes, PD-L1 cut-off, relapse interval) are written into the
#   numbered lines (Jonan, no criteria table). Early special cases sit in the early steps: inflammatory and inoperable
#   locally advanced disease with neoadjuvant therapy, secretory, adenoid cystic, metaplastic and apocrine carcinoma with primary surgery
# One narrative: a point several guidelines agree on is one statement with all their tags. A ・ line gives a difference
#   (a cut-off, a line of treatment, a grade), a condition or a licence. One numbered line per population
# Guideline set (Jonan 2026-11-16): PAGA 2024 (early) and 2023 (metastatic), ESMO 2026 (early, in place of ESMO 2024 from 2026-11-27) and 2026 with Living
#   Guideline v2.0 (metastatic), ASCO, NCCN 9.2026, St Gallen 2025 (early) and ABC8 2026 (advanced). No JBCS.
#   Tag order PAGA, ESMO, ASCO, NCCN, St Gallen, ABC8
#   The ASCO 2021 neoadjuvant guideline (Korde) and its 2022 pembrolizumab update are archived (Jonan 2026-11-16) and
#   are never tagged. Early ASCO tags come only from the 2021 adjuvant olaparib update (Tung), which prints no rating
# Tags give the grade as printed, evidence first and strength second, on the statement the guideline graded:
#   〔PAGA level, grade〕 Pan-Asian adapted ESMO guidelines: early breast cancer 2024 (Park, ESMO Open 2024;9:102974) on
#     the early steps, metastatic breast cancer 2023 (Im, ESMO Open 2023;8:101541) on the metastatic steps. A bare
#     〔PAGA〕 marks body text, a figure footnote, an ungraded Table 1 row or a licence note
#   〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO early breast cancer guideline 2026 (Trapani, Ann Oncol 2026, journal pre-proof accepted
#     9 November 2026, PMID 43085660; Supplementary Material not read) on the early steps,
#     ESMO metastatic breast cancer guideline 2026 (de Azambuja, Ann Oncol 2026;37:1203-1219, Living Guideline v2.0) on
#     the metastatic steps. A bare 〔ESMO〕 marks ungraded text, a figure or algorithm footnote, or a licence note
#   〔ASCO quality, strength〕 ASCO chemotherapy and targeted therapy update for HER2-negative metastatic disease 2021
#     (Moy) with its rapid updates of 2022 on trastuzumab deruxtecan (Moy) and of 2023 on sacituzumab govitecan (Moy,
#     J Clin Oncol 2023;41:1318-1320, PMID 36626701). The 2023 update changes only the recommendation for HR-positive
#     disease. A bare 〔ASCO〕 marks the unrated 2021 adjuvant olaparib update (Tung) or a qualifying statement
#   〔NCCN category〕 NCCN Breast Cancer 9.2026 (22 October 2026), with "preferred" as printed. A bare 〔NCCN〕 marks a
#     footnote, a dosing page or the Discussion
#   〔St Gallen〕 St Gallen consensus 2025 (Burstein, Ann Oncol 2025;36:1433-1446), a panel vote with no grade. The
#     source is declared as "St" so that the tag prints "St Gallen"
#   〔ABC8 level/grade〕 ABC8 international consensus 2026 (Cardoso, Breast 2026;90:104921), LoE/GoR as printed in the
#     statement tables, without the consensus percentage. A bare 〔ABC8〕 marks a figure or the narrative
#   One link per source name (outline.js): PAGA and ASCO open the paper that carries most of their tags, and ESMO opens
#     the ESMO breast cancer guideline page, which lists the early and the metastatic guideline
# Trial marks (not drawn): + overall survival benefit, ~ surrogate endpoint only or single-arm, - primary endpoint not
#   met or no benefit from the tested change (a positive secondary or subgroup result does not change the mark, so
#   GeparNuevo, Gupta et al. (TMC) and TNT are -), * no comparison for the statement
# Single-arm exception (inclusion rule ①, as APT on the HER2+ EBC map and PERUSE on the HER2+ MBC map): NeoPACT, since
#   the NCCN preoperative option of carboplatin and docetaxel with pembrolizumab rests on it and no randomised trial of
#   that regimen exists
# Atezolizumab with nab-paclitaxel sits on a ※ line under the pembrolizumab option (Jonan 2026-11-16)
# ※ lines without a guideline tag: ECOG-ACRIN EA1131 and OptiTROP-Breast01, which no guideline of the set cites.
#   Sacituzumab tirumotecan is licensed in China only and stays on a ※ line (inclusion.txt)

band: Testing
  ① Germline BRCA1/2 testing is offered to patients who meet national criteria and to candidates for adjuvant olaparib 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Triple-negative disease at any age is itself an indication for testing 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔St Gallen〕
    ・Germline testing is not reimbursed in Singapore 〔PAGA〕
  ② Germline BRCA1/2 status is tested in HER2-negative metastatic disease 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・Where treatment is available, germline PALB2 〔ESMO I, A〕 and somatic BRCA1/2 〔ESMO III, A〕 are also tested
    ・Testing of other genes can be considered, and germline PALB2 is the only one with a treatment implication 〔ABC8 Expert opinion/C〕
  ③ PD-L1 is tested before first-line treatment of metastatic disease 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ABC8 I/A〕, and a combined positive score (CPS) of 10 or more selects pembrolizumab 〔PAGA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・The CPS is read with the 22C3 antibody 〔NCCN〕
    ・PD-L1 staining of 1% or more of immune cells selects atezolizumab 〔ESMO〕 〔ABC8 I/A〕, and the staining is read with the SP142 antibody 〔PAGA〕 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・A metastatic sample is preferred for PD-L1 testing 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・When the metastatic sample is negative, the primary tumour is tested, and a positive result on either sample is sufficient 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・In early disease, PD-L1 does not guide treatment 〔PAGA I, E〕 〔ESMO I, E〕
  ④ HER2 is reassessed as low, ultralow or 0 in metastatic disease 〔ESMO I, A〕
    ・HER2-low is IHC 1+, or IHC 2+ with negative in situ hybridisation 〔PAGA〕 〔ASCO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・HER2-low status on one sample, from any stage of the disease, is sufficient for trastuzumab deruxtecan 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・After an initial HER2 0 result, HER2 is reassessed during the course of the disease 〔ABC8 I/A〕
  ⑤ A metastatic lesion is biopsied at the first diagnosis of metastatic disease, to confirm histology and reassess ER, PgR and HER2 〔PAGA I, B〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ABC8 I/B〕
    ・When the receptor status of the metastasis differs from that of the primary tumour, treatment follows the metastasis 〔PAGA〕 〔ESMO V, B〕
    ・When ER or HER2 is positive on at least one biopsy, endocrine or anti-HER2 therapy is considered, whatever the timing of the biopsy 〔ABC8 Expert opinion/B〕
  ⑥ Tumour genomic profiling is performed when the result would change treatment or give access to a trial 〔PAGA V, B〕 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ABC8 I/B〕
    ・MSI-H or dMMR, a high tumour mutational burden, NTRK and RET fusions and BRAF mutations select tumour-agnostic therapies 〔ESMO III, B〕, in the third line or later 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ※ Tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes add prognostic information in early disease, with no threshold for treatment decisions 〔PAGA I, B〕 〔ESMO I, B〕 〔St Gallen〕
  ※ No validated biomarker selects patients for sacituzumab govitecan or datopotamab deruxtecan 〔ABC8〕

band: Regimens and doses in early disease
  ① Pembrolizumab with chemotherapy: pembrolizumab 200 mg every 3 weeks, with paclitaxel 80 mg/m² weekly and carboplatin AUC 5 every 3 weeks or AUC 1.5 weekly for four cycles, then with doxorubicin 60 mg/m² or epirubicin 90 mg/m² and cyclophosphamide 600 mg/m² every 3 weeks for four cycles, then pembrolizumab 200 mg every 3 weeks for nine cycles after surgery 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
    ・Every preoperative cycle is given with G-CSF 〔NCCN〕
    ・The benefit of G-CSF with checkpoint inhibitor regimens is uncertain 〔PAGA〕
  ② Dose-dense anthracycline and taxane with G-CSF 〔PAGA I, B〕 〔ESMO〕: doxorubicin 60 mg/m² and cyclophosphamide 600 mg/m² every 14 days for four cycles, then paclitaxel 175 mg/m² every 14 days for four cycles or 80 mg/m² weekly for 12 weeks 〔NCCN〕
    ・With immunotherapy, dose-dense scheduling lacks high-level evidence and remains an option in high-risk disease 〔ESMO〕
  + INT C9741 (CALGB 9741) {pmid 12668651}
  + EBCTCG 2019 {pmid 30739743}
  + ECOG E1199 {pmid 18420499}
  ③ Carboplatin with paclitaxel: paclitaxel 80 mg/m² weekly with carboplatin AUC 5 to 6 every 3 weeks for four cycles before surgery, or with carboplatin AUC 1.5 to 2 weekly on days 1, 8 and 15 every 28 days for six cycles 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ④ Docetaxel and cyclophosphamide: docetaxel 75 mg/m² and cyclophosphamide 600 mg/m² every 21 days for four to six cycles 〔NCCN〕
  + US Oncology 9735 {pmid 17135639}
  ⑤ Carboplatin and docetaxel with pembrolizumab: carboplatin AUC 6, docetaxel 75 mg/m² and pembrolizumab 200 mg every 21 days for six cycles before surgery, with G-CSF 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ※ Dose-dense doxorubicin and cyclophosphamide then paclitaxel, and docetaxel with cyclophosphamide, are preferred in stage I disease and can be considered in stage II and III disease when pembrolizumab is not given 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
  ※ Chemotherapy lasts 12 to 24 weeks, or four to eight cycles, with or without pembrolizumab 〔PAGA I, A〕
  ※ Neoadjuvant therapy starts promptly after staging, ideally within 2 to 4 weeks 〔ESMO V, A〕, and adjuvant chemotherapy starts within 4 to 6 weeks of surgery 〔ESMO IV, A〕
    ・Efficacy is reduced when adjuvant chemotherapy starts more than 12 weeks after surgery 〔ESMO〕
    ・Surgery usually follows neoadjuvant chemotherapy within 4 to 6 weeks, and outcomes are worse with delays beyond 9 weeks 〔ESMO〕
  ※ Fertility preservation is discussed before systemic treatment 〔PAGA V, A〕 〔ESMO V, A〕

band: Regimens and doses in metastatic disease
  ① Pembrolizumab with chemotherapy: pembrolizumab 200 mg every 21 days or 400 mg every 42 days, with nab-paclitaxel 100 mg/m² or paclitaxel 90 mg/m² on days 1, 8 and 15 every 28 days, or with carboplatin AUC 2 and gemcitabine 1000 mg/m² on days 1 and 8 every 21 days 〔NCCN〕
  ② Sacituzumab govitecan 10 mg/kg on days 1 and 8 every 21 days, alone or with pembrolizumab 200 mg every 21 days 〔NCCN〕
  ③ Datopotamab deruxtecan 6 mg/kg every 21 days 〔NCCN〕
  ④ Trastuzumab deruxtecan 5.4 mg/kg every 21 days 〔NCCN〕
  ⑤ Olaparib 300 mg twice daily, or talazoparib 1 mg daily 〔NCCN〕
  ⑥ Platinum with a germline BRCA1/2 variant: carboplatin AUC 6 every 21 to 28 days, or cisplatin 75 mg/m² every 21 days 〔NCCN〕

band: Toxicity and monitoring
  ① Patients on pembrolizumab are monitored closely for immune-related adverse events during and after treatment 〔PAGA V, A〕, including hypothyroidism and adrenal insufficiency 〔NCCN〕
    ・Immune-related endocrine disease can be permanent, and the effect on fertility is unknown 〔St Gallen〕
    ・A history of autoimmune disease calls for an individual assessment of risk 〔ASCO〕
    ・Corticosteroids and antibiotics may reduce the efficacy of checkpoint inhibitors 〔ABC8 III/A〕
  ② Patients on trastuzumab deruxtecan are monitored regularly for interstitial lung disease 〔NCCN〕, including by CT 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・At grade 1, trastuzumab deruxtecan is withheld and prednisone at 0.5 mg/kg or more is considered 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・Treatment restarts at the same dose if the lung disease resolves within 28 days, and one dose level lower if recovery takes longer 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・At grade 2 or higher, trastuzumab deruxtecan is stopped permanently, and prednisone at 1 mg/kg or more is started promptly and tapered over at least 4 weeks 〔ABC8 I/A〕
  ③ Testing for dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase deficiency is recommended before capecitabine 〔ESMO V, A〕
    ・Testing can be considered before treatment or after unexpected toxicity, and the deficiency is rare in Asian countries 〔PAGA II, D〕
  ° Toxicity of note: neutropenia, diarrhoea, nausea and vomiting with sacituzumab govitecan, managed with patient education, prophylaxis and G-CSF as clinically indicated 〔ABC8 I/A〕
  ° Toxicity of note: stomatitis, nausea and dry eye with datopotamab deruxtecan, managed with steroid mouthwash, antiemetics, eye drops and regular ophthalmological review 〔ABC8 I/A〕

course: Systemic treatment of triple-negative breast cancer
  Triple-negative breast cancer
    ① Triple-negative disease is ER-negative and PgR-negative, with staining in fewer than 1% of cells, and HER2-negative 〔NCCN〕
    ※ Some ER-low tumours (ER 1 to 9%) behave as triple-negative disease, with a higher response to chemotherapy and less benefit from endocrine therapy 〔PAGA II, B〕, and are treated in a similar way 〔ESMO〕
      ・The ER-low range is 1 to 10% 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕 〔ABC8〕
      ・Endocrine therapy is standard in early ER-low disease 〔St Gallen〕, and the decision is individualised 〔NCCN〕
      ・Omission of endocrine therapy may be associated with worse overall survival, particularly with residual disease after neoadjuvant therapy or ER of 6 to 10% 〔ESMO〕
      ・In stage II, high-grade ER-low disease, neoadjuvant chemotherapy with an immune checkpoint inhibitor is recommended 〔St Gallen〕
      ・In metastatic ER-low disease, treatment options extend beyond endocrine therapy, and trials for triple-negative disease are open to these patients 〔ABC8 III/B〕

  Early disease: neoadjuvant therapy
    branch: NEO
    ① Stage II and III, cT2 to cT4 or node-positive: pembrolizumab with paclitaxel and carboplatin, followed by pembrolizumab with an anthracycline and cyclophosphamide 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕 〔St Gallen〕
      ・Pembrolizumab is withheld when risk factors for excessive immune toxicity are present 〔PAGA〕
    + KEYNOTE-522 {pmid 32101663}
    ② cT1c cN0: neoadjuvant chemotherapy without pembrolizumab 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
      ・Regimens include an anthracycline with cyclophosphamide followed by a taxane, with carboplatin 〔ESMO〕
      ・Preoperative chemotherapy can be considered at cT1c cN0, and is preferred from cT2 or cN1 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Neoadjuvant therapy is favoured from about 2 cm, and a T1c N0 tumour is treated with surgery followed by an anthracycline and a taxane 〔St Gallen〕
    ③ Stage II and III when pembrolizumab is not given: an anthracycline with cyclophosphamide and a taxane, with carboplatin 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔St Gallen〕 or without it 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・The regimen with carboplatin is preferred 〔ESMO〕 〔St Gallen〕, whatever the germline BRCA1/2 status 〔ESMO〕
      ・Carboplatin raises the rate of pathological complete response 〔St Gallen〕 and improves disease-free survival 〔ESMO〕 〔St Gallen〕, at the cost of more haematological toxicity 〔ESMO〕
      ・Carboplatin improves overall survival, with a greater benefit suggested before menopause 〔ESMO〕
      ・A gain in overall survival with carboplatin is not proven 〔St Gallen〕

    ~ CALGB 40603 {pmid 25092775}
    ~ BrighTNess {pmid 29501363}

    ④ Anthracycline contraindicated: a regimen without an anthracycline is an option 〔ESMO V, B〕, such as carboplatin and docetaxel with pembrolizumab for six cycles before surgery 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ~ NeoPACT {pmid 37991778}
    ⑤ Inoperable locally advanced or inflammatory disease: chemotherapy with an anthracycline, a taxane and a platinum, with pembrolizumab whatever the PD-L1 status 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ※ Neoadjuvant chemotherapy can be considered in triple-negative lobular carcinoma 〔ESMO IV, B〕
    ※ The association of a pathological complete response with survival is strongest in triple-negative disease 〔NCCN〕

  Metastatic disease: first line
    branch: MET
    ① PD-L1 CPS of 10 or more, de novo disease or relapse 6 months or more after neoadjuvant or adjuvant treatment: pembrolizumab with paclitaxel, nab-paclitaxel or carboplatin and gemcitabine 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕 〔ABC8 I/A〕, or pembrolizumab with sacituzumab govitecan 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕 〔ABC8 I/A〕
