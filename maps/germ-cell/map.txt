# oncOS board map: Germ Cell Tumors (testicular germ cell tumours from stage I to salvage, with primary mediastinal germ cell tumours)
# 2026-11-14, approved by Jonan for publication. Structure plan: board-maps/germ-cell/structure-plan.md. Changes from draft 0
#   and the answer to each independent-review finding: board-maps/germ-cell/draft1-changes.md. Layout QC: fable-layout-qc.md
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
# Scope (Jonan 2026-11-14): one map for the whole sheet. Stages are parallel presentations, never a sequence (Jonan
#   rejected stage I leading into stage II into metastatic disease). The six stage steps start the course with no header
#   step above them (Jonan 2026-11-14: the Seminoma and Non-seminoma boxes were redundant). Relapse follows prior
#   treatment (Jonan 2026-11-14, "a more clinically accurate representation of relapsed disease"): stage I leads to a
#   relapse step per histology for relapse after surveillance or adjuvant treatment, treated as disease at presentation.
#   Stage II and metastatic disease lead to the residual-mass step after chemotherapy, then to first relapse after
#   cisplatin-based chemotherapy, then to second or later relapse or cisplatin-refractory disease. Clinical stage IS is a
#   panel of its own for both histologies (Jonan 2026-11-14). The IGCCCG risk groups are a table panel. Primary
#   mediastinal germ cell tumours are a panel named after the metastatic non-seminoma step, and late relapse a panel
#   named after the first-relapse step. canvas.js puts every panel in the row below the course with no brace, and each
#   named panel takes the pale tint of its step. No panel for other extragonadal primaries or for survivorship.
#   Intracranial germ cell tumours are a separate sheet
# Layout (Jonan 2026-11-14, "lines bending unnecessarily"): the course carries "| parallel", so canvas.js left-aligns the
#   columns and sets each later step level with the steps that lead to it. Line labels only where they add information
# One narrative: a point several guidelines agree on is one statement with all their tags. A ・ line gives a difference
#   only where guidelines disagree (an option, a cut-off, a dose, a number of cycles, the direction of a grade), a
#   condition or a licence. Numbered lines name the option with its condition. ※, ・ and ° lines are full sentences
# East Asian guidelines come first (Jonan 2026-11-10): the JUA tag is printed first. Japanese practice points are recorded
#   with the JUA tag (consolidation with a salvage regimen before confirmed progression, the paclitaxel licence,
#   high-dose chemotherapy at few centres, microRNA unavailable). Nedaplatin regimens sit on ※ lines marked Japan only
# Options NCCN alone lists sit on ※ lines or as ・ lines (inclusion.txt, Jonan 2026-11-10): adjuvant radiotherapy for stage I
#   seminoma, carboplatin with involved-node radiotherapy, intensification for good-risk seminoma with a high LDH, adjuvant
#   chemotherapy after resection of viable seminoma, high-dose chemotherapy at first relapse, tumour-agnostic treatment
# Tags give the grade as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#   〔JUA strength, certainty〕 Japanese Urological Association, clinical practice guideline for testicular cancer, 2024
#     edition (third edition, Kanehara, 29 February 2024, Japanese only). Strength 1 strong or 2 weak (the book prints
#     強い and 弱い), certainty A to D. "No recommendation" is printed where the vote reached none. A bare 〔JUA〕 marks
#     the overview text or an algorithm box, which carry no grade. The tag links to the Minds summary page, not checked
#     from this machine
#   〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO-EURACAN guideline 2022 (Oldenburg, Ann Oncol 2022, 33:362-375). A bare 〔ESMO〕 marks text or
#     a figure without a grade
#   〔EAU Strong〕 or 〔EAU Weak〕 EAU Guidelines on Testicular Cancer 2026, full text (limited update March 2026). 〔EAU LE〕 is
#     a level of evidence from a summary-of-evidence table. A bare 〔EAU〕 marks narrative text or a figure of the full text.
#     Points found only in the 2026 summary paper carry no EAU tag
#   〔NCCN category〕 NCCN Testicular Cancer 1.2027 (6 November 2026). 2A unless stated, with preferred and strongly
#     preferred as printed. A bare 〔NCCN〕 marks a footnote, a principles page or the Discussion
#   〔IGCCCG 1997〕, 〔IGCCCG-NS 2021〕 and 〔IGCCCG-S 2021〕 open the three Evidence rows behind the risk table. They are
#     source: lines because the rows are pooled cohorts, which a trial chip cannot cite (proposal, plan section 9)
# Order where guidelines disagree is Jonan's call (plan section 9). His answers are pending, and the recommended defaults
#   are set, each open to a small edit:
#   stage IIA and IIB seminoma: chemotherapy ①, radiotherapy ②, RPLND ③ (to flip, swap the first two option blocks and
#     move the EAU preference line with chemotherapy)
#   stage I non-seminoma with lymphovascular invasion: one cycle of BEP as the option, surveillance on a ・ line
#   first relapse: four cycles of TIP ①, with high-dose chemotherapy on a ※ line tagged NCCN alone and the ESMO, EAU
#     and JUA positions on ・ lines (to number it, change that ※ to ② and resection to ③, keeping the ・ lines)
#   poor-risk non-seminoma: intensification after an unfavourable marker decline as numbered item ③ on ESMO and EAU,
#     with the JUA recommendation against on a ・ line (to flip, change ③ to ※)
# Trial marks: + overall survival benefit, ~ surrogate endpoint, non-inferiority, equivalence or single-arm, - no benefit
#   or harm from the tested change. Loehrer et al. and MRC TE09/EORTC 30896 are - because the tested change (no bleomycin,
#   carboplatin for cisplatin) gave worse survival. ECOG E3887 and de Wit 1998 are - because VIP was not better than BEP
# Single-arm trials: Kondagunta et al. (TIP), TI-CE, SEMS (RPLND in stage IIA and IIB seminoma) and SAKK 01/10 (carboplatin
#   with involved-node radiotherapy) are cited because the recommendations rest on them and no randomised trial of these
#   options has reported (inclusion.txt ①, plan section 9). Every other non-randomised row is under off:

band: Diagnosis, markers and staging
  ① Radical orchidectomy through an inguinal incision gives the diagnosis and the pT category, and a scrotal approach is avoided 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・In life-threatening metastatic disease with raised markers, chemotherapy starts before orchidectomy 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN〕
  ② Bilateral testicular ultrasound is performed in every patient with a suspected testicular tumour 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ③ AFP, hCG and LDH are measured before and after orchidectomy 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ※ The half-life of AFP is 5 to 7 days and that of hCG 1 to 3 days 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
  ※ Pure seminoma on histology with a clearly raised AFP is treated as non-seminoma 〔JUA〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・A mildly raised, stable AFP can be managed as seminoma 〔NCCN〕
  ④ Contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen and pelvis is the staging examination 〔JUA 1, B〕 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔EAU Strong〕
    ・Seminoma is staged with CT of the abdomen and pelvis and a chest radiograph, with chest CT only when either is abnormal 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ※ FDG-PET is not used for staging 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO V, D〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN〕
  ⑤ Brain MRI is indicated in poor-risk disease, with multiple lung metastases, a high hCG or neurological symptoms 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・The hCG trigger is above 5,000 mIU/mL (5,000 IU/L) 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・In non-seminoma, choriocarcinoma predominance and AFP above 10,000 ng/mL (10,000 µg/L) are further triggers 〔NCCN〕
  ※ Testis-sparing surgery with frozen section is an option for synchronous bilateral tumours or a solitary testis in experienced centres 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ※ Contralateral testicular biopsy is not routine, and is discussed with men at high risk of germ cell neoplasia in situ, such as a testicular volume under 12 mL or cryptorchidism 〔ESMO V, C〕 〔EAU Strong〕
  ※ Serum microRNA-371a-3p is not recommended in routine care 〔JUA no recommendation, C〕 〔ESMO III, D〕
    ・The test is not available in Japan, even at the patient's own cost 〔JUA〕

band: First-line chemotherapy and supportive care
  ① BEP every 21 days: cisplatin 20 mg/m² and etoposide 100 mg/m² on days 1 to 5, with bleomycin 30 units weekly 〔JUA〕 〔NCCN〕
  ~ Williams et al.
  ※ Full doses at 21-day intervals are kept wherever possible 〔JUA〕
  ※ Bleomycin is avoided with heavy smoking, lung disease, older age or reduced renal function 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・A planned lung resection is a further reason 〔JUA〕
  ② VIP every 21 days with primary G-CSF prophylaxis: etoposide 75 mg/m², ifosfamide 1,200 mg/m² with mesna and cisplatin 20 mg/m² on days 1 to 5 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
  ③ Sperm banking is discussed before treatment 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ④ Thromboprophylaxis during first-line chemotherapy is an individual decision 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔EAU Weak〕
  ⑤ Peripheral venous access is used in place of a central venous device 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔EAU Weak〕
  ※ Primary G-CSF prophylaxis is considered in intermediate- and poor-risk disease 〔ESMO〕
  ※ Filgrastim with BEP-based chemotherapy improved delivery of the planned cycles, with no change in failure-free or overall survival
    ~ Fosså 1998 {pmid 9469362}
  ※ G-CSF given with bleomycin was not associated with more bleomycin pulmonary toxicity in observational studies
    ~ Tolstrup 2026 {pmid 42998777}
  ° Toxicity of note: pulmonary toxicity with bleomycin, and more myelosuppression with VIP than with BEP 〔JUA〕 〔EAU〕

course: Testicular germ cell tumours after orchidectomy | parallel
  Stage I seminoma
    branch: SEM
    ① Surveillance 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A, strongly preferred〕
    ※ Relapse on surveillance occurs in 12 to 20% at 5 years, and cancer-specific survival exceeds 99% 〔EAU LE 2a〕
    ※ Abdominal MRI can replace CT for follow-up imaging 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
      ~ TRISST {pmid 35298280}
    ② One cycle of carboplatin AUC 7 when surveillance is declined or unsuitable 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔EAU Strong〕
      ・One or two cycles are given, with no preference between them 〔JUA〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Adjuvant carboplatin is not offered to patients without risk factors 〔ESMO III, D〕 〔EAU Strong〕
    ~ MRC TE19/EORTC 30982
    ※ The risk factors for relapse are tumour size over 4 cm and rete testis invasion 〔JUA〕 〔EAU LE 2b〕
      ・Tumour size and rete testis invasion are used as continuous risk factors without a size cut-off 〔ESMO〕
      ・Hilar invasion, lymphovascular invasion and raised preorchidectomy hCG and LDH also predict relapse 〔EAU LE 2a〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Risk-adapted treatment is not recommended, and any size cut-off is arbitrary 〔NCCN〕
    ※ Adjuvant radiotherapy is not recommended 〔JUA 2, B〕 〔ESMO II, D〕 〔EAU Strong〕
      ・Para-aortic radiotherapy of 20 Gy in 10 fractions is an option for patients who decline surveillance 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Radiotherapy is reserved for highly selected patients unsuitable for chemotherapy 〔EAU Strong〕
      ~ MRC TE18 / EORTC 30942
    ° Toxicity of note: second cancers after adjuvant radiotherapy 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU LE 2b〕
    next: Seminoma relapse after surveillance or adjuvant treatment
  Stage IIA and IIB seminoma
    branch: SEM
    ① Three cycles of BEP, or four cycles of EP 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔EAU Weak〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ~ Giannatempo 2015 {pmid 25214543}
    ② Radiotherapy to the para-aortic and ipsilateral iliac nodes: 30 Gy in stage IIA and 36 Gy in stage IIB 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔EAU Weak〕
      ・Radiotherapy is limited to nodes under 3 cm, with 30 Gy for 1 to 2 cm and 36 Gy for 2 to 3 cm 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・In stage IIB, radiotherapy is for patients unsuitable for chemotherapy 〔ESMO II, A〕
    ③ Nerve-sparing RPLND for marker-negative nodes of 3 cm or less, in expert centres only 〔EAU Weak〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・The grade is lower for nodes of 2 to 3 cm 〔NCCN 2B〕
    ~ SEMS {pmid 36913642}
    ※ Carboplatin AUC 7 followed by involved-node radiotherapy is an option for nodes under 3 cm 〔NCCN 2B〕
      ・The evidence is insufficient for routine practice 〔EAU〕
      ~ SAKK 01/10 {pmid 36228644}
    ※ Single-agent carboplatin has insufficient efficacy in stage II 〔JUA〕
    ※ Marker-negative nodes under 2 cm are confirmed by repeat imaging after 6 to 8 weeks or by biopsy before treatment 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔EAU〕
      ・The interval is 4 to 8 weeks 〔NCCN〕
    ※ Stage IIA means nodes of 2 cm or less, and stage IIB nodes over 2 cm and up to 5 cm 〔JUA〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
    next: Residual mass after chemotherapy for seminoma | After chemotherapy
  Metastatic seminoma, stage IIC and III
    branch: SEM
    ① Good risk: three cycles of BEP 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕, or four cycles of EP 〔JUA〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
      ・EP is given when bleomycin is contraindicated or undesirable 〔JUA 2, C〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕
    ② Intermediate risk: four cycles of BEP 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕, or four cycles of VIP when bleomycin is contraindicated 〔JUA 2, C〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ※ In good-risk disease with LDH above 2.5 times the upper limit of normal, intensification of treatment can be considered 〔NCCN〕
      ・Treatment as for intermediate risk has a rationale, without prospective data 〔EAU〕
    ※ Single-agent carboplatin is inferior to cisplatin-based combination chemotherapy 〔EAU LE 1b〕
      - Bokemeyer 2004 {pmid 15266338}
    next: Residual mass after chemotherapy for seminoma
  Stage I non-seminoma
    branch: NS
    ① Without lymphovascular invasion: surveillance 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
      ・One cycle of BEP is given when surveillance is unsuitable 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ② With lymphovascular invasion: one cycle of BEP 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Surveillance is the alternative 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔EAU Strong〕
      ・Surveillance, one cycle of BEP and RPLND carry no order of preference 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Invasion of the spermatic cord or scrotum also counts as a risk factor 〔NCCN〕
    ~ AUO AH 01/94 (GTCSG) [t-auo-ah-01-94]
    ③ Nerve-sparing RPLND when surveillance and chemotherapy are both unsuitable 〔JUA〕 〔EAU Strong〕
      ・RPLND is not recommended, and is considered only when both other strategies are contraindicated 〔ESMO II, D〕
      ・RPLND is an option in both risk groups at a high-volume centre 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ※ With somatic-type malignancy in the primary tumour, RPLND is preferred 〔JUA〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
    ※ Relapse on surveillance occurs in about 15% without and 50% with lymphovascular invasion 〔JUA〕 〔EAU〕
    next: Non-seminoma relapse after surveillance or one cycle of BEP
  Stage II non-seminoma
    branch: NS
    ① Raised markers: chemotherapy as for good- or intermediate-risk metastatic disease 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 1〕
    ※ Marker-negative stage IIA nodes are restaged after 6 weeks before treatment is chosen 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU Weak〕
    ② Normal markers, stage IIA: nerve-sparing RPLND in a specialised centre 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU Weak〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Three cycles of BEP or four cycles of EP are an equal option 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ③ Normal markers, stage IIB: chemotherapy as for good-risk metastatic disease 〔JUA〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・RPLND is for selected disease confined to the primary landing zone 〔JUA〕 〔NCCN〕
    ※ Pathological stage II after RPLND is followed by surveillance or by adjuvant chemotherapy of at most two cycles 〔JUA〕 〔EAU〕
      ・One cycle of BEP is appropriate 〔ESMO〕
      ・The regimen is two cycles of EP, and surveillance is preferred for pN1 〔NCCN〕
      ・Disease staged pN3 can receive BEP for 2 to 3 cycles or EP for 2 to 4 cycles 〔NCCN〕
    ※ Completely resected pure teratoma is followed without chemotherapy 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
    next: Residual mass after chemotherapy for non-seminoma | After chemotherapy
  Metastatic non-seminoma by IGCCCG group
    branch: NS
    ① Good risk: three cycles of BEP 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔EAU LE 1b〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕, or four cycles of EP 〔JUA〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
      ・EP is given when bleomycin is contraindicated or undesirable 〔JUA 2, C〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔EAU〕
    ~ de Wit et al.
    ~ GETUG T93BP
    ② Intermediate or poor risk: four cycles of BEP 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕, or four cycles of VIP with G-CSF when bleomycin is contraindicated 〔JUA 2, C〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU LE 1b〕 〔NCCN 1〕
    - ECOG E3887
    - de Wit 1998 {pmid 9743309}
    ③ Poor risk: marker decline is assessed after the first cycle, and intensified chemotherapy is considered after an unfavourable decline 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔EAU Weak〕
      ・Intensified first-line regimens, including dose-dense chemotherapy after an unfavourable marker decline, are not recommended 〔JUA 2, C〕
    ~ GETUG-13
    ※ Bleomycin could not be omitted from three cycles of BEP, or from BEP with etoposide 360 mg/m² per cycle, without loss of efficacy
      - Loehrer et al.
      - de Wit 1997 {pmid 9164193}
    ※ Carboplatin in place of cisplatin is inferior, and is used only in patients unfit for cisplatin 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕
      - MRC TE09/EORTC 30896
    ※ First-line high-dose chemotherapy has shown no survival benefit and is not recommended 〔JUA 2, C〕 〔EAU LE 1b〕
      - Motzer 2007 {pmid 17235042}
      - EORTC 30974 {pmid 21059637}
    ※ With extensive lung or liver metastases or poor general condition, a dose-reduced first cycle is given, followed by the full number of full-dose cycles 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕
    ※ Brain metastases at diagnosis are treated with chemotherapy as for poor-risk disease, with radiotherapy or surgery where indicated 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Stereotactic treatment is preferred to whole-brain irradiation 〔EAU〕
      ・The stereotactic limit is four metastases of 3 cm or less 〔JUA〕
      ・Upfront intensified treatment is an option, preferably within a study 〔EAU〕
    ※ Poor-risk disease is treated at a high-volume centre 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU LE 1b〕 〔NCCN〕
    next: Residual mass after chemotherapy for non-seminoma
  Seminoma relapse after surveillance or adjuvant treatment
    branch: SEM pale
    ① Relapse on surveillance: radiotherapy or chemotherapy by the stage at relapse, as for disease at presentation 〔NCCN〕
    ② Relapse after carboplatin or radiotherapy: cisplatin-based chemotherapy by stage 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
    ※ Relapse after carboplatin is mostly retroperitoneal or iliac, and can be late 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕
  Residual mass after chemotherapy for seminoma
    branch: SEM pale
    ① No residual mass, or a mass of 3 cm or less with normal markers: surveillance without PET 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ② Residual mass over 3 cm: FDG-PET at least 6 weeks after chemotherapy 〔JUA 2, B〕 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・The interval is at least 2 months 〔EAU LE 2b〕
    ※ A negative PET is followed by observation only 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
    ③ PET-positive residual mass: repeat PET before any further treatment 〔JUA〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
      ・The interval is 6 weeks 〔EAU〕, 6 to 12 weeks 〔NCCN〕 or 8 to 12 weeks 〔JUA〕
      ・Biopsy is recommended before radiotherapy or resection 〔ESMO〕
      ・Resection, or biopsy in selected patients, is the alternative 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ※ Most positive scans are false positive 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕
    ④ Growing mass or rising markers: second-line treatment 〔JUA〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Without an hCG rise, a growing mass is biopsied first 〔EAU〕
    ※ After complete resection of viable seminoma, two cycles of adjuvant chemotherapy can be considered 〔NCCN 2A〕
    next: First relapse after cisplatin-based chemotherapy
  Non-seminoma relapse after surveillance or one cycle of BEP
    branch: NS pale
    ① Chemotherapy by the IGCCCG group at relapse, as for disease at presentation 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
    ※ RPLND can be appropriate in selected patients 〔NCCN〕
    ※ Relapse on surveillance is marker negative in 15 to 35% of patients 〔JUA〕
    ※ Outcome after relapse following one cycle of BEP may be worse than for metastatic disease at presentation 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕
    ※ About one-third of relapses after one cycle of BEP are late relapses 〔EAU〕
  Residual mass after chemotherapy for non-seminoma
    branch: NS pale
    ① Residual mass over 1 cm with normal or normalising markers: resection of all residual masses 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔EAU Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ※ Bilateral nerve-sparing RPLND is the standard template 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・A modified template is an option in limited disease 〔JUA no recommendation, C〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
    ※ Residual lung metastases are resected as far as safely possible 〔JUA 2, B〕
    ※ FDG-PET has no role in residual non-seminoma 〔JUA〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
    ② Residual mass under 1 cm: surveillance 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
      ・Resection of all residual masses is favoured where feasible 〔JUA no recommendation, C〕
      ・RPLND is an option in selected patients 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN 2B〕
      ・After salvage chemotherapy, masses under 1 cm are resected as well 〔EAU〕
    ③ Necrosis or teratoma in the specimen: no further treatment 〔JUA〕 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ④ Viable cancer in the specimen: two further cycles of chemotherapy 〔JUA 2, C〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・After complete resection with under 10% viable cells, chemotherapy gives no benefit and surveillance follows 〔ESMO IV, C〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Chemotherapy is for incomplete resection or over 10% viable cells in intermediate- or poor-risk disease 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕
      ・No chemotherapy follows resection after second-line treatment 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN〕
    ※ When markers have not normalised at the end of first-line chemotherapy, consolidation with a salvage regimen such as TIP before confirmed progression is widely given in Japan 〔JUA〕
      ・Raised but stable markers are followed closely, and salvage chemotherapy starts only on documented marker progression 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Surgery is an option in highly selected patients with raised but stable markers 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ※ A growing teratoma with normalised markers is resected early 〔EAU〕
    next: First relapse after cisplatin-based chemotherapy
  First relapse after cisplatin-based chemotherapy
    branch: REL
    ① Conventional-dose chemotherapy: four cycles of TIP 〔JUA 2, C〕 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔EAU〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
      ・VeIP for four cycles is an equal option 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
      ・VIP for four cycles is an equal option 〔ESMO〕 〔EAU〕
      ・GIP for four cycles is a possible option 〔EAU〕
      ・Paclitaxel is given at 250 mg/m² over 24 hours or at 175 mg/m² over 3 hours 〔EAU〕
