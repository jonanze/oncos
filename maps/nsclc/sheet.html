BEFORE FIRST LINE:
Tissue NGS, plasma if tissue is short: EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF V600E, KRAS G12C, MET exon 14, RET, HER2, NTRK. PD-L1 TPS (22C3)
Checkpoint inhibitors do little in EGFR, ALK, RET and HER2 driven disease: response 12%, 0%, 6% and 7%, median PFS 2 to 3 months (IMMUNOTARGET). KRAS and BRAF respond as often as unselected disease

No driver: course of treatment

Platinum doublet + PD-1 antibody for 4 cycles, then the PD-1 antibody alone or with pemetrexed for up to 35 cycles, about 2 years. Median PFS 8.8 months with chemotherapy + pembrolizumab; 5-year OS 19.4% vs 11.3% (KEYNOTE-189)
Of those who finished 35 cycles, 82% were alive at 5 years in KEYNOTE-024; 3-year OS after the last cycle 72% in KEYNOTE-189
Non-squamous: pemetrexed continues with the PD-1 antibody. Pemetrexed alone after cisplatin + pemetrexed: PFS 4.1 vs 2.8 months (PARAMOUNT)

Second line, no driver

  1. ①After chemotherapy + PD-1: docetaxel, with ramucirumab if fit (REVEL: OS 10.5 vs 9.1 months, HR 0.86; febrile neutropenia 16% vs 10%)
  2. ②After chemotherapy without a PD-1 antibody: nivolumab (CheckMate 017 squamous, OS 9.2 vs 6.0 months; CheckMate 057 non-squamous, 12.2 vs 9.4 months), pembrolizumab if TPS ≥1% (KEYNOTE-010: OS HR 0.71), atezolizumab at any PD-L1 (OAK: OS 13.8 vs 9.6 months, HR 0.73)
  3. ③KRAS G12C after chemotherapy + PD-1: sotorasib (CodeBreaK 200: PFS 5.6 vs 4.5 months vs docetaxel, HR 0.66) or adagrasib (KRYSTAL-12: PFS 5.5 vs 3.8 months, HR 0.58)
    Liver toxicity is common when sotorasib follows a PD-1 antibody: grade 2 or higher in 76% of 21 patients, most stopped the drug (Chour 2024)
  4. ④Not standard: datopotamab deruxtecan (TROPION-Lung01: PFS 4.4 vs 3.7 months vs docetaxel, OS HR 0.94; squamous did worse, PFS HR 1.41); ramucirumab + pembrolizumab (Lung-MAP S1800A, phase 2: OS 14.5 vs 11.6 months)

Local treatment and bone

3 or fewer metastases, no progression after first line: radiotherapy or surgery to every site. OS 41.2 vs 17.0 months in 49 patients (Gomez); PFS 9.7 vs 3.5 months in 29 (Iyengar). Both phase 2, stopped early
1 to 3 brain metastases: radiosurgery alone. Cognitive decline at 3 months 64% vs 92% when whole brain radiotherapy is added; OS the same (Brown 2016). Osimertinib CNS response 91% vs 68% with older EGFR TKIs (FLAURA)
Bone metastases: denosumab 120 mg every 4 weeks non-inferior to zoledronic acid for first skeletal event, HR 0.84 (Henry 2011)

Driver positive: first line

  1. ①EGFR exon 19 deletion or L858R: osimertinib (FLAURA: PFS 18.9 vs 10.2 months; OS 38.6 vs 31.8 months, HR 0.80)
    Osimertinib + platinum + pemetrexed (FLAURA2): PFS HR 0.62; OS 47.5 vs 37.6 months, HR 0.77; grade 3 or higher events 70% vs 34%
    Amivantamab + lazertinib (MARIPOSA): PFS 23.7 vs 16.6 months, HR 0.70; OS HR 0.75, 3-year OS 60% vs 51%; grade 3 or higher events 80% vs 52%: skin, venous thromboembolism, infusion reactions
    After osimertinib: platinum + pemetrexed. Amivantamab added gives PFS 6.3 vs 4.2 months (MARIPOSA-2). Pembrolizumab added gives nothing (KEYNOTE-789: OS HR 0.84, not significant)
  2. ②EGFR exon 20 insertion: amivantamab + carboplatin + pemetrexed (PAPILLON: PFS 11.4 vs 6.7 months, HR 0.40)
  3. ③ALK: alectinib (ALEX: PFS 34.8 vs 10.9 months; 5-year OS 62.5% vs 45.5%), brigatinib (ALTA-1L: PFS HR 0.49), lorlatinib (CROWN: 5-year PFS 60% vs 8%, HR 0.19; intracranial response 82% vs 23%)
    Lorlatinib: raised cholesterol 81%, triglycerides 60% (phase 2); grade 3–4 events 72% vs 56% in CROWN, mostly lipids
  4. ④ROS1: crizotinib (Shaw 2014: response 72%, PFS 19.2 months), entrectinib (Drilon 2020: response 77%, reaches the CNS), repotrectinib (TRIDENT-1: response 79%, PFS 35.7 months untreated; 38% after one ROS1 TKI; dizziness 58%)
  5. ⑤BRAF V600E: dabrafenib + trametinib (Planchard 2017: response 64%; pyrexia), encorafenib + binimetinib (PHAROS: response 75% untreated, 46% treated)
  6. ⑥MET exon 14 skipping: capmatinib (GEOMETRY: response 68% untreated, 41% treated; peripheral oedema 51%), tepotinib (VISION: response 46%)
  7. ⑦RET fusion: selpercatinib (LIBRETTO-431: PFS 24.8 vs 11.2 months against chemotherapy with or without pembrolizumab, HR 0.46; CNS progression HR 0.28) grade 3 hypertension 14%, raised ALT 12% (LIBRETTO-001)
  8. ⑧KRAS G12C: chemotherapy + PD-1 first; sotorasib or adagrasib at progression, see ③ at left (KRYSTAL-1: response 43%, PFS 6.5 months)
  9. ⑨HER2 mutation: trastuzumab deruxtecan 5.4 mg/kg after chemotherapy (DESTINY-Lung02: response 49%; interstitial lung disease 13%, 28% at 6.4 mg/kg)
  10. ⑩NTRK fusion: larotrectinib (Drilon 2018: response 75% across tumour types)
