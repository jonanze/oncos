# How to read and edit this file
#   Indent 2 spaces per level.
#   tree:   the decision tree; each plain line is a node, its children are indented under it
#   band:   a block above the tree for the whole sheet
#   panel:  a block beside the tree, joined by a brace to the node named after ">"
#   = text          the treatment for a branch
#   + Trial | fig   OS benefit         ~ Trial | fig   surrogate only (PFS, response)
#   - Trial | fig   no benefit or harm * Trial | fig   no comparison drawn
#   Trial [t-...]   picks the Evidence row by its anchor when the name is not enough
#   Trial {pmid N}  a trial with no Evidence row yet: PubMed link, listed for intake
#   ※ text          caveat             ° text          side effects, grey
#   ① text          numbered item; plain lines in a band or panel are text

map: Advanced NSCLC

band: Before first line
  Tissue NGS, plasma if tissue is short: EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF V600E, KRAS G12C, MET exon 14, RET, HER2, NTRK. PD-L1 TPS (22C3)
  ※ Checkpoint inhibitors do little in EGFR, ALK, RET and HER2 driven disease; KRAS and BRAF respond as often as unselected disease
    * IMMUNOTARGET {pmid 31125062} | response 12%, 0%, 6%, 7%
  ※ EGFR mutation, not clinical selection, predicts benefit from an EGFR TKI
    ~ IPASS | PFS HR 0.48 mutated, 2.85 not mutated

band: Local treatment and bone
  ① 3 or fewer metastases, no progression after first line: radiotherapy or surgery to every site
    + Gomez {pmid 27789196} | OS 41.2 vs 17.0 months
    ~ Iyengar {pmid 28973074} | PFS 9.7 vs 3.5 months
    ※ Both phase 2, stopped early
    ※ With osimertinib, radiotherapy to the sites left after 8–10 weeks: single-arm
      ~ Sampath 2025 [t-sampath-et-al] | PFS 32.3 months
  ② 1 to 3 brain metastases: radiosurgery alone
    * Brown 2016 {pmid 27458945} | cognitive decline 63.5% vs 91.7%
  ③ Bone metastases: denosumab 120 mg every 4 weeks
    ~ Henry 2011 {pmid 21343556} | first skeletal event HR 0.84
  ④ Up to 5 sites progressing on systemic therapy: radiotherapy to them, systemic therapy continued
    ~ CURB | NSCLC PFS 2.2 vs 10.0 months
