# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
# Scope (Jonan 2026-11-15): the systemic course by line (first line, second line, third line and later), with two panels:
#   brain metastases, and endocrine-containing therapy in HR-positive HER2-positive disease. Bone-modifying agents, surgery
#   of the primary tumour in de novo stage IV disease and oligometastatic disease are out (they are on the HR+ HER2- MBC map)
# Layout: the course carries "| parallel". Two presentations start side by side: first line in de novo disease or late
#   relapse, and relapse during or within 12 months of neoadjuvant or adjuvant anti-HER2 therapy. First line leads to maintenance
#   after induction chemotherapy, a later step of the same branch (pale tint). Both lanes lead into second line, then third
#   line and later. No header step and no line labels. The HR-positive panel holds endocrine therapy without chemotherapy,
#   and the maintenance step carries HR-positive maintenance. Neither panel is joined to a step.
#   Biopsy and HER2 status, the rules for every line and toxicity monitoring are bands
# One narrative: a point several guidelines agree on is one statement with all their tags. A ・ line gives a real
#   difference (an option, an order, a population, a cut-off, a grade), a condition or a licence. One numbered line per population
# Guideline set (Jonan 2026-11-15): PAGA 2023, ESMO 2026 living, NCCN 9.2026, ASCO 2022 and ABC8 2026, all full members.
#   Tag order PAGA, ESMO, NCCN, ASCO, ABC8 (East Asian guideline first). No JBCS
# Tags give the grade as printed, evidence first and strength second, on the statement the guideline graded:
#   〔PAGA level, grade〕 Pan-Asian adapted ESMO guideline for metastatic breast cancer 2023 (Im, ESMO Open 2023;8:101541),
#     adapted from ESMO 2021. A bare 〔PAGA〕 marks body text, a figure footnote or an ungraded Table 1 row
#   〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO metastatic breast cancer guideline 2026 (de Azambuja, Ann Oncol 2026;37:1203-1219), read
#     from the Living Guideline v2.0 pages (August 2026, first-line pages edited 12 October 2026). A bare 〔ESMO〕 marks an
#     algorithm footnote, a printed order or preference label, a licence note or an ESMO-MCBS score. For brain metastases
#     the grades of the HER2-positive brain metastases page are used, not those of the line algorithms
#   〔NCCN category〕 NCCN Breast Cancer 9.2026 (22 October 2026), with "preferred" as printed. A bare 〔NCCN〕 marks a
#     footnote, a principles page or the Discussion. On the brain metastases panel the tag is NCCN Central Nervous System
#     Cancers 4.2026 (2 November 2026), to which NCCN Breast defers (Jonan 2026-11-15), except the imaging lines, which
#     rest on NCCN Breast
#   〔ASCO quality, strength〕 ASCO 2022 updates on systemic therapy (Giordano, J Clin Oncol 2022;40:2612-2635) and on brain
#     metastases (Ramakrishna, J Clin Oncol 2022;40:2636-2655) in HER2-positive advanced breast cancer. A bare 〔ASCO〕 marks
#     the Discussion, a qualifying statement, an algorithm or the abstract. The source link is the systemic therapy paper
#   〔ASCO-CAP〕 ASCO-CAP HER2 testing guideline update 2023 (Wolff, J Clin Oncol 2023;41:3867-3872), read in full from
#     Jonan's PDF (2026-11-16). It affirms and reprints the 2018 recommendations (Table A2, Fig 1) and adds the reporting
#     comment on IHC 0 versus 1+. Ungraded, so the tag is bare. The HER2 definition lines are the same on the HER2+ EBC
#     and HER2+ MBC maps (Jonan 2026-11-16), with each map's own guideline tags
#   〔ABC8 level/grade〕 ABC8 international consensus 2026 (Cardoso, Breast 2026;90:104921), LoE/GoR as printed, without
#     the consensus percentage. A bare 〔ABC8〕 marks a figure label, a narrative statement or a sentence in an ungraded cell
# Trial marks: + overall survival benefit, ~ surrogate endpoint, non-inferiority or single-arm, - no benefit from the
#   tested change, * no comparison for the statement
# Single-arm exceptions (inclusion rule ①, as LORIN on the early NSCLC map and APT on the HER2+ EBC map): PERUSE, since the
#   choice of paclitaxel or nab-paclitaxel with trastuzumab and pertuzumab rests on it and no randomised trial compares the
#   taxanes, and DESTINY-Breast12, since trastuzumab deruxtecan for active brain metastases rests on it and no randomised
#   trial covers active brain metastases. DESTINY-Breast01 is under off: because DESTINY-Breast02 is the randomised trial
#   in the same setting
# China-only drugs (Jonan 2026-11-15): anbenitamab with HB1801, trastuzumab botidotin, trastuzumab rezetecan and pyrotinib
#   stay on ※ lines that say the trial ran in China and that the drug has no EU or US licence
# PRECIOUS sits on a ※ line beside the ABC8 statement against dual blockade beyond progression, as the trial that
#   conflicts with it (header rule on a trial and a guideline that disagree)

band: Biopsy and HER2 status
  ① A metastatic lesion is biopsied at the first diagnosis of metastatic disease, to confirm histology and reassess ER, PgR and HER2 〔PAGA I, B〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕 〔ABC8 I/B〕
    ・When the receptor status of the metastasis differs from that of the primary tumour, treatment follows the metastasis 〔PAGA〕 〔ESMO V, B〕
    ・Anti-HER2 therapy is considered when HER2 is positive in at least one biopsy 〔ABC8 Expert opinion/B〕
  ② HER2 is positive with an IHC score of 3+, or with dual-probe in situ hybridisation showing a HER2/CEP17 ratio of 2.0 or more and an average HER2 copy number of 4.0 or more signals per cell 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕 〔ABC8 Expert opinion/A〕
    ・An IHC score of 3+ is complete, intense circumferential membrane staining in more than 10% of tumour cells 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
    ・An IHC score of 2+ is weak to moderate complete membrane staining in more than 10% of tumour cells. It is equivocal and is resolved by in situ hybridisation on the same or a new specimen 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
    ・A ratio of 2.0 or more with a copy number below 4.0 (group 2), or a ratio below 2.0 with a copy number of 4.0 to below 6.0 (group 4), is positive only with IHC 3+ on the same tissue 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
    ・A ratio below 2.0 with a copy number of 6.0 or more (group 3) is positive with IHC 3+, or with IHC 2+ when a blinded recount confirms group 3 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
    ・With IHC 2+ in groups 2 to 4, an additional observer, blinded to the first count, recounts at least 20 cells in the area of IHC 2+ staining 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
    ・IHC 0 and 1+ are both HER2-negative. The IHC score is always reported, because IHC 1+, or 2+ without amplification, gives eligibility for trastuzumab deruxtecan 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
    ・HER2-low (IHC 1+, or 2+ without amplification) is named as a category in the report 〔ABC8 Expert opinion/A〕, whereas a HER2-low category is judged premature and the report keeps the HER2-negative result with a comment on eligibility 〔ASCO-CAP〕
  ※ ER-low disease (1 to 10% of cells staining) is eligible for endocrine therapy and often behaves like ER-negative disease 〔NCCN〕
band: Anti-HER2 therapy at every line
  ① Anti-HER2 therapy is part of first-line treatment for every patient without a contraindication 〔ASCO High, Strong〕 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・With clinical heart failure or a markedly reduced LVEF, the use of anti-HER2 therapy is individualised 〔ASCO〕
  + H0648g {pmid 11248153}
  ② After progression, anti-HER2 therapy continues in each further line 〔PAGA〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・The regimen at each line is chosen according to previous anti-HER2 therapy, the disease-free interval and availability 〔NCCN〕 〔ABC8 I/A〕
  ③ An approved biosimilar can replace trastuzumab 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO〕 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・Subcutaneous trastuzumab, alone or with pertuzumab, can replace intravenous trastuzumab and pertuzumab, and is no substitute for trastuzumab emtansine or trastuzumab deruxtecan 〔NCCN〕
    ・The data are insufficient for a recommendation on the subcutaneous route 〔ASCO〕
  ④ Older patients are offered anti-HER2 therapy unless they have an absolute cardiac contraindication 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・Frail older patients may need a lower starting dose of a tyrosine kinase inhibitor or an antibody-drug conjugate 〔ABC8 Expert opinion/A〕
  ※ Anti-HER2 drugs are contraindicated throughout pregnancy 〔NCCN〕 〔ABC8 Expert opinion/A〕
band: Monitoring and toxicity
  ① Interstitial lung disease with trastuzumab deruxtecan is monitored regularly 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO〕, including by CT 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・At grade 1, with radiological changes only, trastuzumab deruxtecan is withheld and prednisone at 0.5 mg/kg or more is considered 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・After grade 1, trastuzumab deruxtecan restarts at the same dose when the changes resolve within 28 days, and one dose level lower when they resolve later 〔ABC8 I/A〕
    ・At grade 2 or higher, trastuzumab deruxtecan is stopped permanently, and prednisone at 1 mg/kg or more is started promptly and tapered over at least 4 weeks 〔ABC8 I/A〕
  ② Cardiac function is monitored during and after anti-HER2 therapy 〔NCCN〕
    ・Trastuzumab and pertuzumab are not given concurrently with an anthracycline 〔NCCN〕
  ° Toxicity of note: nausea and vomiting with trastuzumab deruxtecan, which need prophylaxis 〔ABC8 I/A〕
  ° Toxicity of note: diarrhoea with tucatinib, trastuzumab and capecitabine, managed by patient education and early intervention 〔ABC8 I/A〕
