# oncOS board map: Ovarian Cancer (epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube and primary peritoneal carcinoma)
# 2026-11-13, approved by Jonan. Adjuvant regimen and cycle number are their own step. Recurrence named platinum-sensitive and platinum-resistant
#   Structure plan: board-maps/ovarian/structure-plan.md
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
# Scope (Jonan 2026-11-13): one map for the whole course of the sheet. Newly diagnosed disease, then recurrence, with
#   low-grade serous carcinoma as its own block. Germ cell, sex cord-stromal and borderline tumours have no rows on the
#   sheet and are left out
# One narrative: a point several guidelines agree on is one statement with all their tags. A ・ line gives a difference
#   only where guidelines disagree (a cut-off, a licence, a grade, a recommendation against) or a condition
# Options NCCN alone lists sit on ※ lines or as ・ lines in their drug class (inclusion.txt, Jonan 2026-11-10): olaparib
#   alone in HRD-positive disease, pembrolizumab with paclitaxel, avutometinib with defactinib, tumour-agnostic agents
# Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded. East Asian guidelines first:
#   〔JSGO evidence, strength〕 JSGO 2025, 6th edition (Shoji, J Gynecol Oncol 2026;37(6):e150, provisional PDF, published
#     online 11 September 2026, literature to December 2023). Minds 2020, a modified GRADE. Evidence A to D, strength
#     1 strong or 2 weak. "against" marks a recommendation printed with a down arrow. The English article prints no
#     doses, no trial references and no drug-specific Japanese insurance detail. A bare 〔JSGO〕 marks a statement from
#     its algorithm figures or chapter remarks, which carry no grade
#   〔KSGO-5.0 strength〕 KSGO 5.0 (Lee, J Gynecol Oncol 2024;35:e43, December 2023, search October 2022). GRADE. Quality
#     is printed per outcome, so the tag gives strength only: Strong or Conditional. Four key questions
#   〔KSGO-5.1 level, grade〕 KSGO 5.1 (Lee, J Gynecol Oncol 2025;36:e69, data 2023 to 2024). Adapted IDSA-USPHS: levels
#     I to V, grades A to E. Five new key questions
#   〔PAGA level, grade〕 Pan-Asian adapted ESMO guideline (Nag, ESMO Open 2025;10:105125, online 9 June 2025, consensus
#     meeting 6 July 2024). Table 1 and Figures 1 to 3 read. Adapted IDSA-USPHS: levels I to V, grades A to E. Every
#     statement had 100% consensus. A bare 〔PAGA〕 marks figure or discussion text
#   〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO 2023 (González-Martín, Ann Oncol 2023;34:833-848, last update July 2023) with the Express
#     Update of 28 January 2026 (ESMO Open 2026;11:106032, doi 10.1016/j.esmoop.2025.106032), one tag. Adapted
#     IDSA-USPHS: levels I to V, grades A to E, with ESMO-MCBS. The update adds rucaparib first line, regrades
#     maintenance for HRD-negative tumours and adds mirvetuximab. A bare 〔ESMO〕 marks ungraded text
#   〔ESGO level, grade〕 ESGO-ESMO-ESP consensus conference (Ledermann, Ann Oncol 2024;35:248-266, conference June 2022,
#     data to 14 June 2022, online 1 February 2024). Adapted IDSA-USPHS: levels I to V, grades A to E. Used only where
#     it adds a point or a grade the ESMO guideline lacks (Jonan 2026-11-13)
#   〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Ovarian Cancer 4.2026 (10 April 2026). Categories 1, 2A, 2B and 3, with preferred,
#     other recommended and useful in certain circumstances. 2A unless stated
#   〔ASCO-NACT quality, strength〕 ASCO neoadjuvant chemotherapy update (Gaillard, J Clin Oncol 2025;43:868-891, published
#     22 January 2025, literature to September 2024). ASCO GRADE scheme: evidence High to Very low, Strong or Conditional
#   〔ASCO-PARP quality, strength〕 ASCO PARP inhibitor living guideline 2026.1.0 (Tew, J Clin Oncol 2026, accepted 20 October
#     2026, unedited manuscript, literature to July 2026). ASCO GRADE scheme. Follows US licences
#   〔ASCO-REC quality, strength〕 ASCO recurrence living guideline 2026.1.0 (Lesnock, J Clin Oncol 2026;44:2725-2744,
#     published 13 July 2026, literature to November 2025). ASCO GRADE scheme. A bare ASCO tag marks narrative text
# Figure and text mismatches in ESMO 2023 and PAGA, text grade used: recurrence assessment is [I-III, A] in the text and
#   [I-II, A] in Figure 3. Bevacizumab after interval surgery is [II, A] in Figure 2 only, the text grades bevacizumab
#   around interval surgery [II, B]. The map carries the neoadjuvant statement at [II, B] and no post-surgery grade
# Written before the trial: JSGO 2025 names none of MIRASOL, ROSELLA, KEYNOTE-B96, ATHENA-MONO, DUO-O or KEYLYNK-001.
#   KSGO 5.0 predates MIRASOL, OReO and every later trial. KSGO 5.1, PAGA and ESMO predate ROSELLA, KEYNOTE-B96, DUO-O,
#   KEYLYNK-001 and RAMP 201. ESGO predates MIRASOL, OReO, PRIMA and PAOLA-1 final survival and SOLO-1 at 7 years.
#   ASCO-NACT does not mention DUO-O or LION. ASCO-REC names KEYNOTE-B96 and RAMP 201 without a grade. NCCN 4.2026 does
#   not mention DUO-O, KEYLYNK-001, OReO, DESKTOP III or SOC-1
# Licensing is taken from the guideline texts, labels not read. Rucaparib first line: EU licence, no US licence, not
#   approved in Japan or Korea, not available in Singapore (PAGA, ASCO-PARP). PARP inhibitor maintenance at recurrence:
#   US licences restricted to BRCA1/2 mutations, EU licences unchanged for all three drugs (ESMO, NCCN, ASCO-PARP). PARP
#   inhibitor treatment of recurrence: US licences withdrawn 2022 to 2023 (ASCO-PARP). Repeat PARP inhibitor: no licence
#   (ESMO). Repeat bevacizumab: not licensed in Europe (ESMO). Mirvetuximab: EU and US licences (PAGA). Trastuzumab
#   deruxtecan: US accelerated approval for HER2 IHC 3+ solid tumours (KSGO 5.1). Pembrolizumab with paclitaxel: US
#   licence of 10 February 2026 (ASCO-REC). Avutometinib with defactinib: US accelerated approval (ASCO-REC). Trametinib:
#   no EU or US licence for low-grade serous carcinoma (PAGA). Trabectedin: EU licence, no US licence (ESMO). Irinotecan:
#   licensed in Japan for platinum-resistant disease (PAGA). Relacorilant: no licence statement in any guideline read.
#   Japanese and Singapore status of mirvetuximab, relacorilant, pembrolizumab, trastuzumab deruxtecan and avutometinib
#   is not stated in the guidelines and was not checked
# Trial marks: + OS benefit, ~ surrogate endpoint, non-inferiority or inconclusive, - no benefit or harm. Marks follow
#   the Evidence row or intake paper as it stands. Final reports filed 2026-11-13: JCOG0602 -, ROSELLA +, ARIEL4 - (OS favoured chemotherapy)
#   (JCOG0602, SOLO-1, PRIMA, PAOLA-1, ROSELLA, SOLO3, ARIEL4) for the editor

band: Diagnosis and molecular testing
  ① CA-125, pelvic ultrasound by an expert examiner, and CT of the thorax, abdomen and pelvis 〔PAGA III, A〕 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔ASCO-NACT Moderate, Strong〕
    ・Mucinous tumours: CEA, CA 19-9 and endoscopy of the upper and lower gastrointestinal tract 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ② Assessment by a gynaecological oncologist before treatment 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ASCO-NACT Moderate, Strong〕
  ③ Pathological diagnosis by the WHO 2020 classification, by an expert gynaecological pathologist 〔PAGA IV, A〕 〔ESMO IV, A〕
  ④ Minimum immunohistochemistry of PAX8, ER, WT-1 and p53, with low-grade morphology and aberrant p53 classified as high-grade serous carcinoma 〔ESGO IV, A〕
  ⑤ Germline and somatic BRCA1/2 testing at diagnosis of high-grade non-mucinous carcinoma 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ESGO I, A〕
    ・In every epithelial ovarian cancer 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ASCO-NACT High, Strong〕 〔ASCO-PARP High, Strong〕
  ⑥ HRD testing in stage III–IV high-grade carcinoma before first-line maintenance 〔JSGO B, 1〕 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
    ・Reflex testing when no BRCA1/2 mutation is found 〔ESGO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ASCO-PARP High, Strong〕
    ・A test validated in a phase III trial is preferred 〔ESGO I, A〕 〔ASCO-PARP High, Strong〕
    ・HRD-positive: a BRCA1/2 mutation or a genomic instability score of 42 or more by Myriad myChoice, or genomic loss of heterozygosity of 16% or more by FoundationOne CDx 〔ASCO-PARP〕
  ⑦ Mismatch repair or MSI testing in endometrioid and clear cell carcinoma 〔ESGO II, A〕
    ・All endometrioid carcinoma 〔NCCN 2A〕

band: PARP inhibitor maintenance, first line
  ① Started after a complete or partial response, or no evidence of disease, at the end of platinum chemotherapy 〔KSGO-5.0 Strong〕 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔ESGO I, A〕 〔ASCO-PARP Moderate, Strong〕
  ② Stable disease after chemotherapy with bevacizumab: bevacizumab maintenance 〔JSGO B, 1〕 〔PAGA〕 〔ESMO〕
  ③ Olaparib 300 mg twice daily, rucaparib 600 mg twice daily, niraparib 200 or 300 mg once daily 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Niraparib 200 mg when weight is below 77 kg or platelets are below 150,000/µL (150 × 10⁹/L) 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Olaparib with bevacizumab is given only when bevacizumab was started with chemotherapy 〔PAGA〕 〔ESMO〕
  ④ Dose interruption or reduction is preferred to early discontinuation for chronic low-grade toxicity 〔ASCO-PARP Low, Strong〕
  ° Toxicity of note: anaemia, thrombocytopenia and hypertension with niraparib, and myelodysplastic syndrome or acute myeloid leukaemia
    ・Permanent discontinuation for myelodysplastic syndrome, acute myeloid leukaemia or pneumonitis 〔ASCO-PARP High, Strong〕

band: Follow-up and supportive care
  ① Visits every 2 to 4 months for 2 years, then every 3 to 6 months to 5 years 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Every 1 to 3 months in the first 2 years 〔JSGO C, 2〕
  ② CA-125, examination, and CT when clinically indicated 〔JSGO C, 2〕 〔PAGA IV, B〕 〔ESMO IV, B〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ③ Hormone replacement therapy for surgical menopause 〔JSGO B, 1〕
    ・High-grade serous carcinoma only, avoided in low-grade serous and endometrioid carcinoma 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ④ BRCA1/2 carriers: follow-up beyond 5 years 〔PAGA III, B〕 〔ESMO III, B〕
  ⑤ Referral of BRCA1/2 carriers to a high-risk breast clinic 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
  ⑥ Multidisciplinary support to prevent associated cancers in hereditary cancer syndromes 〔JSGO B, 1〕
  ⑦ Malignant bowel obstruction: palliative surgery in selected patients 〔JSGO C, 2〕 〔ESGO IV, B〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Corticosteroids, octreotide or both 〔JSGO C, 2〕
