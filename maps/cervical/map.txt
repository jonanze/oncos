# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
# Scope (thread default 2026-11-18): one map for the whole sheet. Three parallel stages, never chained: early disease
#   (FIGO 2018 stage IA1 to IB2 and IIA1) with the step after hysterectomy, locally advanced disease (IB3 to IVA), and
#   recurrent or metastatic disease. The third stage has two first boxes in one colour: local treatment (pelvic
#   recurrence, few metastases, palliative radiotherapy) and first-line systemic treatment, which leads to the later
#   lines. Lines run only inside a stage, in time order. Three panels: fertility-sparing surgery and sentinel node
#   biopsy beside the early box, and the risk groups beside the step after hysterectomy. The FIGO 2018 stage groups,
#   the tests and the regimens are bands. No scale: grid, as the adjuvant decision has three groups and the criteria
#   read as numbered lines. Screening, vaccination, neuroendocrine carcinoma, pregnancy, radiotherapy planning,
#   imaging protocols and follow-up schedules are left out
# Guideline set (Jonan's card 2026-11-18) and tag forms, grade as printed, on the statement the guideline graded. Tag
#   order on every line: JSGO, ESMO, NCCN (East Asian guidelines first)
#   〔JSGO evidence, strength〕 Japan Society of Gynecologic Oncology 2022 guidelines for uterine cervical neoplasm
#     treatment (English version: Seino, J Gynecol Oncol 2024;35:e15, PMID 38037547), with the published Japanese
#     updates of CQ19 (drug with chemoradiotherapy), CQ21 (stage IVB) and CQ29 (chemotherapy for recurrence), which
#     replace those three CQs. Evidence A to D, strength 1 recommend or 2 suggest, "against" for a recommendation not
#     to. A bare 〔JSGO〕 marks the explanatory text of an update or a figure footnote. The 2022 search closed in
#     December 2020, before SHAPE, INTERLACE, OUTBACK and GOG-263. The updates predate the BEATcc and innovaTV 301
#     results. Supplementary Table S3 (the JSGO risk groups after surgery) is unread
#   〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO Clinical Practice Guideline for cervical cancer 2026 (Lorusso, ESMO Open 2026;11:108364,
#     PMID 42914498). A bare 〔ESMO〕 marks the narrative, a figure footnote or an ungraded statement. ESMO-MCBS scores
#     are left off the map, as on other maps
#   〔NCCN category〕 NCCN Cervical Cancer 1.2027 (15 October 2026), algorithm pages. 2A unless stated. On the systemic
#     therapy page the preference label is printed too. A bare 〔NCCN〕 marks a footnote, a table or a principles page.
#     The Discussion is older than the algorithm and is not used where they differ
# Grades by stage in locally advanced disease (review M1): NCCN category 1 on chemoradiotherapy is printed for stage
#   IB3 and IIA2 only. JSGO grade A, 1 is for stage III to IVA, and B, 1 for IB3, IIA2 and IIB
# Option order where guidelines disagree (Jonan's card answers 2026-11-19 where marked, otherwise defaults):
#   1. First line, CPS below 1 (Jonan 2026-11-19): bevacizumab with chemotherapy first (all three guidelines), then pembrolizumab with
#      chemotherapy (JSGO, Japan licence regardless of CPS). Atezolizumab with bevacizumab and chemotherapy (BEATcc)
#      is the ※ line directly below (NCCN alone lists it, rule ⑦). No EU, US or Japanese licence for atezolizumab in
#      cervical cancer was found on a web search, 2026-11-19
#   2. Second line without an earlier checkpoint inhibitor (Jonan 2026-11-19): tisotumab vedotin, then pembrolizumab,
#      then cemiplimab (NCCN order)
#   3. Stage IB3 and IIA2 (Jonan 2026-11-19): chemoradiotherapy first, radical hysterectomy second on the same line
#   4. Intermediate risk after hysterectomy: radiotherapy first, chemoradiotherapy second (JSGO alone)
#   5. Cadonilimab is a ※ line (trial in China only). Ipilimumab with nivolumab, nivolumab, pembrolizumab with
#      tisotumab vedotin and neratinib are ※ and ・ lines (NCCN alone, rule ⑦). The NTRK and RET drugs are a numbered
#      line, as each holds a tumour-agnostic FDA licence (review M6)
# Doses in the Regimens band follow the trial protocols, as the guidelines print almost none
# Trial marks (not drawn): + overall survival benefit, ~ surrogate endpoint or single-arm data, - primary endpoint not
#   met, no benefit or harm. LACC, GOG 204, GOG 71 and CALLA are -. SHAPE, JCOG0505 and SENTICOL-2 are ~ (their
#   non-inferiority mark is a question across maps, review L9)
# Single-arm trials cited because a recommendation or licence rests on them and no randomised trial of the option
#   exists in that setting (inclusion.txt ①, Jonan to confirm): KEYNOTE-158 (pembrolizumab after chemotherapy, and its
#   MSI-H and TMB analyses on the Tumour-Agnostic Basket Trials sheet), DESTINY-PanTumor02 (same sheet) and
#   CheckMate 358 (ipilimumab with nivolumab, randomised between two regimens only)
# Chips with the row anchor beside the PMID: SWOG 8797 and GOG 92, whose rows have an empty PMID field (editor fix:
#   10764420 and 10329031), and DESTINY-PanTumor02, whose paper is filed on two sheets
# Not yet on the sheet (intake.txt): GOG-263, Gupta et al. (Tata Memorial), EORTC 55994, SENTICOL-2, Landoni 1997,
#   GOG 71 and CALLA (inclusion ⑥d: durvalumab is licensed in endometrial cancer). RTOG 0724 has no PubMed record,
#   so its statement is plain text with no chip

map: Cervical Cancer

branch: EARLY | Early disease | blue

branch: RM | Recurrent or metastatic disease | rose

band: FIGO 2018 stage groups
  ① Stage IA1 has stromal invasion of 3 mm or less, and stage IA2 has more than 3 mm and up to 5 mm 〔NCCN〕
    ・Lymphovascular space invasion (LVSI) does not change the stage 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
  ② Stage IB is confined to the cervix with invasion deeper than 5 mm: IB1 is 2 cm or less, IB2 is more than 2 cm and up to 4 cm, and IB3 is more than 4 cm 〔NCCN〕
    ・A tumour of exactly 2 cm falls in IB2 and one of exactly 4 cm in IB3 〔ESMO〕
  ③ Stage IIA involves the upper two-thirds of the vagina without parametrial invasion: IIA1 is 4 cm or less and IIA2 more than 4 cm 〔NCCN〕
  ④ Stage IIB involves the parametrium short of the pelvic wall 〔NCCN〕
  ⑤ Stage IIIA involves the lower third of the vagina, and stage IIIB reaches the pelvic wall or causes hydronephrosis or a non-functioning kidney 〔NCCN〕
  ⑥ Stage IIIC1 has pelvic nodal metastases and stage IIIC2 para-aortic nodal metastases, whatever the size and extent of the tumour 〔NCCN〕
    ・Nodal stage is assigned on imaging (r) or pathology (p) 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・Micrometastases count as nodal metastases, and isolated tumour cells do not 〔NCCN〕
    ・Node-positive stage IB2 to IIB by FIGO 2014 is stage IIIC by FIGO 2018 〔JSGO〕
  ⑦ Stage IVA invades the mucosa of the bladder or rectum, and stage IVB has distant metastases 〔NCCN〕

band: Histology and biomarker tests
  ① PD-L1 testing by combined positive score (CPS) in recurrent, progressive or metastatic disease 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ② HER2 immunohistochemistry in advanced, recurrent or metastatic disease 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ③ Multigene panel testing in advanced or recurrent disease, covering at least HER2, MMR or MSI, TMB, NTRK and RET 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Genomic profiling is suggested once standard treatment is completed or expected to be completed 〔JSGO D, 2〕
    ・TMB-high is 10 or more mutations per megabase 〔NCCN〕
  ④ HPV status in every cervical adenocarcinoma 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Gastric-type adenocarcinoma is the most common HPV-independent type 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ・Gastric-type adenocarcinoma has a poor prognosis 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕

band: Regimens
  ① Chemoradiotherapy: cisplatin 40 mg/m² once a week during external-beam radiotherapy, for five or six cycles 〔JSGO〕
    ・Carboplatin replaces cisplatin when cisplatin is not tolerated 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
    ・Carboplatin or nedaplatin is considered when renal function is impaired 〔JSGO〕
  ② Pembrolizumab with chemoradiotherapy: 200 mg every 3 weeks for five cycles, then 400 mg every 6 weeks for 15 cycles
  ③ Induction before chemoradiotherapy: carboplatin AUC 2 with paclitaxel 80 mg/m² once a week for 6 weeks
  ④ Cisplatin with paclitaxel: paclitaxel 175 mg/m² over 3 hours and cisplatin 50 mg/m² on day 1, every 3 weeks
  ⑤ Carboplatin with paclitaxel: paclitaxel 175 mg/m² over 3 hours and carboplatin AUC 5 on day 1, every 3 weeks
  ⑥ Additions to platinum and paclitaxel, all every 3 weeks: pembrolizumab 200 mg for up to 35 cycles, bevacizumab 15 mg/kg, or atezolizumab 1200 mg with bevacizumab
  ⑦ Tisotumab vedotin: 2.0 mg/kg, up to 200 mg, every 3 weeks

course: Cervical cancer | parallel
  Early disease, stage IA1 to IB2 and IIA1: local treatment
    branch: EARLY
    ① Stage IA1 without LVSI: simple hysterectomy 〔JSGO B, 1〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Nodal staging is not required 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
    ② Stage IA1 with LVSI: simple hysterectomy with sentinel node biopsy 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕, or simple or modified radical hysterectomy with pelvic lymph node dissection 〔JSGO C, 2〕
    ③ Stage IA2 to IB1 within the conservative-surgery criteria: simple hysterectomy with sentinel node biopsy 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・The criteria are a tumour of 2 cm or less, stromal invasion under 10 mm on the cone or under 50% on MRI, and no metastasis on imaging 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Squamous cell carcinoma, adenocarcinoma and adenosquamous carcinoma qualify, with or without LVSI 〔ESMO〕
      ・Tumours without LVSI and usual-type adenocarcinoma of grade 1 or 2 are preferred 〔NCCN〕
      ・Simple hysterectomy is performed by an open approach 〔ESMO I, A〕
      ・A vaginal, open or minimally invasive route is accepted 〔NCCN〕
      ・In stage IA2, modified radical hysterectomy with pelvic lymph node dissection is suggested, a grade set before SHAPE was reported 〔JSGO C, 2〕
    ~ SHAPE {pmid 38416430}
    ④ Stage IA2 to IB1 outside the criteria, stage IB2 and stage IIA1: radical hysterectomy with nodal staging 〔JSGO B, 1〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕, or chemoradiotherapy with brachytherapy 〔JSGO B, 1〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Open radical hysterectomy is the standard approach 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕
      ・Minimally invasive surgery with measures against tumour spread is an option for tumours of 2 cm or less 〔JSGO C, 2〕
      ・Radiotherapy with brachytherapy, with or without chemotherapy, is for patients unsuitable for surgery 〔ESMO III, B〕
      ・Ovarian preservation is recommended for young patients with squamous cell carcinoma up to stage IB2 〔JSGO B, 1〕
      ・Ovarian preservation, or transposition outside the radiotherapy field, is considered in premenopausal patients 〔NCCN〕

    + Landoni 1997 {pmid 9284774}
    ⑤ Stage IA1, unsuitable for surgery: radiotherapy 〔JSGO〕 given as brachytherapy 〔ESMO III, B〕, with external-beam radiotherapy added when LVSI is present 〔ESMO IV, C〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Without LVSI and after a cone with negative margins, observation is an option 〔NCCN 2A〕

  Locally advanced disease, stage IB3 to IVA

    ① Stage IB3 and IIA2: chemoradiotherapy with image-guided brachytherapy 〔JSGO B, 1〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕, or radical hysterectomy with pelvic lymph node dissection 〔JSGO B, 1〕 〔ESMO IV, C〕 〔NCCN 2A〕

    ④ Stage IIIC with a primary tumour below IIIA extent: chemoradiotherapy 〔JSGO C, 2〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕, with pembrolizumab as an option 〔JSGO B, 2〕 〔NCCN 2B〕
      ・In stage IIIC1, radical hysterectomy with lymph node dissection is an alternative 〔JSGO C, 2〕
      ・In an exploratory analysis of KEYNOTE-A18, the gain in progression-free survival was seen in FIGO 2014 stage III to IVA and not in node-positive stage IB2 to IIB 〔JSGO〕 〔NCCN〕
      ・Pembrolizumab is given with caution in this group 〔JSGO〕
      ・Entry to KEYNOTE-A18 on nodal grounds required a node of 15 mm or more in short axis 〔JSGO〕
    ⑤ Para-aortic nodal metastases (stage IIIC2): chemoradiotherapy with extended-field radiotherapy 〔JSGO C, 2〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Prophylactic para-aortic irradiation is not suggested when only pelvic nodes are involved 〔JSGO C, 2 against〕
      ・Extended-field radiotherapy may be added for selected pelvic nodal disease, such as common iliac nodes 〔NCCN〕
    ⑥ Stage IB3 to IVA without para-aortic nodal metastases: induction carboplatin with paclitaxel, then chemoradiotherapy within 7 days 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔NCCN 2A, other recommended〕
      ・Induction is limited to stage IB3 to IIB where IMRT, VMAT or image-guided brachytherapy is not available 〔ESMO II, B〕
      ・Chemotherapy before chemoradiotherapy is not suggested, a grade set before INTERLACE was reported 〔JSGO C, 2 against〕
    + INTERLACE {pmid 39419054}

    ※ The whole course of chemoradiotherapy with brachytherapy is completed within 8 weeks 〔ESMO III, A〕 〔NCCN〕
    ※ Adjuvant chemotherapy after chemoradiotherapy is not suggested 〔JSGO C, 2 against〕
      ・Four cycles of carboplatin with paclitaxel after chemoradiotherapy did not improve overall survival 〔ESMO〕

      - EORTC 55994 {pmid 37656948}
