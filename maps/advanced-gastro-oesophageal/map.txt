# oncOS board map: Advanced Gastroesophageal (unresectable, recurrent and metastatic gastric, junctional and oesophageal cancer)
# 2026-11-13, approved by Jonan. Companion of the Localized Gastroesophageal map
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
# One map, two histologies: adenocarcinoma of the stomach, junction and oesophagus together (as ESMO and NCCN treat them),
#   split by HER2 and MSI at first line, and oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma
# Jonan 2026-11-13: the guidelines are consolidated. One statement per option with every agreeing tag on it. A ・ line
#   only where a guideline differs (a PD-L1 cut-off, a line of treatment, a licence)
# Tags give evidence first, strength second, on the statement the guideline graded. East Asian guidelines first:
#   〔JGCA evidence, strength〕 JGCA 2025, 7th edition (Gastric Cancer 2026;29:271-299). Clinical questions carry evidence A to D and
#     Strong or Weak, with "against" where the guideline recommends against. Regimens voted in the text are tagged
#     〔JGCA recommended〕 or 〔JGCA conditionally recommended〕. JGCA prints no evidence letter for reduction gastrectomy
#   〔JES strength〕 JES 2022, 5th edition (Kitagawa, Esophagus 2023;20:343-372). Strength only, the certainty letters were not read
#   〔KGCA evidence, strength〕 KGCA 2024 (Kim IH, J Gastric Cancer 2025;25:5-114). GRADE
#   〔PAGA level, grade〕 Pan-Asian adapted ESMO gastric guideline 2024 (Shitara, ESMO Open 2024;9:102226). Table 1 read 2026-11-13.
#     All recommendations had 100% consensus. Zolbetuximab (7d) is "can be considered" with no grade. Pembrolizumab (8b)
#     is graded II, A in the text and not in Table 1
#   〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO gastric and oesophageal cancer living guideline v1.0, November 2026. Web pages read 2026-11-13
#   〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Gastric Cancer 4.2026 and NCCN Esophageal and EGJ Cancers 4.2026. 2A unless stated
#   〔ASCO quality, strength〕 ASCO living guideline 2026.1.2, immunotherapy and targeted therapy (Shah, J Clin Oncol 2026;44:e170).
#     Evidence quality and Strong or Conditional. Read 2026-11-13. Chemotherapy-only lines are outside its scope
# JGCA 2025, KGCA 2024 and PAGA 2024 were written before DESTINY-Gastric04, HERIZON-GEA-01, GASTFOX, DRAGON-01 and PERISCOPE II.
#   JES 2022 was written before RATIONALE-306, RATIONALE-302 and JUPITER-06. NCCN 4.2026 (16 September 2026) does not yet include
#   PERISCOPE II or DRAGON-01, both published in July 2026
# Licensing is taken from the guideline texts, labels not read. Zanidatamab: US licence, no EU licence. Toripalimab in
#   oesophageal cancer: EU licence, no US licence. Singapore status of zanidatamab, tislelizumab, zolbetuximab and
#   toripalimab not checked. S-1 is on the Singapore Cancer Drug List and is numbered. Camrelizumab,
#   sintilimab, retlirafusp alfa and oral paclitaxel solution are licensed in China only and sit on ※ lines
# Trial marks follow the format header: + OS benefit, ~ surrogate endpoint, non-inferiority or inconclusive, - no benefit

band: Peritoneal metastases from gastric cancer
  ① Chemotherapy, including for poor oral intake or massive ascites after careful assessment of general condition 〔JGCA C, Weak〕
  ※ Gastrectomy with cytoreductive surgery and HIPEC did not improve overall survival over continued systemic treatment
    ・Patients had a peritoneal cancer index below 7 and no progression after 3 or more cycles
    ・Cytoreduction with intraperitoneal chemotherapy remains an option at a peritoneal cancer index of 10 or less after 3 months of systemic treatment 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・This recommendation predates PERISCOPE II
    - PERISCOPE II
  ※ Intraperitoneal with intravenous paclitaxel and S-1 improved overall survival over intravenous paclitaxel with S-1 in a trial in China
    ・An earlier trial in Japan showed no significant survival benefit
    ・Intraperitoneal chemotherapy is graded investigational, in a recommendation predating DRAGON-01 〔KGCA Low, Investigational〕
    + DRAGON-01
    - PHOENIX-GC [t-phoenix-gc]

band: Limited metastases and surgery
  ① Resection after chemotherapy for a few para-aortic nodes in stations 16a2 and 16b1, or resection of a solitary liver metastasis, with no other non-curable factor 〔JGCA C, Weak〕
    ・Only in highly selected patients whose disease responded to chemotherapy 〔PAGA V, C〕
    ・Liver resection is investigational, with very low certainty of evidence 〔KGCA Very low, Investigational〕
  ② Ovarian metastases: gastrectomy, oophorectomy and systemic treatment for selected patients 〔KGCA Very low, Conditional〕
  ※ Surgery after FLOT did not improve overall survival in limited metastatic disease (congress abstract)
    ・Early mortality was higher with surgery
    ・Survival was longer with surgery in the subgroup with retroperitoneal nodal metastases only
    - RENAISSANCE [t-ikf-575-renaissance]
  ※ Gastrectomy is not recommended for metastatic disease, except for symptom control 〔JGCA Strong against〕 〔KGCA High, Strong against〕 〔PAGA I, D〕
    - REGATTA [t-regatta]
  ※ Conversion surgery after a good response is investigational 〔KGCA Low, Investigational〕

band: Symptom control
  ① Dysphagia from oesophageal cancer: palliative radiotherapy 〔JES Weak〕
    ・Addition of chemotherapy did not improve dysphagia relief and increased toxicity
    ・Stenting in selected patients, with shorter duration of relief than radiotherapy 〔NCCN 2A〕
    - TROG 03.01 [t-trog-03-01]
  ② Gastric outlet obstruction: a stent or gastrojejunostomy 〔JGCA D, Weak〕 〔KGCA Low, Conditional〕
    ・Palliative resection for bleeding or stenosis 〔JGCA D, Weak〕
  ③ Tumour bleeding: haemostatic radiotherapy 〔JGCA C, Weak〕
  ④ Early palliative care and nutritional support 〔PAGA I, A〕

course: Advanced gastric and oesophageal cancer
  Unresectable or metastatic disease
    ① HER2, PD-L1, MSI or mismatch repair, and CLDN18.2 testing before first-line treatment 〔JGCA A, Strong〕 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ASCO Good practice〕

      ・Five to eight biopsies, given intratumoural heterogeneity of HER2 expression 〔PAGA IV, B〕

      ・CLDN18.2-positive: moderate or strong membrane staining in 75% or more of viable tumour cells
      ・CPS and TAP are highly concordant and interchangeable 〔NCCN 2A〕

      ・DPYD genotyping before fluoropyrimidine treatment 〔ASCO〕
    ② Systemic treatment for ECOG performance status 0 to 2 〔NCCN 2A〕

    ※ Locally unresectable disease with no distant metastases: chemoradiotherapy 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・T4b oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma: definitive chemoradiotherapy 〔JES Weak〕
