# oncOS board map: Head & Neck SCC (oral cavity, oropharynx, larynx, hypopharynx and unknown primary)
# Built 2026-11-12 from the structure plan, an independent review and Jonan's choices (full detail, KEYNOTE-689 numbered, notable trials as ※ lines)
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
# One map for the sheet, by setting: early disease and unknown primary (bands), locally advanced disease (course), recurrent or metastatic disease (course)
# The first step lists the site preferences and branches by the treatment chosen. Lip, sinonasal, salivary, thyroid and nasopharynx are not on this map
# Tags give evidence first, strength second, on the statement the guideline graded. East Asian guidelines first:
#   〔JSHNC certainty, strength〕 JSHNC 2025 English summary (Hanai, Auris Nasus Larynx 2025;52:709). Certainty A to D, strength Strong or Weak,
#     "against" where the guideline recommends against. Literature cut-off August 2024. Read 2026-11-12
#   〔PAGA level, grade〕 ESMO Pan-Asian adaptation for SCCHN (Keam, ESMO Open 2021;6:100309). Read 2026-11-12
#   〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Head and Neck Cancers 2.2026. Category 2A unless stated (SYST-A). Read 2026-11-12
#   〔ASCO-RM quality, strength〕 ASCO immunotherapy and biomarkers in recurrent or metastatic disease (Yilmaz, J Clin Oncol 2023;41:1132)
#   〔ASCO-TORS quality, strength〕 ASCO transoral robotic surgery in HPV-positive oropharynx (Holsinger, J Clin Oncol 2025;43:1369)
#   〔ASCO-Neck quality, strength〕 ASCO management of the neck in oral cavity and oropharynx (Koyfman, J Clin Oncol 2019;37:1753)
#   〔ASCO-UP quality, strength〕 ASCO unknown primary in the head and neck (Maghami, J Clin Oncol 2020;38:2570)
#   〔Ferrari level, agreement〕 International consensus on larynx preservation (Ferrari, Lancet Oncol 2025;26:e264). Level of evidence I to V
#     (ESMO-adapted IDSA) and the share of the panel that agreed. The consensus gives no strength grade
# JSHNC 2025 and PAGA 2021 predate KEYNOTE-689. The option carries its NCCN tags and licences
# Licensing read 2026-11-12: perioperative pembrolizumab FDA, EC and HSA (CPS 1 or more). First-line pembrolizumab in the EU needs CPS 1 or more
#   for both uses (EPAR). FDA and HSA first-line labels not read. No licence found for adjuvant nivolumab (NIVOPOSTOP)
#   Cetuximab sarotalocan has conditional approval in Japan only. Intra-arterial chemoradiotherapy is a Japanese practice. Both sit on ※ lines marked Japan only
# Staging: NCCN uses AJCC 9th edition, with 8th edition clinical groups in the oropharynx algorithm. PAGA uses UICC 8th edition

band: Work-up
  ① MRI for T staging 〔JSHNC C, Weak〕
    ・CT or MRI of the primary and neck, and CT of the chest 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・FDG-PET is useful for staging 〔JSHNC B, Weak〕
  ② p16 immunohistochemistry for every oropharyngeal cancer 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・HPV testing on the biopsy of HPV-positive oropharynx before transoral surgery 〔ASCO-TORS Low, Strong〕
  ③ PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS) at every site 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Before pembrolizumab in recurrent or metastatic disease 〔JSHNC B, Strong〕 〔PAGA II, B〕 〔ASCO-RM High, Strong〕
    ・A CPS of 1 or more is positive 〔ASCO-RM High, Strong〕
  ④ Hepatitis B screening, and dental, nutrition, swallowing and hearing assessment 〔NCCN 2A〕

band: Early disease, T1–2 N0
  ① Oral cavity: resection of the primary 〔PAGA IV, A〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
    ・Radiotherapy, or brachytherapy for selected T1 〔JSHNC C, Weak〕 〔PAGA IV, B〕
    ・cT2 N0: ipsilateral elective neck dissection 〔ASCO-Neck High, Strong〕
    ・cT1 N0: elective neck dissection, or close ultrasound surveillance in selected reliable patients 〔ASCO-Neck Intermediate, Strong〕
    ・Stage I–II tongue cancer with deep invasion: elective neck dissection improves survival 〔JSHNC B, Weak〕
    + D'Cruz 2015 [t-d-cruz-et-al]
    ・Sentinel node biopsy, with neck dissection if a node is positive 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Sentinel node biopsy is reliable in early tongue cancer 〔JSHNC A, Weak〕
    ~ Hasegawa 2021 [t-hasegawa-et-al]
    ~ Garrel 2020 [t-senti-merorl]
  ② Oropharynx: radiotherapy, or transoral surgery with neck dissection 〔PAGA IV, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・HPV-positive T1–2: transoral robotic surgery is discussed when a clear margin is likely 〔ASCO-TORS Moderate, Strong〕
    ・Transoral resection is an option 〔JSHNC C, Weak〕
    ~ ORATOR [t-orator]
  ③ Larynx: radiotherapy, or larynx-preserving surgery 〔PAGA IV, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Early glottic cancer: 2.25 to 2.5 Gy per fraction 〔JSHNC C, Weak〕
    - JCOG0701 [t-jcog-0701]
    ・T2 with deep invasion or impaired cord movement: chemoradiotherapy is an option 〔JSHNC C, Weak〕
    ・cT2 N0 unsuitable for larynx-preserving surgery: radiotherapy is adequate 〔Ferrari IV, 94%〕
  ④ Hypopharynx: larynx-preserving radiotherapy, or larynx-preserving surgery 〔JSHNC C, Strong〕 〔PAGA IV, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ※ Early disease is treated with a single modality where possible 〔PAGA IV, A〕
  ※ Adverse features at resection lead to postoperative treatment as for locally advanced disease

band: Unknown primary
  ① FDG-PET-CT when examination and CT show no primary 〔JSHNC B, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ASCO-UP Intermediate, Strong〕
    ・HPV testing on level II–III nodes, and EBV testing on HPV-negative nodes 〔ASCO-UP Intermediate, Moderate〕
  ② Tonsillectomy at examination under anaesthesia 〔JSHNC B, Strong〕 〔ASCO-UP Intermediate, Moderate〕
    ・An HPV-positive node is treated as oropharyngeal cancer, and an EBV-positive node as nasopharyngeal cancer 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ③ Unilateral small-volume neck disease: neck dissection or radiotherapy 〔ASCO-UP Intermediate, Moderate〕
    ・Neck dissection is an option 〔JSHNC C, Weak〕
  ④ Large-volume bilateral disease or gross extranodal extension: chemoradiotherapy 〔ASCO-UP Intermediate, Moderate〕
    ・p16-negative N2–3 disease: radiotherapy with concurrent cisplatin 〔ASCO-UP High, Strong〕
  ⑤ After neck dissection with multiple nodes or extranodal extension: radiotherapy or chemoradiotherapy 〔JSHNC B, Strong〕 〔ASCO-UP Intermediate, Strong〕
    ・Concurrent cisplatin for extranodal extension 〔ASCO-UP High, Strong〕
    ・A single node without extranodal extension needs no radiotherapy after a high-quality dissection 〔ASCO-UP Intermediate, Strong〕

    ⑤ Unresectable disease at any site: chemoradiotherapy is the first treatment

  Surgery first
    branch: SX
    ① Resection with neck dissection, then radiotherapy or chemoradiotherapy by pathology 〔PAGA IV, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Unequivocal extranodal extension into soft tissue, or carotid or cranial nerve involvement: non-surgical treatment 〔ASCO-Neck Intermediate, Moderate〕
      ・HPV-positive oropharynx: transoral surgery is not offered when both postoperative radiotherapy and chemotherapy are expected 〔ASCO-TORS Low, Strong〕
    ② PD-L1 CPS 1 or more: pembrolizumab before surgery, with postoperative radiotherapy and after it
      ・Oral cavity 〔NCCN 2A, other recommended〕
      ・p16-negative oropharynx, hypopharynx and larynx 〔NCCN 2A, useful in certain circumstances〕
      ・NCCN does not list it for p16-positive oropharynx or for N3 disease
      ・Cisplatin is added to radiotherapy for extranodal extension or a positive margin
      ・It is licensed by the FDA, the European Commission and the HSA for CPS 1 or more
      ・JSHNC 2025 and PAGA 2021 were written before KEYNOTE-689 was reported
    ~ KEYNOTE-689
    ※ Preoperative chemotherapy is not recommended for locally advanced tongue cancer 〔JSHNC A, Weak against〕
    next: After surgery
  Larynx preservation
    branch: LP
    T2–3 or node-positive disease that would need total laryngectomy
    ① Concurrent chemoradiotherapy with cisplatin 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕
      ・For cT3 it gives the highest larynx preservation rate of the non-surgical options 〔Ferrari IV, 92%〕
      ・It carries more long-term deaths not due to cancer than laryngectomy or induction 〔Ferrari IV, 82%〕
    ~ RTOG 91-11
    ② Induction TPF, then radiotherapy alone in responders 〔JSHNC B, Weak〕 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Induction can select cT3 patients for organ preservation 〔Ferrari I, 95%〕
      ・Complete response: radiotherapy alone 〔NCCN 1〕 〔Ferrari II, 97%〕
      ・Partial response: chemoradiotherapy 〔NCCN 2B〕
      ・Less than a partial response: total laryngectomy
      ・Stable disease when laryngectomy is declined: radiotherapy with concurrent platinum 〔Ferrari IV, 82%〕
      ・Cetuximab is not added to TPF 〔Ferrari II, 93%〕
    ~ VA Larynx [t-va-larynx]
    ~ RTOG 91-11
    ~ GORTEC 2000-01 [t-gortec-2000-01]
    ~ EORTC 24891 [t-eortc-24891]
    ※ Aspiration or recurrent pneumonia from a poorly functioning larynx is a contraindication to non-surgical preservation 〔Ferrari IV, 95%〕
    ※ Unfit for cisplatin and declining laryngectomy: carboplatin-based chemoradiotherapy, or cetuximab with radiotherapy 〔Ferrari II, 90%〕
      ・Unfit for any systemic treatment: accelerated or hyperfractionated radiotherapy alone 〔Ferrari II, 98%〕
    T4a in selected patients, or laryngectomy declined
    ① Chemoradiotherapy, or induction then radiotherapy 〔PAGA IV, B〕
      ・Suitable patients have minimal extralaryngeal spread, limited cartilage invasion and a functional larynx 〔Ferrari IV, 94%〕
      ・A response of more than 50% to induction allows non-surgical preservation 〔Ferrari III, 90%〕
      ・The consensus did not agree whether chemoradiotherapy or induction is better in T4a
      ・Hypopharynx T4a: induction, then radiotherapy or surgery 〔PAGA I, A〕
    next: Chemoradiotherapy
  Non-surgical treatment
    branch: NS

  Chemoradiotherapy
    ① Cisplatin 100 mg/m² on days 1, 22 and 43 of radiotherapy 〔PAGA II, A〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
