# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
# Scope (Jonan 2026-11-15): the systemic course, with two panels: cardiac monitoring, and endocrine therapy and neratinib
#   in HR-positive HER2-positive disease. Surgery and radiotherapy are out, except where they set a systemic decision
#   (the response found at surgery after neoadjuvant therapy). No metastatic section. Chips may cite rows of the shared
#   sheet "General Principles" (CTNeoBC)
# Layout: the course carries "| parallel". Two presentations start side by side: neoadjuvant therapy (clinical stage II
#   or III, and tumours under 2 cm where it is considered), and primary surgery first with adjuvant therapy by
#   pathological stage. The neoadjuvant lane leads to treatment by response at surgery, a later step of the same branch
#   (pale tint). Both lanes end in one step on completing 1 year of anti-HER2 therapy, which stays grey as a step for
#   every branch. No header step and no line labels. HER2 testing and the shared regimens are bands. The criteria for
#   trastuzumab deruxtecan that NCCN prints are written into numbered lines (no table). Trial eligibility that no
#   guideline prints (DESTINY-Breast05: 9 weeks of each drug, no interstitial lung disease) sits on an untagged ・ line
#   resting on the chip below it
# One narrative: a point several guidelines agree on is one statement with all their tags. A ・ line gives a real
#   difference (a population, a cut-off, a grade, an order) or a licence. One numbered line per population
# Guideline set (Jonan 2026-11-15): PAGA 2024, ESMO 2026 (in place of ESMO 2024 from 2026-11-27), NCCN 9.2026 and St Gallen 2025, with ESC 2022 and the US
#   trastuzumab label on the cardiac panel only. Tag order PAGA, ESMO, NCCN, St Gallen, ESC, FDA label. No JBCS. The ASCO guidelines are archived and left out
# Tags give the grade as printed, on the statement the guideline graded: evidence first and strength second, except
#   〔ESC〕, which keeps its printed class then level. A tag at the end of an option covers the option. Doses, cycles and
#   durations on that line follow NCCN and the trial rows (St Gallen prints only the 18-week THP course):
#   〔PAGA level, grade〕 Pan-Asian adapted ESMO guideline 2024 (Park, ESMO Open 2024;9:102974)
#   〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO early breast cancer guideline 2026 (Trapani, Ann Oncol 2026, journal pre-proof accepted
#     9 November 2026, PMID 43085660), which supersedes the 2024 guideline. A bare 〔ESMO〕 marks ungraded text or a
#     figure footnote. ESMO-MCBS scores are not shown. The Supplementary Material was not read
#   〔NCCN category〕 NCCN Breast Cancer 9.2026 (22 October 2026), with "preferred" as printed. A bare 〔NCCN〕 marks a
#     footnote, a principles page or the Discussion
#   〔ASCO-CAP〕 ASCO-CAP HER2 testing guideline update 2023 (Wolff, J Clin Oncol 2023;41:3867-3872), read in full from
#     Jonan's PDF (2026-11-16). It affirms and reprints the 2018 recommendations (Table A2, Fig 1) and adds the reporting
#     comment on IHC 0 versus 1+. Ungraded, so the tag is bare. The HER2 definition lines are the same on the HER2+ EBC
#     and HER2+ MBC maps (Jonan 2026-11-16), with each map's own guideline tags
#   〔St Gallen〕 St Gallen consensus 2025 (Burstein, Ann Oncol 2025;36:1433-1446), a panel vote with no grade, tagged only
#     where it adds a point. The source is declared as "St" so that the tag prints "St Gallen" (outline.js reads the first
#     word of a tag as the source name)
#   〔ESC class, level〕 2022 ESC cardio-oncology guideline (Lyon, Eur Heart J 2022;43:4229-4361), on the cardiac panel
#     only. None of the four guidelines prints LVEF thresholds or rules for holding and restarting anti-HER2
#     therapy (Jonan 2026-11-15). A bare 〔ESC〕 marks ungraded text, the HFA-ICOS table (Table 4), or a table or figure
#     footnote. In the cardiac panel a class I statement is written in
#     the present tense, IIa as "should be considered" and IIb as "can be considered". An ungraded footnote keeps its own verb, with
#     "may" written as "can". The ESMO 2020 cardiac consensus
#     was read and not used (board-maps/her2-ebc/notes-esmo-cardiac-2020.md). Source notes:
#     board-maps/her2-ebc/notes-esc-cardio-2022.md
#   〔FDA label〕 Herceptin US prescribing information, revised 5/2026 (DailyMed), sections 2.5 and 5.1. The cardiac panel
#     leads with the label's schedule and its hold, resume and stop rules, with the ESC position as a ・ line under each
#     rule where the cut-offs differ (Jonan 2026-11-15)
# Trial marks: + overall survival or mortality benefit, ~ surrogate endpoint or non-inferiority, - no benefit from the
#   tested change, * no comparison for the statement. Single-arm trials go under off: (Jonan)
# Exceptions (Jonan 2026-11-15, decided):
#   Rule ⑦: trastuzumab deruxtecan then THP before surgery, and trastuzumab deruxtecan for high-risk residual disease,
#     became numbered options on 2026-11-15 while NCCN alone graded them. ESMO 2026 now grades both (DESTINY-Breast11
#     I, B and DESTINY-Breast05 I, A), so they are numbered options under the usual rules. PAGA 2024 and St Gallen 2025
#     were written before the trials
#   APT stays as a ~ chip under the pT1a-b pN0 line, a single-arm exception like LORIN on the early NSCLC map: the
#     guidelines rest paclitaxel with trastuzumab for small node-negative tumours on it, and no randomised trial of that
#     option exists

band: HER2 testing and work-up
  ① HER2 is positive with an IHC score of 3+, or with dual-probe in situ hybridisation showing a HER2/CEP17 ratio of 2.0 or more and an average HER2 copy number of 4.0 or more signals per cell 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
    ・An IHC score of 3+ is complete, intense circumferential membrane staining in more than 10% of tumour cells 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
    ・An IHC score of 2+ is weak to moderate complete membrane staining in more than 10% of tumour cells. It is equivocal and is resolved by in situ hybridisation on the same or a new specimen 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
    ・A ratio of 2.0 or more with a copy number below 4.0 (group 2), or a ratio below 2.0 with a copy number of 4.0 to below 6.0 (group 4), is positive only with IHC 3+ on the same tissue 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
    ・A ratio below 2.0 with a copy number of 6.0 or more (group 3) is positive with IHC 3+, or with IHC 2+ when a blinded recount confirms group 3 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
    ・With IHC 2+ in groups 2 to 4, an additional observer, blinded to the first count, recounts at least 20 cells in the area of IHC 2+ staining 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
    ・IHC 0 and 1+ are both HER2-negative. The IHC score is always reported, because IHC 1+, or 2+ without amplification, gives eligibility for trastuzumab deruxtecan 〔NCCN〕 〔ASCO-CAP〕
  ※ After a negative HER2 result on a core biopsy, a new test on the excision specimen can be ordered on clinical criteria such as grade 3 〔ASCO-CAP〕
  ② ER, PgR, HER2 and a proliferation marker such as Ki-67 are assessed on every tumour before treatment 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕
  ※ Genomic assays such as HER2DX remain investigational 〔ESMO〕, and HER2DX is not recommended for routine treatment decisions 〔St Gallen〕

course: Systemic treatment of stage I to III HER2-positive breast cancer | parallel
  Neoadjuvant therapy
    branch: NEO
    ① Tumour over 2 cm or node-positive: chemotherapy with trastuzumab and pertuzumab 〔PAGA I, A〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN〕 〔St Gallen〕, with the whole chemotherapy course given before surgery 〔NCCN〕

panel: Endocrine therapy and neratinib > Completing 1 year of anti-HER2 therapy
  ① HR-positive: endocrine therapy 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1〕 〔St Gallen〕, given after chemotherapy 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN〕
  ※ Endocrine therapy can be adapted to whether neoadjuvant therapy achieved a pathological complete response 〔ESMO〕
  ※ The endocrine drug is chosen as in HER2-negative disease 〔NCCN 1〕
  ※ In premenopausal women at higher risk of recurrence, marked by young age or long life expectancy, a high-grade tumour or nodal involvement, 5 years of tamoxifen or an aromatase inhibitor with ovarian function suppression should be considered 〔NCCN 1〕
  ※ Ovarian function suppression for 5 years is optimal, with at least 2 years encouraged 〔NCCN〕
  ※ In men, an aromatase inhibitor is given only with a GnRH agonist 〔NCCN〕
    ・Tamoxifen or an aromatase inhibitor with a GnRH agonist is an option in men 〔ESMO III, B〕
  ② HR-positive at high risk, after 1 year of trastuzumab: neratinib for 1 year can be considered 〔PAGA I, B〕 〔ESMO I, B〕 〔NCCN 2A〕

panel: Cardiac monitoring
  ① Cardiac function is assessed before, during and after anti-HER2 therapy 〔PAGA II, B〕 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔NCCN〕 〔ESC I, B〕 〔FDA label〕
    ・LVEF is measured before trastuzumab and every 3 months during treatment 〔ESC I, B〕 〔FDA label〕, and at its completion 〔FDA label〕
    ・At low risk, without symptoms and with a normal echocardiogram at 3 months, echocardiography every 4 months can be considered 〔ESC IIb, C〕
    ・While trastuzumab is withheld, LVEF is measured every 4 weeks 〔FDA label〕
  ② Trastuzumab is withheld for at least 4 weeks when LVEF falls from the pre-treatment value by 16 percentage points or more, or by 10 percentage points or more to below the institutional lower limit of normal 〔FDA label〕
    ・Without symptoms, with LVEF 50% or more and a relative fall in global longitudinal strain of more than 15%, a new rise in troponin or natriuretic peptide, or both, anti-HER2 therapy continues with more frequent monitoring 〔ESC I, C〕
    ・Without symptoms, at a new LVEF of 40 to 49% after a fall of 10 percentage points or more, or after a smaller fall with a relative fall in global longitudinal strain of more than 15% or a new rise in troponin or natriuretic peptide, continued anti-HER2 therapy with more frequent monitoring should be considered 〔ESC IIa, B〕
    ・In this group, an ACE inhibitor or angiotensin receptor blocker with a beta-blocker is started 〔ESC I, C〕
    ・Anti-HER2 therapy is interrupted at a new LVEF below 40%, or with heart failure that needs intensified treatment or admission to hospital 〔ESC I, C〕
  ③ Trastuzumab can be resumed if, within 4 to 8 weeks, LVEF returns to within normal limits and is no more than 15 percentage points below the pre-treatment value 〔FDA label〕
    ・Without symptoms and with LVEF recovered to 40% or more, and ideally 50% or more, a restart with heart failure treatment should be considered 〔ESC〕
    ・The decision to restart is made by the multidisciplinary team 〔ESC I, C〕
  ④ Trastuzumab is stopped permanently for an LVEF decline that lasts more than 8 weeks, or after it has been withheld on more than 3 occasions for cardiomyopathy 〔FDA label〕
    ・If LVEF stays below 40% or heart failure persists, a restart can be considered when no other cancer treatment exists 〔ESC〕
  ⑤ Cardiovascular toxicity risk is graded before treatment 〔ESC I, B〕
    ・High or very high risk includes heart failure, cardiomyopathy or previous cancer therapy-related cardiac dysfunction, LVEF below 50%, previous myocardial infarction or coronary revascularisation, stable angina, severe valve disease, age 80 years or over, previous trastuzumab, an anthracycline given concurrently with trastuzumab, and 5 or more points from moderate-risk factors 〔ESC〕
  ⑥ At high or very high risk, cardiology referral is made before treatment 〔ESC I, C〕
    ・Echocardiography, troponin and natriuretic peptide every 2 to 3 cycles should be considered 〔ESC IIa, C〕
    ・An ACE inhibitor or angiotensin receptor blocker with a beta-blocker should be considered for primary prevention 〔ESC IIa, B〕
  ⑦ After adjuvant trastuzumab, LVEF is measured every 6 months for at least 2 years 〔FDA label〕
    ・An echocardiogram is recorded within 12 months after the end of treatment 〔ESC I, B〕
    ・Without symptoms and with normal cardiac function, cardiac follow-up is as clinically indicated 〔PAGA III, B〕
    ・A cardiovascular risk assessment with an ECG and natriuretic peptide takes place every year 〔ESC I, B〕
  ※ Concurrent use of trastuzumab and pertuzumab with an anthracycline should be avoided 〔NCCN〕
  ※ A taxane with carboplatin causes less cardiac toxicity than an anthracycline regimen 〔ESMO〕 〔NCCN〕
  ※ Docetaxel and carboplatin with trastuzumab is preferred with risk factors for cardiac toxicity 〔NCCN〕
