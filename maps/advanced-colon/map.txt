# Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#   〔JSCCR evidence, strength〕 JSCCR guidelines 2024, English edition (Int J Clin Oncol 2025;30:2410-63), CQ boxes only.

band: Resectable liver or lung metastases
  ① Resection of all disease when an R0 resection is feasible 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Weak〕
    ・Resection when the primary is controlled and the liver remnant is adequate 〔JSCCR〕
    ・Resection of both when liver and lung metastases are resectable 〔JSCCR〕
  ② FOLFOX or CAPOX before and after resection, or after it, for 6 months in total 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔ESMO II, B〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Weak〕
    ・Chemotherapy before resection of resectable liver metastases is weakly not recommended 〔JSCCR C, 2〕
    ・Adjuvant chemotherapy after hepatectomy 〔JSCCR B, 2〕
    ~ EPOC
    ~ JCOG0603 [t-jcog-0603]
    ※ Addition of cetuximab to perioperative chemotherapy for liver resection is not recommended 〔NCCN 2A〕
      - New EPOC
  ③ Thermal ablation for liver metastases of 3 cm or less 〔NCCN 2A〕 〔ESMO II, B〕
    ・Ablation first for resectable liver metastases is not recommended 〔JSCCR C, 1〕
    ・Ablation or SBRT for lung metastases, with or instead of resection 〔ESMO III, B〕
  ④ SBRT for liver metastases that cannot be resected or ablated 〔ESMO III, B〕 〔ASCO Low, Weak〕

band: Potentially resectable metastases
  ① First-line treatment, then resection as soon as it is feasible 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Resectability is reassessed every 2 to 3 months 〔ESMO IV, A〕
    ~ CAIRO5 [t-cairo5]
    ※ Debulking of multiorgan disease is not recommended 〔NCCN 2A〕
      - ORCHESTRA
  ※ Liver transplantation with chemotherapy for unresectable liver-only metastases in selected patients 〔ESMO II, A〕
    ・Selection: BRAF wild-type, CEA below 80 ng/mL (80 µg/L), disease control for 3 months and no extrahepatic disease 〔ESMO〕
    ・Transplantation is not addressed in JSCCR, NCCN or ASCO
    + TransMet
  ※ SIRT is not routinely recommended 〔ASCO Low, Weak〕

band: Peritoneum and the primary tumour
  ① Peritoneal metastases only: complete cytoreductive surgery in a specialised centre 〔ESMO II, A〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Weak〕
    ・Complete resection of limited peritoneal metastases 〔JSCCR〕
    ※ Addition of oxaliplatin HIPEC to cytoreductive surgery is not recommended 〔ESMO II, D〕 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
      - PRODIGE 7
  ② Asymptomatic primary tumour with unresectable metastases: systemic treatment without resection 〔JSCCR B, 2〕 〔ESMO I, D〕
    ・A symptomatic primary tumour is resected first 〔JSCCR C, 1〕
    - JCOG1007 [t-ipacs]
