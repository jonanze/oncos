# oncOS board map: SCLC
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
#   join: Step | label  the line of next:, but the two steps stay in separate branches (limited stage runs down to relapse)
#   Trial {url https://...}  a randomised trial with no PubMed record yet (congress abstract): link, listed for intake
#
# Rules (Jonan 2026-11-08/09/10; node maps/check.cjs enforces the starred ones)
#   Layout fits the disease: a course, limited stage and extensive stage, both ending in relapse
#   Relapse is split into sensitive and refractory at 90 days, as ASCO does (JLCS about 60–90 days); NCCN no longer
#     chooses treatment by interval, which a ※ line says (Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   Square lines only; no result colours
# * Every sheet row is on the map or under off: with a reason
# * Trial lines cite clinical trials, pooled analyses of trials or meta-analyses only
#   Inclusion: maps/inclusion.txt; trials left out are in excluded.txt
#   Standard options only; an EU or US licensed option that a guideline does not list stays on the
#     line with its licence noted and says which guidelines leave it out
#   Option order follows the guidelines' preference; where they disagree each differing guideline gets its own ・ line
#   One statement per line, no semicolon chains: a guideline's position, a licence or a condition goes on a
#     ・ line under the line it qualifies (Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   Consolidation, PCI and maintenance are a later step after the primary treatment, never a branch beside it
# * Plain clinical wording
#   Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#     〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Small Cell Lung Cancer 1.2027 (1 July 2026); options NCCN alone
#       lists sit on ※ lines or as ・ lines in their drug class; the tag names the guideline, so the text
#       does not repeat it (Jonan 2026-11-11)
#     〔ASCO quality, strength〕 ASCO-Ontario Health 2023 (Khurshid), systemic therapy only, with the
#       ASCO rapid update of 20 November 2024 (Kalemkerian: 2.4, 4.1, 4.2, 5.3.1)
#     〔JLCS evidence, strength〕 Japan Lung Cancer Society guideline 2025 (web edition, 6 November 2025),
#       chapter Ⅲ, CQ1–19 and BQ1–2; strength 1 strong or 2 weak, evidence A to D; a BQ has an
#       evidence grade only; errata of 15 January 2026 checked, none in this chapter
#   ESMO 2021 (Dingemans) dropped as stale: it predates ADRIATIC, IMforte, DeLLphi-304 and the
#     tarlatamab and serplulimab licences (Jonan 2026-11-10); ASTRO 2020 not used

# Named by Jonan under maps/inclusion.txt: DeLLphi-305 on the main line before licence (③, 2026-11-09);
#   KEYNOTE-604 and CheckMate 451 kept although negative experimental trials (⑥c: PD-1 alone, and as
#   maintenance, does not work in extensive stage); TAISHAN-302 on a ※ line (③, trial in China) (2026-11-10);
#   LAGOON has no trial line until an abstract or paper appears; the FDA withdrawal ※ line stays (2026-11-10)

band: Staging before treatment
  ① TNM 8th edition with two stages 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ・Limited: stage I–III that a tolerable radiotherapy plan can cover
  ・Extensive: stage IV, or T3–4 with lung nodules or tumour volume too large for a radiotherapy plan
  ② Contrast CT of chest, abdomen and pelvis, and brain MRI, or contrast CT of the brain when MRI is not possible, at every stage 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ③ PET-CT when needed to clarify the extent of disease 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ・An isolated or equivocal lesion that would change treatment is confirmed by biopsy
  ④ Pathological mediastinal staging before surgery for clinical T1–2 N0, by EBUS first where the expertise exists 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ⑤ Biomarker testing only in rare cases: never or light smokers, remote smoking, diagnostic doubt, or at relapse if not done before 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ・Molecular profiling does not guide standard treatment 〔ASCO Low, Weak〕

  Thoracic radiotherapy
    branch: CRT pale
    Thoracic radiotherapy with chemotherapy improves survival
      + Pignon 1992 [t-pignon-et-al]
    ① 45 Gy in 30 fractions twice daily 〔NCCN 1〕 〔JLCS B, 1〕
    ・Starting with chemotherapy cycle 1 or 2 〔NCCN 1〕, early concurrent start 〔JLCS B, 1〕
    ・Chemotherapy is not delayed until radiotherapy can start 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
      + Intergroup 0096
    ※ Concurrent radiotherapy gave longer survival than sequential radiotherapy, a difference that was not significant
      - JCOG 9104 [t-jcog-9104]
    ② 66–70 Gy in once-daily fractions over 6.5–7 weeks, an accepted alternative 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・JLCS keeps once-daily treatment for when twice daily is not feasible
      - CONVERT
    ※ 70 Gy daily was no better than 45 Gy twice daily
      - CALGB 30610 [t-calgb-30610-rtog-0538]
    ※ Higher-dose accelerated radiotherapy
    ・54 Gy in 30 twice-daily fractions, with a simultaneous boost to the gross tumour, improved OS in a phase III trial in China
    ・60 Gy twice daily and 65 Gy daily were tested in randomised phase II trials
    ・NCCN notes that these trials indicate an advantage
    ・NCCN recommends no dose above 45 Gy twice daily and does not define the dose with durvalumab
    ・JLCS does not discuss them
      + Yu 2024 {pmid 39146944}
      + THORA [t-thora]
      ~ Qiu 2021 {pmid 33992717}
