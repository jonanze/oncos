#   join: Step | label  the line of next:, but the two steps stay in separate branches (limited stage runs down to relapse)
#   Trial {url https://...}  a randomised trial with no PubMed record yet (congress abstract): link, listed for intake
#
# Rules (Jonan 2026-11-08/09/10; node maps/check.cjs enforces the starred ones)
#   Layout fits the disease: a course, limited stage and extensive stage, both ending in relapse
#   Relapse is split into sensitive and refractory at 90 days, as ASCO does (JLCS about 60–90 days); NCCN no longer
#     chooses treatment by interval, which a ※ line says (Jonan 2026-11-10)
#   Square lines only; no result colours
# * Every sheet row is on the map or under off: with a reason
# * Trial lines cite clinical trials, pooled analyses of trials or meta-analyses only
#   Inclusion: maps/inclusion.txt; trials left out are in excluded.txt
#   Standard options only; an EU or US licensed option that a guideline does not list stays on the
#     line with its licence noted and says which guidelines leave it out
#   Option order follows the guidelines' preference; where they disagree a ※ line names each
#   Consolidation, PCI and maintenance are a later step after the primary treatment, never a branch beside it
# * Plain clinical wording
#   Tags give evidence first, strength second, as printed, on the statement the guideline graded:
#     〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Small Cell Lung Cancer 1.2027 (1 July 2026); options NCCN alone
#       lists sit on ※ lines
#     〔ASCO quality, strength〕 ASCO-Ontario Health 2023 (Khurshid), systemic therapy only, with the
#       ASCO rapid update of 20 November 2024 (Kalemkerian: 2.4, 4.1, 4.2, 5.3.1)
#     〔JLCS evidence, strength〕 Japan Lung Cancer Society guideline 2025 (web edition, 6 November 2025),
#       chapter Ⅲ, CQ1–19 and BQ1–2; strength 1 strong or 2 weak, evidence A to D; a BQ has an
#       evidence grade only; errata of 15 January 2026 checked, none in this chapter
#   ESMO 2021 (Dingemans) dropped as stale: it predates ADRIATIC, IMforte, DeLLphi-304 and the
#     tarlatamab and serplulimab licences (Jonan 2026-11-10); ASTRO 2020 not used

band: Staging before treatment
  ① TNM 8th edition with two stages: limited, stage I–III that a tolerable radiotherapy plan can cover; extensive, stage IV, or T3–4 with lung nodules or tumour volume too large for a radiotherapy plan 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ② Contrast CT of chest, abdomen and pelvis, and brain MRI, or contrast CT of the brain when MRI is not possible, at every stage 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ③ PET-CT when needed to clarify the extent of disease; an isolated or equivocal lesion that would change treatment is confirmed by biopsy 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ④ Pathological mediastinal staging before surgery for clinical T1–2 N0, by EBUS first where the expertise exists 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ⑤ Biomarker testing only in rare cases: never or light smokers, remote smoking, diagnostic doubt, or at relapse if not done before 〔NCCN 2A〕; molecular profiling does not guide standard treatment 〔ASCO Low, Weak〕

    Platinum and etoposide for 4 cycles with atezolizumab (carboplatin) or durvalumab (cisplatin or carboplatin), then maintenance immunotherapy 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕; PS 0–1 〔JLCS A, 1〕; older patients with PS 0–1 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕

    ※ PS 2: the same regimens as for PS 0–1 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕; carboplatin, etoposide and immunotherapy 〔ASCO Low, Weak〕; platinum with etoposide or irinotecan 〔JLCS B, 1〕, as JLCS limits immunotherapy to PS 0–1
    ※ PS 3–4 due to SCLC: systemic treatment 〔NCCN 2A〕; chemotherapy 〔ASCO Low, Weak〕; PS 3, carboplatin and etoposide, or cisplatin and etoposide in split doses 〔JLCS C, 2〕; PS 4, no drug treatment 〔JLCS D, 2〕
    ※ Immunotherapy contraindicated: platinum and etoposide for 4–6 cycles 〔NCCN 2A, other recommended〕

      - SWOG S0124 [t-swog-s0124] | OS 9.9 vs 9.1 months
    ※ Serplulimab with carboplatin and etoposide: EU licence 2025; trial mostly in China; NCCN, ASCO and JLCS do not list it
      + ASTRUM-005 | OS HR
    ※ Pembrolizumab improved PFS but OS missed its significance boundary; not licensed
      ~ KEYNOTE-604 | PFS HR

  Surgery or SABR
    By MDT decision, after pathological mediastinal staging 〔NCCN 2A〕; surgery 〔JLCS C, 1〕
    ① Lobectomy, preferred, with mediastinal node dissection or systematic sampling of at least 3 N2 and 1 N1 stations; no pneumonectomy 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ② Medically inoperable 〔JLCS D, 2〕 or declining surgery: SABR, then chemotherapy, or chemoradiotherapy 〔NCCN 2A〕; chemotherapy may start first when SABR is delayed 〔NCCN 2A〕

  Chemoradiotherapy
    Concurrent chemotherapy and thoracic radiotherapy, PS 0–2 〔NCCN 1〕 〔JLCS A, 1〕; PS 2 due to SCLC 〔ASCO Low, Weak〕; older patients with PS 0–1 〔ASCO Moderate, Strong〕
    Platinum and etoposide for 4 cycles 〔JLCS C, 1〕: cisplatin and etoposide 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔ASCO High, Strong〕; carboplatin and etoposide when cisplatin is contraindicated or not tolerated 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
      + Sundstrøm 2002 [t-sundstr-m-et-al] | Limited stage 2-year OS 25% vs 8%, against CEV
    Thoracic radiotherapy with chemotherapy improves survival
      + Pignon 1992 [t-pignon-et-al] | 3-year OS 14.3% vs 8.9%
    ① 45 Gy in 30 fractions twice daily 〔NCCN 1〕 〔JLCS B, 1〕, starting with chemotherapy cycle 1 or 2 〔NCCN 1〕; early concurrent start 〔JLCS B, 1〕; chemotherapy is not delayed until radiotherapy can start 〔ASCO Low, Strong〕
      + Intergroup 0096 | 5-year OS 26% vs 16%
    ※ Concurrent radiotherapy gave longer survival than sequential radiotherapy, a difference that was not significant
      - JCOG 9104 [t-jcog-9104] | Median OS 27.2 vs 19.7 months, not significant
    ② 66–70 Gy in once-daily fractions over 6.5–7 weeks, an accepted alternative 〔NCCN 2A〕; JLCS keeps once-daily treatment for when twice daily is not feasible
      - CONVERT | OS HR 1.18 for once daily against twice daily, not significant
    ※ 70 Gy daily was no better than 45 Gy twice daily
      - CALGB 30610 [t-calgb-30610-rtog-0538] | OS HR 0.94
    ※ Higher-dose accelerated radiotherapy: 54 Gy in 30 twice-daily fractions, with a simultaneous boost to the gross tumour, improved OS in a phase III trial in China; 60 Gy twice daily and 65 Gy daily were tested in randomised phase II trials. NCCN says these trials indicate an advantage but recommends no dose above 45 Gy twice daily, and notes the dose with durvalumab is not defined; JLCS does not discuss them
