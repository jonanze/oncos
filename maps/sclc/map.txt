map: SCLC
source: ESMO {url https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2021.03.207}

band: Staging before treatment
  ① TNM 8th edition; trials use limited stage (one hemithorax and regional nodes, within one radiotherapy field) and extensive stage 〔ESMO IV, A〕
  ② Contrast CT of chest and abdomen 〔ESMO IV, A〕
  ③ Brain MRI in limited stage 〔ESMO III, A〕, and in extensive stage when PCI is not given 〔ESMO II, B〕
  ④ PET-CT optional for staging; a finding that changes treatment is confirmed by biopsy 〔ESMO II, C〕. PET-CT for radiotherapy planning 〔ESMO III, A〕. Bone scintigraphy in limited stage when PET-CT is not done 〔ESMO V, B〕
  ⑤ No PD-L1 or tumour mutational burden testing 〔ESMO I, D〕

course: Small-cell lung cancer
  Limited stage
    Stage I–III, encompassable in one radiotherapy field
    next: Surgery | Clinical T1–2 N0
    next: Chemoradiotherapy | T1–4 N0–3, PS 0–1
  Extensive stage, first line
    Platinum and etoposide for 4 cycles with atezolizumab (carboplatin only) or durvalumab (cisplatin or carboplatin), PS 0–1 〔ESMO I, A〕
      + IMpower133 | OS HR
      + CASPIAN | OS HR
    ※ Serplulimab with carboplatin and etoposide: EU licence 2025; trial mostly in China
      + ASTRUM-005 | OS HR
    ※ Pembrolizumab improved PFS but OS missed its significance boundary; not licensed
      ~ KEYNOTE-604 | PFS HR
    ※ Immunotherapy contraindicated, or PS 2 due to SCLC: platinum and etoposide for 4–6 cycles 〔ESMO I, A〕; carboplatin can replace cisplatin 〔ESMO I, B〕
    ※ Irinotecan with cisplatin is an alternative 〔ESMO II, C〕; the survival gain in Japan was not confirmed in North America
      + JCOG9511 | OS median
      - SWOG S0124 [t-swog-s0124] | OS 9.9 vs 9.1 months
    next: Extensive stage, after induction | No progression
  Surgery
    By MDT decision 〔ESMO III, B〕, after pathological mediastinal staging 〔ESMO IV, A〕
    ① Complete resection 〔ESMO III, A〕 with systematic nodal dissection 〔ESMO IV, A〕; sublobar resection is not recommended 〔ESMO V, E〕
    next: After resection
  Chemoradiotherapy
    Concurrent chemotherapy and thoracic radiotherapy 〔ESMO I, A〕
    Cisplatin and etoposide for 4 cycles 〔ESMO I, A〕, carboplatin when cisplatin is contraindicated 〔ESMO II, A〕
      + Sundstrøm 2002 [t-sundstr-m-et-al] | Limited stage 2-year OS 25% vs 8%, against CEV
    Thoracic radiotherapy with chemotherapy improves survival
      + Pignon 1992 [t-pignon-et-al] | 3-year OS 14.3% vs 8.9%
    ※ Concurrent radiotherapy gave longer survival than sequential radiotherapy, a difference that was not significant
      - JCOG 9104 [t-jcog-9104] | Median OS 27.2 vs 19.7 months, not significant
    ① 45 Gy in 30 fractions twice daily 〔ESMO I, A〕, starting with chemotherapy cycle 1 or 2 〔ESMO II, A〕, or with cycle 3 when PS or organ doses do not allow an earlier start 〔ESMO II, B〕
      + Intergroup 0096 | 5-year OS 26% vs 16%
    ② 66 Gy in 33 daily fractions when twice daily is not possible
      - CONVERT | OS HR 1.18 for once daily against twice daily, not significant
    ※ 70 Gy daily was no better than 45 Gy twice daily
      - CALGB 30610 [t-calgb-30610-rtog-0538] | OS HR 0.94
    ※ 60 Gy in 40 fractions twice daily raised 2-year OS in a randomised phase II trial
      + THORA [t-thora] | 2-year OS 74.2% vs 48.1%
    ※ Unfit for concurrent treatment: sequential chemoradiotherapy 〔ESMO V, B〕
    next: Limited stage, after chemoradiotherapy | No progression
  After resection
    ① Adjuvant platinum and etoposide 〔ESMO IV, A〕
    ② N2 or R1–R2: radiotherapy with the chemotherapy, preferably concurrent 〔ESMO IV, A〕
    ※ PCI after resection of stage I–II: shared decision 〔ESMO V, C〕
    join: Recurrent/progressive disease | Relapse
  Limited stage, after chemoradiotherapy
    ① PCI 25 Gy in 10 fractions after response, PS 0–1 〔ESMO I, A〕; PS 2 may be considered 〔ESMO III, B〕
      + Aupérin 1999 [t-aup-rin-et-al] | 3-year OS 20.7% vs 15.3%
      - PCI 99-01 [t-pci-99-01-eortc-22003-08004-rtog-0212-ifct-99-01] | 2-year OS 42% vs 37% for 25 Gy against 36 Gy
      ※ Stage I–II, over 70 or frail: shared decision 〔ESMO V, C〕
      ※ MRI surveillance without PCI: OS, the registered primary endpoint, did not differ at a preliminary analysis after 128 of 190 planned deaths; cognitive failure-free survival was better; limited and extensive stage; congress abstract
        ~ MAVERICK {url https://www.iaslc.org/iaslc-news/press-release/phase-iii-maverick-trial-supports-brain-mri-surveillance-alone-standard} | OS HR 0.90, primary; cognitive failure-free survival HR 0.60
    ② Durvalumab every 4 weeks for up to 24 months, after PCI where given; EU licence 2025
      + ADRIATIC | OS HR
    ※ Atezolizumab with and after chemoradiotherapy, or for 1 year after it, did not improve OS; ACHILES is a congress abstract
      - NRG LU005 [t-nrg-lu005] | OS HR
      - ACHILES [t-achiles] | OS HR
    join: Recurrent/progressive disease | Relapse
  Extensive stage, after induction
    ① Continue atezolizumab or durvalumab
    ② After induction with atezolizumab: atezolizumab with lurbinectedin; US licence 2025, EU licence 2026
      + IMforte | OS HR
    ③ After induction with durvalumab: tarlatamab with durvalumab; not licensed as of November 2026
      + DeLLphi-305 | OS HR
    ※ Nivolumab with or without ipilimumab after chemotherapy alone did not improve OS
      - CheckMate 451 | OS HR 0.92
    ※ Thoracic radiotherapy 30 Gy in 10 fractions after response, PS 0–2 〔ESMO II, C〕; with immunotherapy, shared decision 〔ESMO IV, C〕
      - CREST | 1-year OS 33% vs 28%, not significant; 2-year OS 13% vs 3%, secondary
    ※ PCI 20 Gy in 5 or 25 Gy in 10 fractions under 75, PS 0–2, when MRI staging and MRI follow-up are not available 〔ESMO II, B〕; may be left out when MRI after chemotherapy is clear and MRI follow-up is possible 〔ESMO II, B〕
      + EORTC 22993-08993 | OS HR
      - Takahashi 2017 [t-takahashi-et-al] | OS HR
      ~ MAVERICK {url https://www.iaslc.org/iaslc-news/press-release/phase-iii-maverick-trial-supports-brain-mri-surveillance-alone-standard} | OS HR 0.90, primary; cognitive failure-free survival HR 0.60
    next: Recurrent/progressive disease | Progression
  Recurrent/progressive disease
    Platinum-sensitive, relapse 3 months or more after platinum
    ① Carboplatin and etoposide again 〔ESMO II, B〕, or tarlatamab; the two have not been compared
      ~ GFPC 01–13 [t-groupe-fran-ais-de-pneumo-canc-rologie-01-13] | PFS HR
    ※ ESMO and GFPC 01–13 use 3 months; other guidelines use 6 months for rechallenge. In GFPC 01–13 the PFS gain was significant only for relapse 180 days or more after chemotherapy
    ② Topotecan oral or intravenous; CAV is an alternative 〔ESMO II, B〕
      + O'Brien 2006 [t-o-brien-et-al] | OS 25.9 vs 13.9 weeks, against supportive care
      - von Pawel 1999 [t-von-pawel-et-al] | Response 24.3% vs 18.3%, against CAV
    Platinum-resistant, relapse within 3 months
    ① Tarlatamab; US accelerated licence 2024, full licence 2025; EU licence 2026
      + DeLLphi-304 | OS HR
    ② Topotecan oral or intravenous 〔ESMO II, B〕
    Platinum-refractory, progression during platinum
    ① Tarlatamab; DeLLphi-304 included progression during first-line platinum
    ※ Before tarlatamab, ESMO advised a clinical trial or supportive care 〔ESMO II, C〕
    Any interval
    ※ A B7-H3 antibody-drug conjugate, tambotatug pelitecan, improved OS over topotecan in second line at an interim analysis; trial in China; not licensed
      + TAISHAN-302 | OS HR
    ※ Lurbinectedin alone did not improve OS over topotecan or irinotecan, and the US second-line indication is being withdrawn; ESMO 2021 had listed it 〔ESMO III, C〕
      - LAGOON {url https://www.targetedonc.com/view/phase-3-lagoon-trial-fails-to-confirm-survival-benefit-of-lurbinectedin-in-relapsed-sclc} | OS HR 1.19 against topotecan or irinotecan
