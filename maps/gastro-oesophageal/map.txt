# oncOS board map: Localized Gastroesophageal (non-metastatic gastric, junctional and oesophageal cancer)
# Built 2026-11-12. Metastatic disease is on the Advanced Gastroesophageal sheet
# Format and rules: see the header of maps/early-nsclc/map.txt
# One map, three branches (Jonan 2026-11-12): gastric adenocarcinoma with Siewert type III, oesophageal and junctional
#   adenocarcinoma with Siewert types I and II, and oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma
# Tags give evidence first, strength second, on the statement the guideline graded. East Asian guidelines first:
#   〔JGCA evidence, strength〕 JGCA 2025, 7th edition (Gastric Cancer 2026;29:271-299). Clinical questions carry evidence A to D and
#     Strong or Weak, with "against" where the guideline recommends against. Regimens in the text sections are voted
#     "recommended" or "conditionally recommended" and are tagged 〔JGCA recommended〕. Read 2026-11-12
#   〔JES strength〕 JES 2022, 5th edition (Kitagawa, Esophagus 2023;20:343-372). Strength only. The certainty letters were lost
#     in the text read. They can be added from the PDF tables later, as 〔JES B, Strong〕. Read 2026-11-12
#   〔KGCA evidence, strength〕 KGCA 2024 (Kim IH, J Gastric Cancer 2025;25:5-114). GRADE. Read 2026-11-12
#   〔ESMO level, grade〕 ESMO gastric and oesophageal cancer living guideline v1.0, November 2026 (Al-Batran, Ann Oncol 2026).
#     Web pages read 2026-11-12. The full Annals text was not read (endoscopic criteria, MSI-H advice)
#   〔NCCN category, preference〕 NCCN Gastric Cancer 4.2026 and NCCN Esophageal and EGJ Cancers 4.2026. 2A unless stated. Read 2026-11-12
# JGCA 2025 and KGCA 2024 were written before the MATTERHORN survival results
# Licensing read 2026-11-12 from news reports, labels not read: durvalumab with FLOT, FDA November 2025 and European Commission March 2026.
#   S-1 is on the Singapore Cancer Drug List and is numbered. Camrelizumab, rivoceranib and serplulimab are not licensed for gastric or
#   oesophageal cancer outside China and sit on ※ lines

band: Work-up
  ① Endoscopy with biopsy, and CT of the chest, abdomen and pelvis 〔ESMO V, A〕
    ・Endoscopic ultrasound for T and N stage 〔ESMO V, B〕
  ② FDG-PET for candidates for oesophagectomy 〔ESMO III, B〕
    ・FDG-PET is not routine in gastric cancer 〔JGCA C, Weak against〕 〔ESMO III, D〕
    ・KGCA allows FDG-PET as an added staging test in gastric cancer 〔KGCA Low, Conditional〕
  ③ Staging laparoscopy in gastric cancer when peritoneal disease is likely 〔JGCA B, Weak〕
    ・Before perioperative chemotherapy for resectable gastric cancer 〔ESMO III, B〕
    ・T3–4 junctional adenocarcinoma that extends into the cardia 〔ESMO IV, B〕
  ④ Mismatch repair or MSI 〔ESMO II, A〕 and PD-L1 〔ESMO I, A〕 on the biopsy
  ⑤ Nutritional assessment 〔ESMO III, A〕
    ・Nutritional support 〔ESMO II, A〕
  ※ JGCA stages by the Japanese classification 15th edition, which matches UICC TNM 8th edition
    ・JES stages by the Japanese Classification of Esophageal Cancer 12th edition

band: Early gastric cancer, cT1 N0
  ① Differentiated cT1a without ulceration, up to 2 cm: endoscopic mucosal resection or submucosal dissection 〔KGCA Moderate, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Stage I is treated by endoscopic or surgical resection alone 〔ESMO III, B〕
  ② Endoscopic submucosal dissection for wider groups of cT1a tumours
    ・Differentiated without ulceration, over 2 cm 〔KGCA Moderate, Strong〕
    ・Differentiated with ulceration, up to 3 cm 〔KGCA Moderate, Strong〕
    ・Undifferentiated without ulceration, up to 2 cm 〔KGCA Low, Conditional〕
  ③ Not suitable for endoscopic resection: gastrectomy with D1 or D1+ dissection 〔KGCA Low, Strong〕
    ・Deeper cT1b tumours are treated by surgery 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Laparoscopic distal gastrectomy for clinical stage I 〔JGCA A, Strong〕 〔KGCA High, Strong〕
    ~ KLASS-01
    ~ JCOG0912 [t-jcog-0912]
  ※ A non-curative endoscopic resection (eCura C-2) is followed by gastrectomy with lymphadenectomy 〔KGCA Low, Strong〕

band: Early oesophageal cancer, cT1 N0
  ① cT1a: endoscopic resection 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
    ・Stage I is treated by endoscopic or surgical resection alone 〔ESMO III, B〕
  ② After endoscopic resection, pT1a in the muscularis mucosae with vascular invasion, or pT1b: oesophagectomy or chemoradiotherapy
    ・JES states no preference between the two
    ・pT1b N0: oesophagectomy 〔NCCN 2A〕
  ③ cT1b N0 squamous cell carcinoma: oesophagectomy 〔JES Weak〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ・Definitive chemoradiotherapy with salvage treatment when the patient wants to keep the oesophagus 〔JES Weak〕

course: Localised gastric and oesophageal cancer
  Histology and site
    cT2 or more, or node-positive, with no distant metastases
    ① Gastric adenocarcinoma, including Siewert type III junctional tumours
    ② Oesophageal adenocarcinoma, and Siewert type I and II junctional tumours
    ③ Oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma
    ※ JGCA defines junctional cancer as a tumour with its centre within 2 cm of the junction
    ※ KGCA covers Siewert type II and III tumours with gastric cancer

  Gastric adenocarcinoma
    branch: GA
    ① Perioperative FLOT with durvalumab, then durvalumab alone 〔ESMO I, A〕
      ・ESMO prefers it where it is available
      ・PD-L1 CPS 1 or more, or TAP 1% or more 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
      ・cN0, CPS below 1 or diffuse type 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
      ・The diffuse type had no overall survival benefit from durvalumab in MATTERHORN
      ・JGCA 2025 and KGCA 2024 were written before the MATTERHORN survival results
    + MATTERHORN
    ② Perioperative FLOT 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
      ・A fluoropyrimidine with oxaliplatin 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
      ・Fluorouracil with cisplatin 〔NCCN 1, other recommended〕
      ・A fluoropyrimidine with oxaliplatin when FLOT is unsuitable 〔ESMO IV, B〕
    + FLOT4-AIO
    + MAGIC
    ③ Docetaxel, oxaliplatin and S-1 before gastrectomy and S-1 after it, or perioperative SOX 〔KGCA High, Conditional〕
      ・KGCA favours chemotherapy before surgery over upfront surgery 〔KGCA High, Conditional〕
      ・KGCA states that FLOT should not be adopted in Asia without further evidence
    ~ PRODIGY
    ~ RESOLVE
    ~ RESONANCE
    ④ Gastrectomy with D2 dissection, then adjuvant chemotherapy for pathological stage II or III 〔KGCA High, Strong〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・JGCA keeps surgery first as the standard in Japan
      ・JGCA makes no clear recommendation on chemotherapy before surgery
    ※ MSI-H or mismatch repair deficient: consider an immune checkpoint inhibitor before surgery 〔NCCN 2A, useful in certain circumstances〕
      ・NCCN lists dostarlimab, nivolumab with ipilimumab, pembrolizumab, and tremelimumab with durvalumab
      ・KGCA finds the benefit of adjuvant chemotherapy unclear in MSI-H disease
    next: After gastrectomy
  Oesophageal and junctional adenocarcinoma
    branch: OA

      ・Junctional tumour with CPS 1 or more, or TAP 1% or more 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
      ・Oesophageal tumour with CPS 1 or more, or TAP 1% or more 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
    + MATTERHORN
    ② Perioperative FLOT 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
    + FLOT4-AIO
    + ESOPEC
    ③ Unsuitable for FLOT: chemoradiotherapy, then oesophagectomy 〔ESMO I, B〕 〔NCCN 2A〕
      ・Carboplatin with paclitaxel 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
      ・NCCN also uses it for borderline resectable disease
    + CROSS
    - Neo-AEGIS
    ④ Unsuitable for FLOT and for chemoradiotherapy: oxaliplatin with a fluoropyrimidine 〔ESMO IV, B〕 〔NCCN 2A, other recommended〕

    ※ Adding radiotherapy to chemotherapy before surgery raised the pathological complete response rate but did not improve survival

      ~ NeoRes [t-neores]

    ※ MSI-H or mismatch repair deficient: an immune checkpoint inhibitor before surgery, as for gastric cancer 〔NCCN 2A, useful in certain circumstances〕
    ※ Unresectable junctional tumour, or a patient unfit for or declining surgery: definitive chemoradiotherapy 〔ESMO III, B〕
      ・NCCN keeps it for patients who decline surgery 〔NCCN 2A〕
    next: After preoperative treatment
  Oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma
    branch: SC
    Resectable clinical stage II or III
    ① Chemoradiotherapy, restaging, then oesophagectomy 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
      ・Carboplatin with paclitaxel 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
      ・Cisplatin with vinorelbine was the regimen in NEOCRTEC5010
      ・Radiotherapy added to cisplatin and fluorouracil did not improve survival in JCOG1109
    + CROSS
    + NEOCRTEC5010
    ② Docetaxel, cisplatin and fluorouracil, then oesophagectomy 〔JES Strong〕
      ・ESMO uses it when radiotherapy is contraindicated 〔ESMO I, B〕
      ・Cisplatin with fluorouracil remains an option for older patients and for comorbidity
      ・NCCN lists no chemotherapy before surgery for squamous cell carcinoma
    + JCOG1109 [t-jcog1109]
    + JCOG9907
    ※ Treatment that includes surgery is preferred to definitive chemoradiotherapy 〔JES Weak〕
      ・NCCN lists definitive chemoradiotherapy as an alternative 〔NCCN 2A〕
    ※ China only: camrelizumab with chemotherapy before surgery raised the pathological complete response rate
      ・Event-free survival data are immature
      ・Camrelizumab is not licensed outside China
      ~ ESCORT-NEO [t-escort-neo-ncces01]
    next: After preoperative treatment

  After preoperative treatment
    ① Restaging, then oesophagectomy 〔NCCN 2A, preferred〕
      ・Squamous cell carcinoma: surgery even after a clinical complete response 〔ESMO I, A〕
      ・Observation after a clinical complete response to chemoradiotherapy 〔NCCN 2B〕
      ・Overall survival at 5 years did not differ between active surveillance and surgery in SANO
      ・More distant metastases were found with active surveillance
    ~ SANO
    ② After chemoradiotherapy and R0 resection with residual tumour: nivolumab for 1 year 〔JES Strong〕 〔ESMO I, A〕 〔NCCN 1, preferred〕
      ・JES gives it for any histology and any PD-L1 status
      ・Adenocarcinoma: only when PD-L1 is positive 〔ESMO I, B〕
      ・JES makes no recommendation after chemotherapy without radiotherapy
    ~ CheckMate 577
