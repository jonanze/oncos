# How to read and edit this file
#   Indent 2 spaces per level.
#   tree:   a decision tree; each plain line is a node, its children are indented under it
#   course: a disease course instead of a tree; each line at 2 spaces is a step, everything indented
#           under it is its content, and "next: Step | label" draws a line to a later step
#   band:   a block above the tree for the whole sheet
#   panel:  a block beside the tree, joined by a brace to the node named after ">"
#   = text          the treatment for a branch
#   + Trial | fig   a trial line; the mark records the result and is not drawn:
#                   + OS benefit, ~ surrogate only (PFS, response, single-arm), - no benefit or harm, * no comparison
#   fig with no digits is read from the Evidence row at each publish:
#     OS HR, PFS HR   the HR on the row's OS or PFS line
#     OS median       the medians on that line, "a vs b months"
#     response        the first percentage on the ORR line
#   fig with digits is shown as written; the map check fails if the row no longer has those numbers
#   Trial [t-...]   picks the Evidence row by its anchor when the name is not enough
#   Trial {pmid N}  a trial with no Evidence row yet: PubMed link, listed for intake
#   ※ text          caveat             ° text          side effects, grey
#   ① text          numbered item; plain lines in a band or panel are text
#   | a | b |       a row of a ruled grid; the first row is the header, - leaves a cell empty
#   off:            sheet rows left off on purpose: Trial [t-...] | reason
#   source: ESGO [t-...]   a guideline row; 〔ESGO II, A〕 at the end of a statement marks it as resting on
#                   that guideline, with its printed level of evidence and grade, and opens its Evidence card
#   source: ESMO {url https://...}   the same for a guideline held only as a link; the tag opens the link
#
# Rules (Jonan 2026-11-08; node maps/check.cjs enforces the starred ones)
#   Layout fits the disease: a tree where care branches on tests, a course where one state follows another
#   Square lines only; joins line up; no result colours (the marks above are kept as data, not drawn)
# * Every sheet row is on the map or under off: with a reason
# * Trial lines cite clinical trials, pooled analyses of trials or meta-analyses; no cohort, review,
#   consensus, PK, animal or healthy-volunteer papers (the statement can stay as plain text)
#   Superseded trials go under off: unless an option still in use rests on them
#   Standard options only; trials of options not in standard use go under off: (Jonan 2026-11-09, Lung-MAP)
# * Plain clinical wording: no arrows, no metaphors such as "backbone"
# * Labels on connecting lines start with a capital, like every other label
#   A statement that rests on a guideline rather than a trial carries its 〔source level, grade〕 tag;
#   where a trial and the guideline disagree, or the trial came later, a ※ line says so

map: Endometrial Cancer
source: ESGO [t-esgo-estro-esp-endometrial-carcinoma-management-guidelines]
scale: Low | Intermediate | High-intermediate | High

band: Diagnosis and surgery
  ① Total hysterectomy, bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy and sentinel node biopsy, by minimally invasive surgery 〔ESGO I, A〕
  ※ Infracolic omentectomy for serous carcinoma, carcinosarcoma and undifferentiated carcinoma 〔ESGO IV, B〕
  ② Unfit for surgery: definitive radiotherapy, EBRT with image-guided brachytherapy for high-grade or deeply invasive tumours 〔ESGO II, B〕
  ※ Brachytherapy alone is an option for low-grade tumours without deep invasion 〔ESGO II, B〕
  ③ Molecular class and ER in every tumour, on the biopsy where possible 〔ESGO IV, A〕
  ④ MMR immunohistochemistry in every tumour, which also screens for Lynch syndrome 〔ESGO III, A〕
  ⑤ HER2 in advanced or recurrent p53 abnormal tumours, and in all serous carcinomas and carcinosarcomas 〔ESGO IV, C〕

course:
  Stage III–IV at diagnosis
    ① Complete resection feasible: cytoreduction, removing only suspicious nodes 〔ESGO IV, B〕
    ② Unresectable from local extent: definitive radiotherapy with image-guided brachytherapy, or systemic therapy first, chosen by molecular class 〔ESGO IV, C〕
    ③ Disseminated disease, or residual disease after surgery: first-line systemic therapy
    next: Advanced or recurrent, first line
  Newly diagnosed, after hysterectomy and staging
    Classify in this order: POLE, then MMR, then p53. With more than one feature, POLE outranks MMR and MMR outranks p53 〔ESGO IV, B〕
    Tumours with none of these are NSMP, split by ER (positive if 10% or more) and grade
    | Risk group | POLE mutated | MMR deficient | NSMP, ER positive, low grade | p53 abnormal; NSMP, ER negative or high grade | Adjuvant treatment |
    | Low | Stage I–II | Low grade with myometrial invasion under 50%; high grade without invasion | Myometrial invasion under 50% | - | None 〔ESGO II, A〕 |
    | Intermediate | - | Myometrial invasion 50% or more, or high grade with any invasion | Myometrial invasion 50% or more, or cervical stromal invasion | - | Vaginal brachytherapy 〔ESGO I, A〕 |
    | High-intermediate | - | Substantial LVSI, or cervical stromal invasion | Substantial LVSI | - | Pelvic EBRT 〔ESGO II, A〕 |
    | High | - | Stage III–IVA | Stage III–IVA | Any myometrial invasion, stage I–IVA | Chemoradiotherapy 〔ESGO I, A〕 |
    | Uncertain | Stage III–IVA | - | - | No myometrial invasion | POLE mutated: less than high-risk treatment can be considered 〔ESGO IV, B〕. No invasion: usually none 〔ESGO IV, C〕 |
    ※ Risk groups use FIGO 2023 stage with molecular class
    ※ Intermediate: no adjuvant treatment is an option, especially under 60 years or in low-grade tumours 〔ESGO III, C〕
    ※ High-intermediate: vaginal brachytherapy instead, especially if node-negative 〔ESGO II, B〕; no adjuvant treatment if node-negative, low grade and without substantial LVSI 〔ESGO IV, B〕
    ※ High: sequential chemotherapy and radiotherapy, or chemotherapy with or without vaginal brachytherapy, are alternatives 〔ESGO I, B〕
    ※ High, MMR deficient stage III–IVA: chemotherapy with a checkpoint inhibitor, with or without EBRT 〔ESGO II, B〕
      - KEYNOTE-B21 | DFS HR 1.02; MMR deficient HR 0.31
      ※ Pembrolizumab with adjuvant chemotherapy did not improve DFS in all patients; the ESGO statement rests on the MMR deficient subgroup
    ① Intermediate risk: vaginal brachytherapy controls the vagina as well as pelvic EBRT, with fewer bowel effects
      ~ PORTEC-1 | Locoregional recurrence 4% vs 14%
      ~ GOG 99 | Recurrence 3% vs 12%
      ※ Neither trial improved overall survival
      ~ PORTEC-2 | Vaginal recurrence 1.8% vs 1.6%
    ② High-intermediate risk: pelvic EBRT; vaginal brachytherapy with chemotherapy was not better and left more nodal relapses
      - GOG 249 | RFS HR 0.92
    ③ High risk: chemoradiotherapy improved survival over radiotherapy alone, most in stage III and serous carcinoma
      + PORTEC-3 | OS HR 0.70
      + PORTEC-3 molecular | p53 abnormal OS HR 0.55
      ※ Chemotherapy added nothing in MMR deficient tumours; ESGO 2025 judged this too little evidence to omit it in stage III
      - GOG 258 | RFS HR 0.90
      ※ Chemotherapy alone gave the same relapse-free survival as chemoradiotherapy, with more pelvic and nodal relapses
    next: Recurrence | Recurrence
  Recurrence
    Vaginal, pelvic or in a few sites: local treatment first
    ① No radiotherapy before: EBRT with image-guided brachytherapy, with or without chemotherapy 〔ESGO IV, A〕
    ② Vaginal brachytherapy only before: EBRT with a brachytherapy boost 〔ESGO IV, C〕
    ③ EBRT before: radical surgery if clear margins are feasible 〔ESGO IV, A〕; otherwise systemic therapy chosen by molecular class
    ※ MMR deficient, no checkpoint inhibitor before: chemotherapy with a checkpoint inhibitor, then the checkpoint inhibitor alone 〔ESGO II, B〕
    ④ One to five metastases in up to three regions: surgery, stereotactic radiotherapy or ablation 〔ESGO IV, B〕
    Widespread, or not suited to local treatment: first-line systemic therapy
    next: Advanced or recurrent, first line | Widespread
  Advanced or recurrent, first line
    Choose by MMR status; six cycles of carboplatin-paclitaxel is the standard chemotherapy 〔ESGO I, A〕
    ① MMR deficient: carboplatin-paclitaxel with dostarlimab, durvalumab or pembrolizumab, then the antibody as maintenance 〔ESGO I, A〕
      ~ RUBY | MMR deficient PFS HR 0.28
      ~ NRG-GY018 [t-nrg-gy018-keynote-868] | MMR deficient PFS HR 0.30
      ~ DUO-E [t-duo-e] | MMR deficient PFS HR 0.42
    ② MMR proficient: carboplatin-paclitaxel 〔ESGO I, A〕; adding pembrolizumab or dostarlimab, or durvalumab followed by durvalumab with olaparib, can be considered 〔ESGO I, B〕
      ~ RUBY | All patients PFS HR 0.64; OS HR 0.64 at first interim, boundary not met
      ~ NRG-GY018 [t-nrg-gy018-keynote-868] | MMR proficient PFS HR 0.54
      ~ DUO-E [t-duo-e] | MMR proficient, with olaparib, PFS HR 0.57
    ③ HER2 3+, when a checkpoint inhibitor cannot be given: carboplatin-paclitaxel with trastuzumab 〔ESGO II, B〕
      + Fader et al. | OS HR 0.58
      ※ Fader et al. enrolled serous carcinoma only
    ④ Low grade, ER positive, low volume or slowly growing: a progestin, medroxyprogesterone or megestrol 〔ESGO III, A〕; an aromatase inhibitor or tamoxifen are alternatives 〔ESGO IV, C〕
    ※ MMR proficient relapse after adjuvant chemotherapy, when chemotherapy cannot be given: lenvatinib with pembrolizumab can be considered 〔ESGO III, C〕
    ※ Carboplatin-paclitaxel replaced doxorubicin, cisplatin and paclitaxel
      ~ GOG 209 | Non-inferior, OS HR 1.002
    next: Progression after platinum | Progression
  Progression after platinum
    Repeat MMR testing on the relapse when feasible 〔ESGO IV, B〕
    ① MMR deficient, no checkpoint inhibitor before: pembrolizumab or dostarlimab alone 〔ESGO III, A〕; lenvatinib with pembrolizumab is an option 〔ESGO I, B〕
      * KEYNOTE-158 [t-keynote-158--endometrial-cancer] | Response
      * GARNET | Response
    ② MMR proficient, no checkpoint inhibitor before: lenvatinib with pembrolizumab 〔ESGO I, A〕
      + KEYNOTE-775 [t-keynote-775-study-309] | OS HR
    ③ After a checkpoint inhibitor: platinum rechallenge, doxorubicin, weekly paclitaxel or endocrine therapy 〔ESGO IV, B〕
    ④ HER2 2+ or 3+: trastuzumab deruxtecan 〔ESGO III, B〕
      * DESTINY-PanTumor02 [t-destiny-pantumor02] | IHC 3+ response 84.6%
      ※ HER2 3+ with platinum rechallenge possible: carboplatin-paclitaxel with trastuzumab 〔ESGO II, B〕
