# oncOS inclusion rule: which trials go in the Evidence database and on the board maps
# Jonan 2026-11-10, after independent review and the audit of all 49 sheets; applies to every sheet and map
# Canonical here; oncos-automation agents/references/curator-discovery.md points to this file

Definitions
  Setting: disease, stage or line of treatment, and biomarker or histology group
  Reference sources: the guideline versions on the map's source: lines, chosen for each map by Jonan from a
    dated list of candidates (a guideline that predates a licence or randomised trial changing a numbered
    option on the map is marked stale there; see the spec, Building a new map), the current NCCN guideline for the
    disease (database and maps; an option NCCN alone lists sits on a ※ line with its NCCN tag; Jonan
    2026-11-10), and current EU and US licences;
    other regulators only where Jonan names them for a disease (China for sugemalimab)
  Licence: a current full, conditional or accelerated marketing authorisation in that setting;
    a withdrawn licence or a humanitarian device exemption is not current
  Tested intervention: what differs between the arms; each randomisation is classed on its own
  Class A, standard option: licensed in that setting, or recommended at any grade by a reference
    guideline in that setting; trials of dose, duration, schedule, sequence or omission of a standard
    option, and trials comparing two standard options, are class A
    Class A also covers a treatment licensed or recommended in the same disease in another stage, line or
      biomarker group, tested to extend its use (an approved drug expanding its indication, as LORIN)
  Class B, former or addressed practice: class A under an earlier guideline version or a withdrawn
    licence; or a reference guideline recommends against it; or the trial combines two class A treatments
  Class C, experimental: a treatment with no licence or reference-guideline recommendation anywhere in
    that disease
  Result: positive, prespecified primary endpoint met (non-inferiority: margin met); negative, not met;
    mixed, co-primary endpoints split; a subgroup result does not change the label; a trial closed
    early with fewer than half its planned events is inconclusive; mixed is judged at the final analysis,
    so an interim split is not yet mixed
  China-only phase III trials: included in the database in their own right; on the map as ※ lines
    while class C

Rules
  ① Designs: randomised trials in patients, pooled analyses of them and meta-analyses, with a
    peer-reviewed report. Non-randomised data only when a current licence or reference-guideline
    recommendation rests on them and no randomised trial exists for that option in that setting;
    they stay ordinary trial lines marked ~, as the map format defines. A congress abstract is allowed
    for a class A or B trial on a ※ line that says "congress abstract"; a company or society press
    release is not
  ② Class A or B: in the database whatever the result. This covers practice-changing and
    practice-informing trials, negative trials that affirm or stop a practice, non-inferiority trials,
    and trials showing harm from a standard or former standard treatment
  ③ Class C, positive: in the database, off the map with the reason "no licence or recommendation"
    until it becomes class A; Jonan may name one for a ※ line earlier. Standing naming (Jonan
    2026-11-09): positive trials run in one country only, such as China, stay as ※ lines that say so
    and say the drug is not licensed in the EU or US. Other namings are listed in the map header
  ④ Class C, negative, mixed or inconclusive: not in the database and not on the map; listed in the
    map folder's excluded.txt. A positive subgroup does not change this unless a licence or
    reference-guideline recommendation follows for that subgroup
  ⑤ A guideline citation counts only when the trial is cited for a specific statement for or against;
    mention in background text does not count
  ⑥ Exceptions to ④, each written in excluded.txt or on the row with its letter:
    a. the trial shows that a biomarker used to select a standard option does not predict benefit
    b. a regulator restricted or withdrew a licence because of it
    c. Jonan names it, with a one-line reason (for example a trial that ended a drug class)
    d. the drug is licensed or recommended in a cancer of the same organ system or lineage, and the
       trial is randomised evidence on using it in this disease (bevacizumab in endometrial cancer);
       the drug itself counts, not its class
    e. a published analysis of the trial's patients reports a prognostic or predictive result for a
       biomarker that a reference guideline uses in that disease (p53 in GOG-86P)
    f. the trial answers a question about standard care in that setting: combining two treatment
       classes each standard there (COMBI-i, BEAT-meso); duration or omission of a standard class
       (GeparNuevo); or an intervention in wide use without randomised evidence there (aspirin in ASAC)
  ⑦ Map: a row is on the map only if it supports a statement there (an option, a "do not" line or a
    ※ caveat). A drug or device recommended by one reference guideline only, with no EU or US licence and
    no NCCN listing, is not a map option; its trial goes under off: with that reason (Jonan 2026-11-10,
    pegargiminase). Every other row goes under off: with a reason: superseded; no licence or
    recommendation; non-randomised with randomised evidence available; not a map statement.
    Conflicting trials on one statement are both cited
  ⑧ Where EU and US licences, or reference guidelines, disagree, the trial is included and the line
    says so
  ⑨ Re-apply the rule to a sheet when a reference guideline is updated, a licence is granted,
    restricted or withdrawn, or a final survival analysis is published. Rows move between the map and
    off:, they are not deleted; excluded.txt entries are screened again against ⑤ and ⑥

excluded.txt format, one trial per line:  Trial | PMID, DOI or URL | date screened | class and result | reason
